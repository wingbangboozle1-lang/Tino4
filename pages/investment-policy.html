Investment policy for the proceeds

A written policy prepared before any money arrives: staging and renewal rules, rebalance bands, tax handling, prohibited actions, the liquidity clauses that cover the estate itself, and the conditions that would change the plan.

Write it before the money arrives

An investment policy written after proceeds land is a document drafted under pressure: the market is moving, the house may still be unsold, and the temptation is to decide by headline. Written in advance, the same policy settles the routine questions once — allocation, staging, rebalancing, cash reserve, tax handling and what would justify a change — and leaves only the genuinely unexpected to the review clause. Sign it, date it, and record in the decision log which version applied on which date.

The ten sections to write, in order

A policy is a set of decisions made in advance. Each row is written as a rule the owner could follow without further judgment in the moment, which is the entire point.

Liquidity clauses the policy has to contain while the estate is still open

An investment policy for inheritance proceeds is really two policies: how the estate pays what it owes, and what the heir does with what is left. They are written in that order.

Estate liquidity needA Form 706 filing is tested on the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts, and the estate must be able to pay tax without a forced sale of real property; the IRS notes the nine-month due date and the extension available on request.Estates whose assets are mostly one house solve this with life insurance, an existing cash reserve or a partial sale; the point of the policy is to decide which before the death.VerifiedIRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs · IRS — Estate tax

Agreeing the answer before the figures arrive

The owner's own discipline — agreeing in writing what would make the household choose each path before the projections are produced — is the difference between a decision and a rationalisation. Put the reversal conditions on the first screen, the signed policy here, and the dated record in the decision log. If the figures later argue for a different path, the written conditions show whether the answer moved because the facts moved or because the wish moved.
