Operating-cost evidence plan
Cost categoryWhat drives the costEvidence / actionEvidenceOfficial source / scope
Property taxUse the official tax bill and assessor roll; identify base-year value, direct assessments, exemptions, reassessment triggers, the tax-rate area and its published rate.Obtain the actual bill and official tax-rate-area schedule; do not infer a parcel-specific rate from a generic levy.GuidanceCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19 · California Revenue and Taxation Code §51
InsurancePremium depends on property, use, coverage, deductible, underwriting and market availability.Get multiple written owner-occupied and landlord-policy quotes; compare exclusions and FAIR Plan/DIC gaps.GuidanceCalifornia Department of Insurance — Residential insurance
Water, power and solarTariffs, metering, solar export rules and household usage determine actual bills and credits.Collect account bills and interconnection/tariff documents; no generic bill is substituted.GuidanceCalifornia Public Utilities Commission — Net metering and net billing · California State Water Resources Control Board — Drinking Water Program
Maintenance and capitalA property-condition assessment and repair history are needed; a percentage-of-value shortcut is not a bid.Create five-year safety, roof, structure, plumbing, electrical, HVAC and accessibility plans with bids.GuidanceCalifornia Contractors State License Board — Check a license
Five-year plan — years 1-2Safety and habitability first: inspect roof, envelope, electrical, plumbing and any seismic elements; urgent items are priced separately from routine maintenance.Obtain inspection reports and at least two licensed bids per item before ranking paths that depend on carrying costs.GuidanceCalifornia Contractors State License Board — Check a license · IRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
Five-year plan — years 3-5Systems renewal window: water heater and HVAC service life, exterior envelope, windows and doors, and any solar equipment service and inverter life.Record equipment ages and service contracts; capital items above the plan are separate decisions, not surprises.GuidanceCalifornia Contractors State License Board — Check a license · California Public Utilities Commission — Net metering and net billing
Reserve policyFund a dedicated repair-and-capital reserve monthly; for rentals, repairs and improvements are treated differently for income tax, so keep invoices classified as incurred.Adopt a written reserve policy and ask the CPA for a repair-versus-improvement classification rule.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · California Contractors State License Board — Check a license
Rental operationGross rent must be reduced for vacancy, operating costs, management, taxes, repairs and depreciation treatment.Reconcile leases and deposit ledgers, verify unit legality and check local tenant rules.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · California Civil Code §1947.12
How to research insurance cost without publishing a guess
StepWhat to doOutput to recordEvidenceOfficial source / scope
Compare official market data firstUse the California Department of Insurance premium-comparison tool to see how premiums vary by coverage amount, deductible and location band.A saved comparison run with its date, showing the coverage and deductible used.GuidanceCalifornia Department of Insurance — Compare premiums tool
Obtain written quotes for the actual useRequest quotes for the true occupancy: an owner-occupied policy and a landlord or rental-dwelling policy are different products.At least two written quotes per property per use, with coverage, exclusions and deductibles.GuidanceCalifornia Department of Insurance — Residential insurance
Check availability and gapsIf the voluntary market will not quote, ask about the FAIR Plan as insurer of last resort and what a difference-in-conditions policy would add.Written confirmation of what is covered and what is not, including theft, liability and water perils.GuidanceCalifornia Department of Insurance — Residential insurance
Model renewal, not just year onePremiums are re-underwritten; a first-year quote is not a renewal promise.A renewal-date log and a planning band that admits re-pricing.GuidanceCalifornia Department of Insurance — Compare premiums tool · U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — CPI
Keep the quotes out of the repositoryQuotes contain property identifiers and coverage detail.Store them in the private workpaper; publish only labelled ranges, if at all.GuidanceGitHub Docs — Removing sensitive data from a repository
Utilities, solar and water evidence
UtilityWhy a flat monthly number is unreliableEvidence to collectEvidenceOfficial source / scope
Water and sewerBills follow tariff tiers, seasonal use, household size and the number of units metered; a single month proves nothing.Twelve months of statements for the account, plus the water system's tariff sheet.GuidanceCalifornia State Water Resources Control Board — Drinking Water Program
Electricity and solar exportExport credits depend on the tariff, the interconnection date, metered usage and the utility's billing rules; a credit in one month can be a charge in another.Twelve months of statements, the interconnection agreement and the applicable net billing or net metering tariff document.GuidanceCalifornia Public Utilities Commission — Net metering and net billing
Solar equipmentProduction falls with equipment age and soiling; inverters have a shorter service life than panels.Equipment ages, warranty terms, service records and an inverter replacement line in the five-year capital plan.GuidanceCalifornia Public Utilities Commission — Net metering and net billing · California Contractors State License Board — Check a license
Vacant or partly occupied periodsMinimum charges continue and insurance terms can change when a property is unoccupied.Confirm the insurer's unoccupied-property terms in writing before modelling a vacancy.GuidanceCalifornia Department of Insurance — Residential insurance · IRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
