Rental depreciation, the clawback it creates, and the personal-use switch
RuleWhat the official source statesHow it changes a path comparisonEvidenceOfficial source / scope
Only the building depreciatesResidential rental property is recovered straight-line over the stated recovery period under the general depreciation system, using a mid-month convention; land is never depreciable and the basis used for depreciation excludes it.A gross-value figure is not a depreciable base. The private model needs a land/building allocation, and an addition to a rental house follows the same class life.Verified ruleIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
Deductions count whether or not they were takenDepreciation reduces basis, and the reduction applies to depreciation allowed or allowable; a sale is measured against adjusted basis.Skipping the deduction does not avoid the repayment. A building that has been rented carries the reduced basis even if no return claimed it, so the CPA must reconstruct it.Verified ruleIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets
The recovered amount returns as gain at a capped rateGain is the excess over adjusted basis; the part attributable to depreciation on real property used in a business or for investment is unrecaptured section 1250 gain, taxed at a maximum 25% federal rate. Short-term gain is ordinary income.Sheltering at a high marginal rate and repaying at up to 25% is a timing benefit, not a saving. Publish both halves or neither, and remember a ceiling is not the taxpayer's rate.Verified ruleIRS Topic 409 — Capital gains and losses · IRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
California does not discount the gainThe state Franchise Tax Board states there is no special tax rate for capital gains; gains are taxed like other income, and an additional one percent applies to the portion of taxable income above the statutory threshold.A federal-only recapture estimate understates the exit cost. Model the sale year with the household's ordinary rates and the threshold, not a federal capital-gains bracket.Verified ruleFTB — Capital gains and losses · California statute — additional tax on high taxable income · FTB — 2025 Form 540-ES instructions
An inherited rental starts from a new baseBasis for property acquired from a decedent is generally the fair market value at death, and an estate that must report basis can bind the beneficiary's initial basis to the reported value.Depreciation accumulated before death does not attach to the stepped-up portion, but the heir's own recovery period begins at the new base — so the recapture question belongs to the years after the transfer.Verified ruleIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets · IRS — About Form 8971 (basis reporting)
Living in part of a rented building changes the income lineIf the owner occupies part of a structure, gross rental income includes the fair rental value of the part not held out for rent, and personal-use limits decide which deductions may be claimed against rental income.The conversion year is not a free switch: the same year can carry both rental and personal-use treatment, and mis-stating it moves both the deduction and the later repayment.Verified ruleIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
A main home is not depreciableDepreciation is allowed for property held for the production of income or for business use; a residence the owner occupies is not such property.The path that lives in the inherited home forgoes the deduction stream — and forgoes the clawback with it. That symmetry is the honest way to compare the paths.Verified ruleIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · IRS Topic 701 — Sale of your home
Five-year capital plan: what to inspect, and how each item is treated
Building systemWhat the plan must recordTreatment to confirmEvidenceOfficial source / scope
Roof, structure, drainageInspection date, remaining life, defect list, two or more licensed bids per item, and whether a failure creates a habitability or disclosure issue.Ordinary repairs that keep the property working are expenses; a replacement that adds value or extends life is a capital improvement recovered through depreciation.GuidanceCalifornia Contractors State License Board — Check a license · IRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
Electrical, HVAC, plumbing, fire safetyAge and capacity against the configuration actually intended — a multi-unit rental and a single-family home need different panel, egress and separation answers.Work done at the same time as a change of use may need to be allocated between the rental and personal-use periods.GuidanceCalifornia Contractors State License Board — Check a license · IRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
Windows, envelope, insulation, solarWhether equipment is shared between units or with an owner-occupied portion, and the interconnection date that sets the export arrangement.An addition to a rental house is residential rental property; shared or personal-use portions need allocation.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · California Public Utilities Commission — Net metering and net billing
Water heating, leaks, sewer lateralsThe condition items a water provider or an insurer will ask about, and any mandatory upgrade with its compliance deadline.Emergency repairs are usually expenses; a betterment or adaptation is capitalized, and casualty treatment is a separate question for the CPA.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · California State Water Resources Control Board — Drinking Water Program
Seismic bracing and tenant safety itemsThe scope the household is willing to fund, with a dated decision either way rather than silence.Deferred capital is a cost that arrives later; recording the decision to defer is what makes it a choice instead of an error.GuidanceCalifornia Contractors State License Board — Check a license · California Department of Insurance — Residential insurance
Reserve fundingA monthly sinking-fund target per building built from bids, not from a percentage of value, beside the deductibles and coverage limits that decide who pays when something fails.Reserves are not deductible; they are the cash that prevents a forced sale. Coverage limits decide whether a failure is an inconvenience or a liquidity event.GuidanceCalifornia Department of Insurance — Residential insurance · California Contractors State License Board — Check a license

What this page cannot tell you

No rent, premium, bill or repair figure for a specific property is published here, and none is inferred from an average. The evidence standard above exists so the private workpaper can be filled by a professional instead of guessed in public.
