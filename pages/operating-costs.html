The California Department of Insurance publishes historical policy-level and ZIP-level residential insurance experience for periods through 2023. Those data can provide context but do not establish a current premium, coverage availability, or quote for a particular owner-occupied or rental dwelling. Quotes depend on replacement cost, deductibles, occupancy, coverage, location and insurer. Confirm the solar tariff, annual true-up and whether credits are actually payable. Use a landlord policy where appropriate; do not substitute the statewide homeowner average or historical ZIP data for a quote.
