No premiums or monthly cost estimates are published

The exact quote or bill is private, provider-specific and time-sensitive. The previously supplied estimates are not reproduced or treated as verified. Obtain current written quotes and actual bills for each property's intended use.

Coverage and use checks

Insurance gap

The state explains that the FAIR Plan has limited covered perils; compare standard coverage and any DIC policy rather than treating a FAIR Plan policy as complete coverage.

Solar tariff

Determine interconnection date, NEM/NBT tariff, true-up rules and export-credit treatment from the account records.

Rental use

Confirm permitted units, local rent restrictions, deposits and lease terms before applying projected gross rent.

Working figures published below the blank framework

The owner's working figures, rounded to the nearest ten dollars and stripped of every personal identifier, are published below so the analysis can be checked rather than asserted. They are the owner's own records, not official determinations. Each row names the evidence that would replace an estimate. These are working figures, not conclusions. A rounded assessment history plus a public postal area could in principle be matched against a county assessment roll. That residual risk is documented on the privacy page. If it is not acceptable, remove the postal areas and re-run the utility and insurance research at county level.
