The largest operating fact in this plan

The inherited home's tax bill is modeled to rise from $2,400 a year to $18,030 with a Proposition 19 claim, or $23,660 without one. Insurance, water, maintenance and reserves are all smaller than that change; do not spend the first review round on them.
