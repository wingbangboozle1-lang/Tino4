Insurance, water, electricity, solar and upkeep: what the owner reported, what official sources say, and what the model uses.

Insurance could not be priced from an official source. The California Department of Insurance publishes a premium survey, but it only returns figures after choosing a location, coverage amount and home age in an interactive tool, and it does not publish averages by ZIP code. The owner's own estimates are used and flagged. Because the two homes need different policies — an owner-occupied homeowners policy for Property A, a landlord (rental dwelling) policy for Property B — quotes should be requested separately.
