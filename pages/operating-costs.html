Insurance is the largest unverified operating input

The owner's insurance figure is an estimate for both properties. Official sources publish shopping tools and explain that the FAIR Plan covers only fire or lightning, internal explosion and smoke, with theft and liability requiring a separate Differences in Conditions policy. A three-unit building with a partial owner-occupancy needs a landlord or mixed-use policy. Obtain written quotes before relying on any path.
