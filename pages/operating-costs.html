Why the $1,500/yr insurance estimate is too low to rely on

ConsiderationDetailOfficial basis
Statewide average vs. a high-value homeThe statewide average homeowner premium is roughly the same order of magnitude as the owner's estimate, but averages are dominated by ordinary homes. A rebuild of a multi-million-dollar dwelling at current construction cost is a different exposure, and the modeled figure reflects that.S36
Insurance is repricing faster than inflationMarket-wide premium increases have run ahead of consumer price inflation, which is why the model escalates insurance at 4.0% rather than 2.3%.S36 S37
A 3-unit building is not a homeowner policyA 3-unit building needs a dwelling policy covering rented units, loss of rents and landlord liability; the premium and the deductible structure differ from an owner-occupied single-family policy.S36 S12
Availability, not just priceThe FAIR Plan is the insurer of last resort; its policy count has grown sharply, which matters if either property becomes hard to insure.S37
