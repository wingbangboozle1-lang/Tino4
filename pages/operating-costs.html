Costs & insurance

Current law, owner figures, stated assumptions and open questions, kept apart on purpose.

No verified insurance premium exists for either property

The California Department of Insurance publishes comparison tools, a list of carriers writing difference-in-conditions policies and the coverage gaps in the FAIR Plan. It publishes no premium for any address. The owner estimate of $1,500 a year is carried as an owner figure everywhere it appears, never as a verified quote. For a rented A that same number is very likely too low, because a landlord policy on a high-value single-family home in this county is a different underwriting class from an owner-occupied policy.
