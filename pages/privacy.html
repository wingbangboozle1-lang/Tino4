Privacy & limits

Privacy is a design constraint, not a rounding promise.

Privacy comes first, by construction rather than by omission

This site publishes no property locality, address, parcel identifier, account number, lease, policy detail, tax document or unrounded figure. What it does publish is a rounded scenario model, on the owner's instruction, so that the arithmetic can be checked line by line; the exact records belong outside this repository.
