Privacy & publication

A clear distinction between current law, missing evidence and illustrative scenarios.

Highest priority: do not publish personal information

This is a public static site in a public Git repository. Noindex is a search preference, not authentication or confidentiality. The public build does not read private household inputs, contact private accounts or research addresses/parcel records.

Privacy safeguards and residual exposure

Official links support the legal framework only where a row is labeled Guidance, Missing or Synthetic; they do not verify private facts or invented scenario assumptions.
