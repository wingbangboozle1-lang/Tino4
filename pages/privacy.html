Privacy & limits

Privacy is enforced by keeping household inputs out of this public project, not by rounding them.

Privacy boundary

This public guide is generic and does not accept or publish household figures, property identifiers, private documents or a household-specific recommendation. Any property records, estimates, scenarios and decisions belong in a separate access-controlled offline workpaper. A public website, even with noindex metadata, is not private storage.

A static-site rebuild cannot remove prior Git objects or recall copies already downloaded. Before any history rewrite, preserve a private encrypted archive, coordinate collaborators, and follow the hosting provider's sensitive-data removal guidance. This repository's current checkout is not proof that all prior copies have been contained.
