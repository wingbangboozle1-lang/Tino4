Privacy & rounding

Current law, owner figures, stated assumptions and open questions, kept apart on purpose.

What was done to protect identity, and what was not

No person, street address, ZIP code, parcel number, account number or permit number appears anywhere in this site. No private lookup was performed: this session did not search for an owner, an address or a parcel record. Every dollar figure is rounded half-up to the nearest ten dollars before it is used in any calculation, so a published number cannot be worked back to an exact bill. Property names are labels, A and B. That is the whole of the protection, and it should be understood for exactly what it is.

Privacy safeguards and the limits of each

Status says what kind of evidence a row rests on: Verified means an official source states it, Owner means you supplied it and it is not independently confirmed, Projection means arithmetic on rounded inputs under a stated assumption, Assumption means an invented planning parameter, and Missing means the cell is blank because no evidence exists. A linked source supports the rule, not a private fact.

The remediation that only the owner can authorise

If the exposure of earlier commits matters, the options are: make the repository private, rewrite history and force-push, or start a clean repository and archive the old one. Each has a cost. A history rewrite invalidates every clone, every fork and every open pull request, and it cannot reach copies that have already been made. This session did none of those things, because they are not reversible and they affect other work.
