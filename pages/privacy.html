Privacy

The public dashboard excludes case-specific financial inputs and derived dollar projections.

Names, addresses, parcel numbers, exact case financial inputs, trust documents and other personal information do not belong in a public repository or website. Rounding to $10 and replacing names alone cannot guarantee anonymity, especially when distinctive figures are combined.

Location wording. Some official citations are jurisdiction-specific and their publisher names or URLs may identify the issuing jurisdiction. They provide legal context only; a citation does not verify that a household property is located there. The generated pages omit property addresses, ZIP codes, parcel/account identifiers and case-specific financial estimates, but this is not a guarantee of anonymity.

An earlier public Pages release and repository commits contained case-derived material. The current site withholds it, but a normal change cannot remove it from Git history, pull-request diffs, forks, caches or other branches. Ten-dollar rounding and placeholder labels are not sufficient anonymization. Repository owners should evaluate coordinated history remediation using GitHub’s sensitive-data guidance; do not place private material in issues, pull requests, task-board entries, build logs or public repository files.
