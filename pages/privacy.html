Privacy & limits

What this site publishes, what it refuses to publish, how rounding and placeholders protect the family, and the honest boundary between the current tree and Git history.

Privacy is the highest-urgency constraint

Personal information is anonymized with placeholders and rounded figures; anything discovered during research stays internal and never reaches the website; the verification rules are automated so a future edit cannot quietly leak a value.

Manual review checklist

Before any edit merges: run tools/build.py and tools/verify.py; search the diff for any five-digit number, address pattern, or unrounded currency; confirm no new source violates the official-host list; and re-read the flagged page so new assumptions are visible. The verifier automates the first two checks; the reviewer supplies the judgment.

Planning framework only. Not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal or investment advice. A source supports only its stated scope, not a private fact. Property figures are anonymized placeholders rounded to the nearest $10 per the owner's anti-tracing rule.
