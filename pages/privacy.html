Privacy & publication

A clear distinction between current law, missing evidence and review-only comparisons.

Highest priority: do not publish personal information

This is a public static site in a public Git repository. Noindex is a search preference, not authentication or confidentiality. The Pages build reads generic reviewed content only; it does not read household input files, access private accounts or research addresses or parcel records.

Official links support the stated rule or context; they do not verify household-specific facts or future outcomes.
