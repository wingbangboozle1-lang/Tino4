Privacy and limits

What is published, what is withheld, what is rounded, and what exposure still remains.

Privacy is the first release gate, and it is not fully closed

The published site contains no personal identifier and no unrounded private figure. It does contain rounded working figures and a public postal area, which is a real but bounded residual risk that is documented here rather than hidden. The larger unresolved exposure is the public Git history, which this branch cannot rewrite.

What to do if the residual risk is not acceptable

Remove the postal areas and re-run the utility and insurance research at county level. Widen the rounding to bands. Or keep the analysis in a private document and publish only the generic framework. Any of these keeps the structure of the analysis while reducing what can be matched against a public record.

Independently of the site, coordinate with the repository owner and GitHub Support about the public history. A clone, a fork or a cached view is not fixed by changing the pages.

Planning framework only. Not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal or investment advice. A source supports only its stated scope, never a private fact. Blank means unknown, not zero. No personal identifier is published on this site.
