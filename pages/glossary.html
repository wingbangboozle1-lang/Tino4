TermMeaningUseEvidenceOfficial source / scope
Estate taxTax potentially imposed on a decedent's taxable estate under federal law; not a tax simply triggered by every inheritance.Compute at estate level with counsel.GuidanceIRS — Estate tax
Income-tax basisThe tax basis used to determine gain or loss; inherited basis is generally tied to date-of-death value subject to exceptions.Obtain appraisal and CPA basis schedule.Verified ruleIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets
Assessed valueValue used for property-tax purposes under applicable assessment rules; not the same as income-tax basis or market value.Confirm with assessor and tax bill.GuidanceCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §51
Proposition 19 exclusionConditional property-tax treatment for qualifying family transfers of a principal residence or family farm.Confirm relationship, occupancy, filing and value-limit requirements.Verified ruleCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19 · California Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2
Depreciation recapturePotential income-tax consequences when depreciable rental property is disposed of; amount depends on depreciation and tax details.CPA reconciles depreciation allowed/allowable, basis and disposition.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets
