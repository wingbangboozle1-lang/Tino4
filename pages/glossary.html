Glossary

Plain-language definitions with official scope links.

Terms
TermMeaningUseEvidenceOfficial source / scope
Estate taxTax potentially imposed on a decedent's taxable estate under federal law; not a tax simply triggered by every inheritance.Compute at estate level with counsel.GuidanceIRS — Estate tax
Income-tax basisThe tax basis used to determine gain or loss; inherited basis is generally tied to date-of-death value subject to exceptions.Obtain appraisal and CPA basis schedule.Verified ruleIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets
Assessed valueValue used for property-tax purposes under applicable assessment rules; not the same as income-tax basis or market value.Confirm with assessor and tax bill.GuidanceCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §51
Proposition 19 exclusionConditional property-tax treatment for qualifying family transfers of a principal residence or family farm.Confirm relationship, occupancy, filing and value-limit requirements.Verified ruleCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19 · California Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2
Depreciation recapturePotential income-tax consequences when depreciable rental property is disposed of; amount depends on depreciation and tax details.CPA reconciles depreciation allowed/allowable, basis and disposition.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets
Sequence / reinvestment riskInvestment outcome depends on when gains and losses occur and when reserve instruments mature or are reinvested.Stress early and late crashes, lower renewal yields, and rising-market lag.GuidanceTreasuryDirect — Treasury bills · SEC Investor.gov — Stocks
Unrecaptured section 1250 gainThe part of gain on a sold rental equal to depreciation previously claimed; federally taxed at a maximum 25% rate, separate from the preferential long-term rates.CPA computes it from the private depreciation schedule.Verified ruleIRS Topic no. 409 — Capital gains and losses · IRS — Instructions for Schedule D (Form 1040)
Like-kind exchange (section 1031)A swap of business or investment real property that can defer gain; it involves a qualified intermediary, statutory deadlines, taxable boot, and carryover basis in the replacement.Decide in writing before listing; a primary residence never qualifies.Verified ruleIRS Publication 544 — Sales and Other Dispositions of Assets · IRS — Like-kind exchanges (real estate tax tips)
Documentary transfer taxA tax on the transfer of real property, generally computed on value in statutory increments and collected at recording under state authority and the local schedule.Price it into every sale path from the property's own enacted schedule.Verified ruleCalifornia statute — documentary transfer tax · California statute — recording fees
Imputed rentThe housing value consumed by living in a home one owns instead of paying rent elsewhere; a planning comparison, not a tax or a deduction.Include it when comparing live paths with rent or sell paths.GuidanceHUD — Fair Market Rents · IRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
Tax rate area (TRA)The geographic zone that determines which taxing jurisdictions apply and the total property-tax rate; identified on the tax bill and in the county's official rate compilation.Read the area and its published rate from the bill and county compilation.GuidanceCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §51 · California BOE — County assessor contacts
Factored base year value (FBYV)The Prop 13 base-year value adjusted by the annual inflation factor; it is the number the family-home exclusion protects.Read from the assessor's bill.Verified ruleCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §51 · California BOE — Letter to Assessors 2025/009
Behavioral Health Services TaxCalifornia's additional 1% tax on taxable income above $1,000,000 (2025 Form 540 line 62); it is income tax, not an inheritance tax.Model into any high-gain sale year with the CPA.Verified ruleCalifornia statute — additional tax on high taxable income · FTB — 2025 Form 540 instructions
Parcel taxA flat or size-based charge on parcels of real property, billed with the property-tax bill; the local 'Prop H' measure is a 15-year parcel tax for transit operations in Property B's jurisdiction.Confirm the parcel's tier and exemptions with the taxing authority.Verified ruleSFMTA - Proposition H parcel tax (approved Nov. 3, 2026)
Portability (estate tax)A surviving spouse's ability to use the deceased spouse's unused basic exclusion, generally by filing a timely estate-tax return.Ask about prior gifts and file Form 706 when advised.GuidanceIRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs · IRS — Estate tax
