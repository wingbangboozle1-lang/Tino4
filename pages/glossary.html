Each meaning is a plain-language summary of the linked official page. Read the page itself before relying on a figure.

Glossary
TermPlain meaningWhere it is usedStatusOfficial source / scope
Assessed valueThe value on the county roll that property tax is calculated on. It is not the market price.Property taxVerifiedBOE publication 29
Base-year valueThe assessed value set at the last change in ownership. Proposition 13 then limits its growth.Property taxVerifiedBOE publication 29
Change in ownershipA transfer that resets the assessed value to market value, unless an exclusion applies.Inheritance stepsVerifiedBOE publication 29
Proposition 19 (family-home transfer)Lets an eligible heir keep a lower assessed value on a family home when the heir uses it as a principal residence and files a claim within three years.Brief, timeline, decisionVerifiedBOE Proposition 19
Family-home allowanceThe amount by which market value may exceed the carried base value and still transfer under the exclusion. Re-indexed every second 16 February.Property taxVerifiedRTC 63.2 BOE Prop 19 fact sheet
Principal residenceThe home the owner actually lives in as the main home. Required on both sides of a Proposition 19 transfer.Live, rent or sellVerifiedBOE Proposition 19
Change-in-ownership statement (death transfer)Filed within 150 days after death when property passes through a trust, by the trustee or the transferee.TimelineVerifiedRTC 480
Trustee notificationThe trustee must serve notice within 60 days of the event that requires it, such as a revocable trust becoming irrevocable.TimelineVerifiedProbate Code 16061.7
Small-estate affidavitA simpler transfer procedure for estates below the state ceiling of $166,250.Inheritance stepsVerifiedProbate Code 13100
Stepped-up basisThe inherited property's tax basis is generally its fair market value on the date of death. Selling above that basis still produces taxable gain.Attorney and CPA sheetVerifiedIRS inherited basis
Form 706The federal estate and GST tax return. It is due nine months after death when a return is required.Estate taxVerifiedForm 706 instructions
Basic exclusion amountThe federal amount an estate can pass free of estate tax: $15,000,000 for deaths in 2026, $15,480,000 for deaths in 2027.Estate taxVerifiedIRS 2027 exclusion IRS 2026 exclusion
Annual gift exclusionThe amount that can be given to one recipient each year without a gift-tax return. $20,000 for 2027.Estate taxVerifiedIRS 2027 exclusion
California estate or inheritance taxCalifornia imposes none of its own. A dormant pickup provision applies only if a federal state-death-tax credit is reinstated.Estate taxVerifiedRTC 13301 RTC 13302
Unrecaptured section 1250 gainThe part of a gain on depreciated real estate that is taxed at a maximum 25% rate.Attorney and CPA sheetVerifiedIRS capital gains
Depreciation on rental propertyThe annual deduction for a rental building over its recovery period. It is recaptured on sale.CostsVerifiedIRS publication 527
Net investment income taxA tax on investment income, including rents and gains, above income thresholds. The rate and thresholds are on the linked IRS page.Attorney and CPA sheetVerifiedIRS NIIT
Homeowners' exemptionA $7,000 reduction in taxable value for an owner-occupied home, claimed by 15 February.CostsVerifiedBOE homeowners exemption
Inflation factor for assessmentsThe BOE factor applied to the assessed value each year. The 2026 factor is 1.02, the 2% cap.Property taxVerifiedBOE LTA 2026/002
Treasury billShort-term US government debt of 4 to 52 weeks. Its interest is subject to federal tax and not to state or local tax.Black-swan pageVerifiedTreasuryDirect bills
Rent ceiling (Civil Code 1947.12)Caps yearly rent increases at 5% plus the cost-of-living change, or 10%, whichever is lower. The statute expires on 1 January 2030.CostsVerifiedCivil Code 1947.12
FAIR PlanThe California property insurance plan for properties that standard insurers will not cover. Coverage is limited.CostsVerifiedDept of Insurance
Export credit (solar)Under net energy metering, exported power earns bill credits. Their value is usually lower than the retail rate.CostsVerifiedCPUC solar tariffs
