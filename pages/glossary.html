Glossary

Plain-language definitions for the decision framework.

Assessment / tax basis

Assessment is the value used by a property-tax authority to calculate property tax. Income-tax basis is a separate concept used to determine taxable gain; confirm the applicable basis with a tax professional.

Reinvestment risk

The risk that proceeds from a maturing investment must be reinvested at a lower yield.

Sequence risk

The effect of the timing of returns, especially losses early in a withdrawal or investment period.

Mixed path

A combination of choices, such as occupying part of a property, renting another lawful unit, retaining a separate property, or investing sale proceeds in stages.
