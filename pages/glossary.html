Plain meanings for the terms this site uses, each tied to the official source that defines it.

Glossary

Each definition is drawn from the official source linked beside it. Where a term is used loosely in the market, this glossary uses the statutory or official meaning.

Glossary
TermMeaning as used on this siteEvidenceOfficial source
Alternate valuation dateA valuation date six months after death that may be used only if a federal estate return is filed and the election is made on it.OfficialIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ
Assessment appeal windowThe annual period, July 2 to September 15 or to November 30, during which an application for reduction in assessed value is filed with the clerk of the county board of supervisors.OfficialBOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax: An Overview (March 2025)
Base year valueThe assessed value established when a property changes ownership or new construction is completed. It is generally increased by no more than 2 percent a year afterwards.OfficialBOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax: An Overview (March 2025)
Break-even rentThe gross annual rent needed to cover property tax, operating cost, vacancy and management before any financing. Used here instead of an invented market rent.OfficialIRS — Publication 527, Residential Rental Property
Change in ownershipThe event that triggers reassessment in California. A transfer by reason of death is a change in ownership unless an exclusion applies.OfficialBOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax: An Overview (March 2025)
Change-in-ownership statementThe signed statement filed with the county recorder or assessor. It is due within 150 days after death where the transfer is by reason of death outside probate, including through a trust.OfficialCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §480
Consistent basis reportingA rule that can require a recipient's basis to match the value finally determined for federal estate tax purposes, tied to Form 8971 Schedule A.OfficialIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ
Equity return assumptionThe nominal annual return used to grow invested proceeds in the model. It is an input, not a forecast.OfficialSEC Investor.gov — stocks
Factored base year valueThe base year value after the annual inflation adjustments permitted by the Constitution.OfficialCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2
FAIR PlanCalifornia's insurer of last resort. It covers fire or lightning, internal explosion and smoke, and other perils must usually be added through a Differences in Conditions policy.OfficialCalifornia Department of Insurance — home/residential insurance
Form 706The United States Estate and Generation-Skipping Transfer Tax Return. Generally due nine months after death, with a six-month extension only if requested before the due date.OfficialIRS — Filing estate and gift tax returns
Gross estateEverything the decedent owns or has certain interests in at death, valued at fair market value, before deductions.OfficialIRS — Estate tax
Homeowners' exemptionA $7,000 reduction in the full value of an owner-occupied dwelling. It does not apply where the dwelling is rented, vacant or a secondary home.OfficialCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §218
Intergenerational transfer exclusionThe Proposition 19 exclusion for a transfer of a principal residence between parents and children, or in defined circumstances grandparents and grandchildren, subject to occupancy, filing and value conditions.OfficialCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2
Lien dateJanuary 1. The date on which property is valued and exemptions are determined for the following fiscal year.OfficialBOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax: An Overview (March 2025)
Net billing tariffThe solar export tariff under which credits are usually below retail rates, as opposed to the older net energy metering tariffs.OfficialCPUC — net energy metering and net billing
PhasingMoving staged cash into equities gradually over a stated period rather than in one transaction.OfficialTreasuryDirect — Treasury notes
PortabilityAn election allowing a surviving spouse to use a deceased spouse's unused federal exclusion. It is made on a timely filed estate tax return.OfficialIRS — Frequently asked questions on estate taxes
Reinvestment riskThe risk that the yield available when staged cash is finally invested is lower than the yield available when the plan started.OfficialU.S. Treasury — Daily Treasury par yield curve rates
Sequence riskThe risk that the order of returns matters, so a decline early in the period does more damage than the same decline later.OfficialFederal Reserve History — Stock Market Crash of 1929
StagingHolding sale proceeds in short-dated government paper for a stated window before investing in equities.OfficialTreasuryDirect — Treasury bills
State and local tax deduction limitThe cap on the itemized deduction for state and local taxes: $40,000, or $20,000 if married filing separately, subject to a modified adjusted gross income limitation but not reduced below $10,000.OfficialIRS — Topic no. 503, Deductible taxes
Step-up in basisThe general rule that the basis of inherited property is its fair market value on the date of death rather than the decedent's original cost.OfficialIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ
Tax-rate areaThe geographic combination of local agencies and voter-approved debt whose rates apply to a parcel. It sets the composite rate on the bill.OfficialCalifornia BOE — boe.ca.gov entry point
Trustee notificationThe notice a trustee must serve within 60 days after a revocable trust becomes irrevocable by reason of death.OfficialCalifornia Probate Code §16061.7
Value limitUnder Proposition 19, the prior taxable value plus the inflation-adjusted amount. Any fair market value above it is added to the new taxable value.OfficialBOE Letter To Assessors No. 2025/009
