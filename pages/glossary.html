Glossary

Short definitions for the legal, tax, property and investment terms used in the decision process.

Terms used in this workspace
TermPlain-language meaningPractical distinctionEvidence statusOfficial source / scope
Tax structure: Property and interests included in the federal estate-tax calculation before allowable deductions; includes more than one house.Do not compare one inheritor's share alone with the federal filing threshold.VerifiedIRS — Estate tax
Tax structure: Federal estate/gift tax amount available under current law for a given year, subject to the statute and prior taxable transfers.It is not the annual gift exclusion and not a guarantee about a later year.VerifiedIRS — Estate tax · IRS IR-2026-133 — 2027 estate and gift adjustments
Tax structure: A tax-accounting amount used to compute gain or loss on sale; inherited property basis is generally date-of-death fair market value, subject to exceptions.Keep separate from the county's property-tax assessed value.VerifiedIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ
Property tax: Value used in the property-tax system; California rules can constrain growth and change the base on a change in ownership, with specified exclusions.Not the same as current market value, sale price or income-tax basis.VerifiedCalifornia BOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax · California BOE — Proposition 19
Property tax: A conditional intergenerational property-tax rule with relationship, residence, occupancy and claim requirements.Trust title alone does not prove qualification.VerifiedCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2 · California BOE — Proposition 19
Decision structure: A combination such as occupying one lawful unit, renting another, retaining an already-owned property, or selling one asset and staging the proceeds.Model each asset, unit and tax position separately.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · California Civil Code §1947.12
Decision structure: The effect of when returns occur; an early decline can matter more than the same decline later when funds are being invested or withdrawn.Stress both crash-during-staging and crash-after-staging cases; neither is a probability forecast.GuidanceFederal Reserve History — Stock Market Crash of 1929 · Federal Reserve History — The Great Recession · SEC Investor.gov — Stocks FAQs
Decision structure: The possibility that proceeds from a maturing investment must be reinvested at a lower yield.A short Treasury ladder can defer equity exposure but does not lock a future renewal yield.VerifiedTreasuryDirect — Treasury bills · TreasuryDirect — Treasury notes
Fair Market Rent (FMR)HUD's annual 40th-percentile gross-rent benchmark used for specified housing programs.It is not a property appraisal, a lease amount or a determination of legal rent.VerifiedHUD — Fair Market Rents
Factored base year valueThe property's long-held taxable value under Proposition 13, grown by up to 2% per year; the starting point for any exclusion math at transfer.Owner-supplied history on this site is rounded to the nearest ten dollars; the assessor's roll is authoritative.VerifiedCalifornia BOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax · California Revenue and Taxation Code §51 — Taxable value of real property
Homeowners' exemptionA $7,000 full-value exemption for an owner-occupied principal residence; Proposition 19 requires filing it within one year of a qualifying family-home transfer.Its absence can disqualify the intergenerational exclusion even if occupancy started in time.VerifiedCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §218 — Homeowners' property tax exemption · California BOE — Proposition 19
Step-up in basisInherited property's income-tax basis is generally its date-of-death fair market value, so appreciation during the decedent's life is not taxed at transfer.Separate from property-tax assessed value; a later sale can still create gain from post-death appreciation.VerifiedIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets
Section 121 exclusionUp to $250,000 ($500,000 married filing jointly) of gain excluded when selling a main home after the two-out-of-five-year ownership and use tests.A rented inherited home generally fails the use test unless the owner lived in it two of the last five years first.VerifiedIRS Topic no. 701 — Sale of your home
Net investment income taxA 3.8% tax on net investment income (including most rent and capital gains) above the $200,000/$250,000 MAGI thresholds.Applies on top of ordinary capital-gains tax; a CPA must confirm applicability.Cross-checkedIRS — Net Investment Income Tax
FAIR PlanCalifornia's insurer of last resort for fire perils when the standard market will not write a policy; coverage is narrower than a standard homeowners form.Third-party reporting describes an average 29% dwelling rate increase from late 2026; treat insurance cost as a stressed input.Cross-checkedCalifornia Department of Insurance — Residential insurance · California Department of Insurance — Newsroom
Laddered / staged entrySplitting a lump sum into scheduled purchases over months (here, after parking in 3-month-to-2-year Treasuries for 24-36 months).Reduces the chance of buying everything at a pre-crash peak; costs relative performance in a steadily rising market and adds renewal-rate risk.GuidanceTreasuryDirect — Treasury bills · TreasuryDirect — Treasury notes · SEC Investor.gov — Stocks FAQs
Documentary transfer taxA county/city tax on recorded property transfers (about 55 cents per $500 statewide base, with local variations); certain family transfers are excluded by statute.Confirm the current statutory exclusion and local rate with the recorder at the actual transfer.GuidanceCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §480
Depreciation (rental real property)The annual deduction for wear and tear on a rental BUILDING — never the land — taken straight-line over the MACRS residential-rental recovery period of 27.5 years.It reduces taxable rent now and reduces the basis, so the same amount is waiting at sale. A home you live in is not depreciable.VerifiedIRS Publication 527 — Depreciation of rental property (MACRS)
Unrecaptured section 1250 gain (depreciation recapture)The part of a long-term gain on depreciated real property that is attributable to the depreciation allowed or allowable; taxed at a maximum 25% federal rate.It is not a penalty for taking depreciation — the deduction is clawed back whether or not it was actually claimed. California taxes the same amount as ordinary income.VerifiedIRS Topic 409 — Capital gains and losses · FTB — Capital gains and losses
Stepped-up basis at deathFor property acquired from a decedent, basis is generally the fair market value at the date of death (or the alternate valuation), and a Schedule A from the estate can bind the beneficiary's initial basis.This is why a sale in the same tax year as the transfer can produce little or no gain — and why an inherited rental starts with a NEW depreciation base rather than the decedent's.VerifiedIRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets (Inherited Property; Like-Kind Exchanges)
Imputed (avoided) rentThe market rent a household does not pay because it owns and occupies the home; used only to compare an owner-occupied path against a path that must rent.It is not income, not a deduction, and not cash. Without it, a live-in path looks free and a sell-and-reinvest path looks better than it is.GuidanceHUD — Fair Market Rents · IRS Publication 527 — Depreciation of rental property (MACRS)
Documentary / real-estate transfer taxA tax on the document that transfers real property, charged per $500 of consideration at the applicable tier; some jurisdictions exempt transfers at death and specified family transfers.In the city-county schedule the tier rate applies to the ENTIRE consideration, not marginally, so a price just below a tier line is cheaper than one just above it.VerifiedSan Francisco Assessor-Recorder — Fees to record a document in San Francisco
Preliminary Change of Ownership Report (PCOR)The screening form filed with a transfer document so the assessor can evaluate whether the ownership change reassesses the property.Recording a transfer document without it can trigger a fee in lieu; on a death transfer the separate change-in-ownership statement has its own 150-day deadline.VerifiedSan Francisco Assessor-Recorder — Fees to record a document in San Francisco · California Revenue and Taxation Code §480
Section 1031 like-kind exchangeDeferral, not exemption: exchanging real property held for business or investment for replacement real property held for business or investment, with a generally carried-over basis.A home the heir lives in is not held for investment, so it does not qualify; deferring the gain also carries the recapture forward into the replacement property.VerifiedIRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets (Inherited Property; Like-Kind Exchanges) · IRS — About Form 8824, Like-Kind Exchanges
Outcome distribution vs point estimateThe set of ending values a model produces across its assumption bands, shown as a spread (percentile bars) rather than a single number.A '6% a year' headline is one bar of a wide histogram; the ranking of hold-versus-sell depends on the tails, not the middle.GuidanceSEC Investor.gov — Stocks FAQs · Congressional Budget Office — The Long-Term Budget Outlook: 2025 to 2055
