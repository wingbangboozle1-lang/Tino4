Glossary

Short definitions for the legal, tax, property and investment terms used in the decision process.

Terms used in this workspace
TermPlain-language meaningPractical distinctionEvidence statusOfficial source / scope
Tax structure: Property and interests included in the federal estate-tax calculation before allowable deductions; includes more than one house.Do not compare one inheritor's share alone with the federal filing threshold.VerifiedIRS — Estate tax
Tax structure: Federal estate/gift tax amount available under current law for a given year, subject to the statute and prior taxable transfers.It is not the annual gift exclusion and not a guarantee about a later year.VerifiedIRS — Estate tax · IRS IR-2026-133 — 2027 estate and gift adjustments
Tax structure: A tax-accounting amount used to compute gain or loss on sale; inherited property basis is generally date-of-death fair market value, subject to exceptions.Keep separate from the county's property-tax assessed value.VerifiedIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ
Property tax: Value used in the property-tax system; California rules can constrain growth and change the base on a change in ownership, with specified exclusions.Not the same as current market value, sale price or income-tax basis.VerifiedCalifornia BOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax · California BOE — Proposition 19
Property tax: A conditional intergenerational property-tax rule with relationship, residence, occupancy and claim requirements.Trust title alone does not prove qualification.VerifiedCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2 · California BOE — Proposition 19
Decision structure: A combination such as occupying one lawful unit, renting another, retaining an already-owned property, or selling one asset and staging the proceeds.Model each asset, unit and tax position separately.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · California Civil Code §1947.12
Decision structure: The effect of when returns occur; an early decline can matter more than the same decline later when funds are being invested or withdrawn.Stress both crash-during-staging and crash-after-staging cases; neither is a probability forecast.GuidanceFederal Reserve History — Stock Market Crash of 1929 · Federal Reserve History — The Great Recession · SEC Investor.gov — Stocks FAQs
Decision structure: The possibility that proceeds from a maturing investment must be reinvested at a lower yield.A short Treasury ladder can defer equity exposure but does not lock a future renewal yield.VerifiedTreasuryDirect — Treasury bills · TreasuryDirect — Treasury notes
Fair Market Rent (FMR)HUD's annual 40th-percentile gross-rent benchmark used for specified housing programs.It is not a property appraisal, a lease amount or a determination of legal rent.VerifiedHUD — Fair Market Rents
Factored base year valueThe property's long-held taxable value under Proposition 13, grown by up to 2% per year; the starting point for any exclusion math at transfer.Owner-supplied history on this site is rounded to the nearest ten dollars; the assessor's roll is authoritative.VerifiedCalifornia BOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax · California Revenue and Taxation Code §51 — Taxable value of real property
Homeowners' exemptionA $7,000 full-value exemption for an owner-occupied principal residence; Proposition 19 requires filing it within one year of a qualifying family-home transfer.Its absence can disqualify the intergenerational exclusion even if occupancy started in time.VerifiedCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §218 — Homeowners' property tax exemption · California BOE — Proposition 19
Step-up in basisInherited property's income-tax basis is generally its date-of-death fair market value, so appreciation during the decedent's life is not taxed at transfer.Separate from property-tax assessed value; a later sale can still create gain from post-death appreciation.VerifiedIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets
Section 121 exclusionUp to $250,000 ($500,000 married filing jointly) of gain excluded when selling a main home after the two-out-of-five-year ownership and use tests.A rented inherited home generally fails the use test unless the owner lived in it two of the last five years first.VerifiedIRS Topic no. 701 — Sale of your home
Net investment income taxA 3.8% tax on net investment income (including most rent and capital gains) above the $200,000/$250,000 MAGI thresholds.Applies on top of ordinary capital-gains tax; a CPA must confirm applicability.Cross-checkedIRS — Net Investment Income Tax
FAIR PlanCalifornia's insurer of last resort for fire perils when the standard market will not write a policy; coverage is narrower than a standard homeowners form.Third-party reporting describes an average 29% dwelling rate increase from late 2026; treat insurance cost as a stressed input.Cross-checkedCalifornia Department of Insurance — Residential insurance · California Department of Insurance — Newsroom
Laddered / staged entrySplitting a lump sum into scheduled purchases over months (here, after parking in 3-month-to-2-year Treasuries for 24-36 months).Reduces the chance of buying everything at a pre-crash peak; costs relative performance in a steadily rising market and adds renewal-rate risk.GuidanceTreasuryDirect — Treasury bills · TreasuryDirect — Treasury notes · SEC Investor.gov — Stocks FAQs
Documentary transfer taxA county/city tax on recorded property transfers, imposed at 55 cents per 500 dollars of consideration over 100 dollars where the county has adopted it; §11930 excludes an instrument that transfers realty outright to, or in trust for, any person by reason of an inter vivos gift or a death, and §11932 requires any tax due to be shown on the face of the recorded document.Confirm the current statutory exclusion and local rate with the recorder at the actual transfer.GuidanceCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §480
Unrecaptured section 1250 gain (depreciation recapture)The part of a sale gain on depreciated real property that is taxed at a maximum 25% federal rate instead of the ordinary long-term rate.It is the price of having claimed depreciation while renting; it cannot be avoided by reclassifying the gain.VerifiedIRS Tax Topic 409 — Capital gain and loss
Value limit (Proposition 19)The excluded amount for an intergenerational family-home transfer is the transferor's factored base year value plus a periodically adjusted limit; value above that sum is added to the transferred base.It is why a high-value inherited home does not keep its old tax bill, and why the modeled relief is far smaller than the raw difference between the old and new bases.VerifiedCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19 · California BOE — Letter to Assessors 2026/026 (Proposition 19)
Base-year value transfer (replacement home)A separate Proposition 19 benefit allowing an eligible seller who occupied the original home to carry its factored base year value to a replacement principal residence bought or built within two years, with the excess in value added to the base.Distinct from the family-home exclusion: different conditions, different filing clock, and it is the allowance that can be carried forward after the heir occupies and later sells.VerifiedCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19
Alternate valuationAn estate may value property six months after the date of death — and at the date of any sale or distribution inside that window — only if it lowers both the gross estate and the combined estate and generation-skipping transfer tax; basis then follows the alternate figure.A sale signed inside six months can tie the estate value and the heir's basis to one number.VerifiedIRS — About Form 706 (United States Estate Tax Return) · IRS — Instructions for Schedule D (Form 1041)
PortabilityThe election by which an estate transfers its unused federal exclusion to a surviving spouse, which can require a Form 706 filing even when no return would otherwise be due.It is an election on a return, not an automatic entitlement; whether one was made is a question to ask now.VerifiedIRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs
Adjusted taxable giftsLifetime taxable gifts added to the gross estate for the estate-tax filing test and reducing the exclusion available at death; reported on Form 706 Schedule K.A headroom calculation that omits prior gifts is wrong in the direction that matters most.VerifiedIRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs · IRS — About Form 706 (United States Estate Tax Return)
Like-kind exchange (section 1031)A deferral, available only for real property held for business or investment, of gain when it is exchanged for other business or investment real property; property held primarily for sale never qualifies.With a stepped-up basis there is usually little gain to defer, so the decision is normally 'no' — and it should be written down before listing.VerifiedIRS — Like-kind exchanges: real estate tax tips
Imputed rentA modeling convention that values the housing a resident owner receives by occupying a property they could otherwise let, usually gross market rent net of operating load.It is not income to anyone, not deductible by anyone and not taxable; it exists so that occupying and letting are compared on the same line.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
Rent hurdle and break-even rentTwo derived numbers, not market data: the hurdle is the gross rent a rented path must earn to offset the property-tax branch difference; the break-even is the rent at which letting overtakes the value of the Proposition 19 relief alone.They differ because of how the operating load treats the tax line. Both move with the assessor's actual levy, so read the bill first.ProjectionOwner-supplied records — Property A (privacy-rounded to nearest $10) · IRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
Tax rate areaThe parcel-level levy jurisdiction used in Santa Clara County's published rate compilation; 816 areas exist for fiscal 2025-26 and the first two digits identify the city or unsecured area.It is read off the parcel's own bill. The number is a parcel identifier, so it is deliberately not published on this site.VerifiedSanta Clara County — Compilation of Tax Rates FY 2025-26
Supplemental assessmentA separate, prorated bill issued when a change in ownership or new construction causes reassessment, appealable within 60 days of the notice and usually not handled inside escrow.It can arrive against the estate in the transfer year, before or without a sale.VerifiedSanta Clara County Assessor — 2026 Assessment Information booklet
Proposition H (San Francisco)The 15-year Muni parcel tax approved on November 3, 2026, billed with the property tax from fiscal 2027-28 through 2041-42, with separate single-family, multifamily, commercial and vacant-land rates and a limited pass-through for rent-controlled owners.It is an owner-paid parcel tax, not a rent regulation, and it belongs in Property B's carrying stack in every path.VerifiedSFMTA — Muni Proposition H parcel tax · San Francisco Elections — November 3, 2026 results
