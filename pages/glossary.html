Glossary

Short definitions for the legal, tax, property and investment terms used in the decision process.

Terms used in this workspace

Terms used in this workspace
TermPlain-language meaningPractical distinctionEvidence statusOfficial source / scope
Gross estateProperty and interests included in the federal estate-tax calculation before allowable deductions; includes more than one house.Do not compare one inheritor's share alone with the federal filing threshold.VerifiedIRS — Estate tax
Basic exclusion amountFederal estate/gift tax amount available under current law for a given year, subject to the statute and prior taxable transfers.It is not the annual gift exclusion and not a guarantee about a later year.VerifiedIRS — Estate tax · IRS IR-2026-133 — 2027 estate and gift adjustments
Income-tax basisA tax-accounting amount used to compute gain or loss on sale; inherited property basis is generally date-of-death fair market value, subject to exceptions.Keep separate from the county's property-tax assessed value.VerifiedIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ
Assessed valueValue used in the property-tax system; California rules can constrain growth and change the base on a change in ownership, with specified exclusions.Not the same as current market value, sale price or income-tax basis.VerifiedCalifornia BOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax · California BOE — Proposition 19
Proposition 19 family-home exclusionA conditional intergenerational property-tax rule with relationship, residence, occupancy and claim requirements.Trust title alone does not prove qualification.VerifiedCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2 · California BOE — Proposition 19
Mixed pathA combination such as occupying one lawful unit, renting another, retaining an already-owned property, or selling one asset and staging the proceeds.Model each asset, unit and tax position separately.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · California Civil Code §1947.12
Sequence riskThe effect of when returns occur; an early decline can matter more than the same decline later when funds are being invested or withdrawn.Stress both crash-during-staging and crash-after-staging cases; neither is a probability forecast.GuidanceFederal Reserve History — Stock Market Crash of 1929 · Federal Reserve History — The Great Recession · SEC Investor.gov — Stocks FAQs
Reinvestment riskThe possibility that proceeds from a maturing investment must be reinvested at a lower yield.A short Treasury ladder can defer equity exposure but does not lock a future renewal yield.VerifiedTreasuryDirect — Treasury bills · TreasuryDirect — Treasury notes
Fair Market Rent (FMR)HUD's annual 40th-percentile gross-rent benchmark used for specified housing programs.It is not a property appraisal, a lease amount or a determination of legal rent.VerifiedHUD — Fair Market Rents
