Glossary

Short definitions for the legal, tax, property and investment terms used across the decision process.

Terms used in this workspace

Terms used in this workspace
TermPlain-language meaningPractical distinctionEvidence statusOfficial source / scope
Gross estateProperty and interests included in the federal estate-tax calculation before deductions; includes more than one house plus cash, securities and more.Do not compare one heir's share with the federal filing threshold.VerifiedIRS — Estate tax
Basic exclusion amountFederal estate/gift amount available for a given year under current law, subject to prior taxable transfers.Not the annual gift exclusion; not a guarantee about later years.VerifiedIRS — Estate tax · IRS — Rev. Proc. 2025-32 (2026 inflation adjustments)
Portability / DSUEElection (on a timely Form 706) to transfer a deceased spouse's unused exclusion to the surviving spouse.Can require filing even below the threshold; Rev. Proc. 2022-32 offers a limited late-election method.VerifiedIRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs
IRD (income in respect of a decedent)Assets whose income was never taxed to the decedent — typically pre-tax retirement accounts — taxed to the beneficiary on receipt/withdrawal.A dollar of IRA inheritance is worth less after income tax than a dollar of stepped-up securities.VerifiedIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ
Income-tax basisAmount used to compute gain on sale; inherited property basis is generally date-of-death fair market value, subject to exceptions.Separate from the county's property-tax assessed value.VerifiedIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ
Factored base year valueCalifornia's Prop 13 assessed value: the original base-year value adjusted by up to 2% per year.Why A's assessment (≈$550,540) is far below market value (≈$3,100,000).VerifiedCalifornia BOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax
Proposition 19 value capThe family-home exclusion covers value up to the factored base year value plus an adjusted $1,000,000 amount ($1,044,586 for transfers 2/16/2025–2/15/2027; adjusted biennially by the FHFA California HPI).Above the cap, the excess is reassessed even when the exclusion applies.VerifiedCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19 · California BOE Letter to Assessors 2025/009
Partial reassessmentResult of the cap math: taxable value = factored base year value + the amount by which market value exceeds factored value + cap.For high-value homes this is close to full reassessment minus the cap — the 2040/2050 tables quantify it.VerifiedCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19
Section 121 exclusionUp to $250,000 ($500,000 joint) of gain on a principal residence may be excluded if the 2-of-5-year ownership and use tests are met.Relevant only to Property B if ever sold while/after being the residence.VerifiedIRS Topic no. 701 — Sale of your home
Mixed pathA combination — e.g., occupy one property, rent units of another, sell one asset and stage the proceeds.Model each asset, unit and tax position separately.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · California Civil Code §1947.12
Sequence riskThe effect of when returns occur; an early decline hurts more than the same decline later while funds are being invested.Stress crash-during-staging and crash-after-staging; neither is a probability.GuidanceFederal Reserve History — Stock Market Crash of 1929 · Federal Reserve History — The Great Recession · SEC Investor.gov — Stocks FAQs
Reinvestment riskThe possibility that maturing proceeds must be reinvested at a lower yield.A 24–36 month Treasury ladder defers equity exposure but does not lock renewal yields.VerifiedTreasuryDirect — Treasury bills · TreasuryDirect — Treasury notes
NEM / net billing (NBT)California solar export-credit regimes; under net billing, export credits are usually below retail rates.Equipment alone does not establish future credit value — the account tariff does.VerifiedCalifornia Public Utilities Commission — Net metering and net billing
FAIR Plan / DIC wrapCalifornia's insurer-of-last-resort dwelling-fire program (limited perils) plus a Difference-in-Conditions policy that fills gaps.A FAIR Plan policy alone is not complete homeowners coverage.VerifiedCalifornia Department of Insurance — Residential insurance
Fair Market Rent (FMR)HUD's annual 40th-percentile gross-rent benchmark for specified programs.Not an appraisal, lease amount or lawful-rent determination.VerifiedHUD — Fair Market Rents
SB 79California transit-oriented development law (effective July 1, 2026) allowing qualifying housing near transit stops in urban transit counties.Can affect zoning expectations and value near transit in San Francisco.VerifiedCalifornia HCD — SB 79 Transit-Oriented Development
