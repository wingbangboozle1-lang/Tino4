Glossary

Plain-language definitions with official scope links.

Terms
TermMeaningUseEvidenceOfficial source / scope
Estate taxTax potentially imposed on a decedent's taxable estate under federal law; not a tax simply triggered by every inheritance.Compute at estate level with counsel.GuidanceIRS — Estate tax
Income-tax basisThe tax basis used to determine gain or loss; inherited basis is generally tied to date-of-death value subject to exceptions.Obtain appraisal and CPA basis schedule.Verified ruleIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets
Assessed valueValue used for property-tax purposes under applicable assessment rules; not the same as income-tax basis or market value.Confirm with assessor and tax bill.GuidanceCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §51
Proposition 19 exclusionConditional property-tax treatment for qualifying family transfers of a principal residence or family farm.Confirm relationship, occupancy, filing and value-limit requirements.Verified ruleCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19 · California Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2
Depreciation recapturePotential income-tax consequences when depreciable rental property is disposed of; amount depends on depreciation and tax details.CPA reconciles depreciation allowed/allowable, basis and disposition.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets
Sequence / reinvestment riskInvestment outcome depends on when gains and losses occur and when reserve instruments mature or are reinvested.Stress early and late crashes, lower renewal yields, and rising-market lag.GuidanceTreasuryDirect — Treasury bills · SEC Investor.gov — Stocks
Unrecaptured section 1250 gainThe part of a long-term gain on depreciated real property attributable to depreciation allowed or allowable, taxed at a capped federal rate.Treat depreciation as a loan from the tax authority: it shelters income now and is repaid at sale, and California taxes the same amount as ordinary income.Verified ruleIRS Topic 409 — Capital gains and losses · FTB — Capital gains and losses
Basis used for depreciationThe depreciable portion of cost or other basis — the building, not the land — reduced by depreciation taken or required to be taken.Ask for the land/building allocation and the depreciation schedule, not just the purchase price.Verified ruleIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets
Stepped-up basisFor property acquired from a decedent, basis is generally the fair market value at death, subject to exceptions and to estate basis-reporting consistency.It explains why a sale soon after a transfer can produce little gain, and why an inherited rental begins a fresh recovery period.Verified ruleIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets · IRS — About Form 8971 (basis reporting)
Documentary transfer taxA tax on the document that transfers real property, commonly stated per 500 dollars of consideration, with local rates, tiers and exemptions set by ordinance.Price the sale, not just the price: the tier method can make a lower price meaningfully cheaper to convey.Verified ruleCalifornia statute — documentary transfer tax · San Francisco County Recorder — fees to record a document
Change-of-ownership screeningThe filing that lets the assessor evaluate whether a transfer changes the taxable value, required on its own deadline for specified death transfers, with a fee when a transfer document is recorded without it.Filing it is not agreement that reassessment is owed; the property-tax claim is a separate, later filing.Verified ruleCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §480 · San Francisco County Recorder — fees to record a document
Imputed, or avoided, rentThe market rent a household does not pay because it occupies a home it owns, used only to compare an owned path against a path that must buy housing.Never booked as income. Its whole purpose is to stop a live-in path being credited with free housing.GuidanceHUD — Fair Market Rents · IRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
Outcome distributionThe set of ending values a model produces across its assumption bands, reported as a spread with the number of cells shown.A point estimate is one bar of a histogram; the ranking of live, rent and sell depends on the tails.GuidanceCongressional Budget Office — Long-Term Budget Outlook · Federal Housing Finance Agency — House Price Index · SEC Investor.gov — Stocks
Re-check cadenceThe published schedule on which each rule, amount and assumption is re-read from its official source.A decision aid goes stale in silence unless the re-read is scheduled and named.GuidanceIRS — 2027 tax inflation adjustments · California BOE — Letter to Assessors 2025/009 · Congress.gov — Public Law 119-21
