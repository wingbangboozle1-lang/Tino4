Black-swan conditions

A clear distinction between current law, missing evidence and illustrative scenarios.

Stress tests are not probabilities

These are invented adverse paths, not historical backtests or predictions. Known shocks do not bound unknown black swans. No win probability, 98% success rate or perpetual growth claim is made. Tax rates and future law are held fixed only to isolate economic shocks.
