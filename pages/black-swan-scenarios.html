Black-swan conditions

A clear distinction between current law, missing evidence and review-only comparisons.

Stress conditions, not invented probabilities

A long horizon can include interacting, non-uniform shocks. This page identifies conditions to test without assigning unsupported probabilities, returns, cost escalators or household dollar outcomes. Historical observations are context, not forecasts.
