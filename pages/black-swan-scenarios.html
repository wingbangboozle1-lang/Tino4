Black-swan economic scenarios

A 50-year index illustrates uneven returns and inflation, not a prediction or estimate of either property.

Hypothetical, not an official forecast. Both series start at 100 in 2026. Smooth home prices rise 3% annually and smooth prices rise 2%; stress uses the same defaults except for the shocks listed below. No private financial inputs are used. Source links describe historical context only, not these chosen assumptions.

Why stress test?

National CPI rose 9.1% in the 12 months ending June 2022, showing that inflation need not be stable. A price index is not a parcel appraisal and history does not predict the next shock.
