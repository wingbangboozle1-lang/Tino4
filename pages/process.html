Inheritance process

What actually happens, in order

Fourteen steps, four phases. The expensive mistakes are all deadline mistakes: the one-year occupancy and exemption filings that preserve Prop 19 relief, the nine-month federal estate tax return, and the decision to rent the house out before the exclusion has been secured.

The 30-second version

Trustee deeds the house to you → assessor reassesses it as of the death date → you move in within 12 months and file the homeowners' exemption → you file BOE-19-P within 3 years → the taxable value drops back to the parent's base plus any excess over the indexed $1M cap. If you rent it out instead, the reassessment stands and the exclusion never applies.

Deadlines are the ones that carry a tax consequence. Probate timelines are deliberately excluded: with the property titled to the trust, no probate is expected for the real property.

How the trust changes the picture

  • No probate for the real property if the deed is in the trust's name. That saves the statutory probate fees and, more importantly, the 12–18 months a probate can add — which matters because the Prop 19 clock starts at the transfer, not at the end of an estate administration.
  • The trust does not create a property tax exclusion. A trust is a title-holding device; Prop 19 is about who occupied the house and who occupies it next.
  • If the trust keeps the house (for example, a continuing trust with a right of occupancy), the assessor looks at who has the present beneficial ownership and whether the occupancy and exemption conditions are met. Get this structure reviewed before death, not after.
  • Trustee duties continue after death. The trustee must administer prudently, account to beneficiaries and file fiduciary income tax returns (Probate Code §16000 et seq.).

Document checklist

Before death

  • Recorded deed showing the trust as owner
  • Trust instrument and any amendments
  • Purchase closing statement and improvement receipts
  • Homeowners' exemption confirmation for the residence
  • Insurance declarations pages
  • Solar agreement / NEM tariff documentation

After death

  • Certified death certificates
  • Date-of-death appraisal
  • Trustee's deed and PCOR
  • BOE-19-P and homeowners' exemption filings (with proof of filing)
  • Form 706 if required; Form 541 / 1041
  • Supplemental tax bills and payment confirmations
  • Boundary of coverage: new insurance policy in the heir's name
