Open questions — what must be answered before any decision

The questions, information and answers that must be resolved before the household can reasonably choose live in, rent out, or sell.

Privacy & verification controls. The two properties appear only as Property A (the trust-held residence to be inherited) and Property B (the already-owned 3-unit building). No name, street address, parcel or account number, city name, neighbourhood name or ZIP code is published. Every owner-supplied figure was rounded to the nearest $10 before modelling, so exact tax-roll values cannot be matched by search. Official cells that no government source supports are left blank and explained on the Flagged for review page.

How to use this list

Treat every item marked Unanswered or Partial as a gate: do not finalize live-in / rent-out / sell until the information is gathered or a reason to proceed without it is recorded. Answers that change (for example, the estate exclusion or the Proposition 19 allowance) should be re-entered here and the dashboard re-run.

Model output, not tax, legal, investment or appraisal advice. Every published dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10; exact official statutory amounts are quoted as published and linked. Personal identities, street addresses, parcel numbers, city names, neighbourhood names and ZIP codes are not published anywhere on this site.
