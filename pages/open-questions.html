Suggestions for discovering the missing information

  • Review the trust instrument with an estate attorney: stepped-up basis qualification and Prop. 19 eligibility depend on it (IRC section 1014 / CA RTC section 18031; BOE Prop. 19 rules).
  • Ask the trustee for the estate marshaling summary and the Form 706 filing determination (IRS Form 706 instructions).
  • Commission a date-of-death appraisal and a rental market appraisal (county assessor guidance; HUD small-area rent benchmarks).
  • Obtain binding insurance quotes from the admitted market and check last-resort plan eligibility (California Department of Insurance).
  • Order a pre-transfer home inspection: roof, seismic condition, solar interconnection date, inverter replacement (CPUC net metering rules).
  • Check the Prop. 19 allowance at each biennial Board of Equalization adjustment, and watch Congress.gov for basis legislation before every transfer year.
  • File the county change-of-ownership report within 150 days and the Prop. 19 claims inside their windows (one year for the exemption; three years for the exclusion).
