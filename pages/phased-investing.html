Phased investing & reinvestment risk

A general scenario framework for comparing immediate investment with a 24–36 month Treasury staging period; no return is guaranteed.

Sequence-risk question

If sale proceeds are invested shortly before a major equity decline, an investor may experience a large early loss. A Treasury holding period changes exposure during that interval but creates reinvestment risk if yields fall before reinvestment.

Compare the pathways

Model immediate diversified investment; staged short-duration Treasury holdings followed by scheduled equity purchases over 24–36 months; and a blended allocation with a retained liquidity reserve.

Stress-test

Use multiple historical crash-entry dates and inflation regimes, including the Great Depression and global financial crisis, as historical illustrations rather than forecasts. Compare ending wealth, drawdown, purchasing power, income, and liquidity.

Required inputs

At decision time obtain the actual distributable amount, taxes and costs, current Treasury yields, tax status, portfolio allocation, risk tolerance, liquidity needs, and adviser-approved investment policy.

Planning framework only; not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, or investment advice. No household-specific inputs are published.
