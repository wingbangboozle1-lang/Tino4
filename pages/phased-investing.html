Treasury staging tool

Park the sale proceeds in 3-month to 2-year Treasury paper for 24 to 36 months, then phase into equities, and see what that does to 1929 and 2008 style entry risk.

A stress illustration, not a backtest

The calculator normalises the starting portfolio to 100 units and applies a clearly labelled synthetic path. It uses historical drawdown endpoints as the depth of the fall, not as a replay of history. It is not a forecast and not a personal investment recommendation.

    How to read the three cases

    The calculator reports three cases side by side. Staged entry parks the proceeds in Treasury paper for the staging window, then phases into equities over the phasing period. Immediate entry puts the whole amount into equities at once. Cash simply stays in Treasury paper for the whole horizon.

    The fall can be timed to start during the staging window, during the phasing period, or after the money is fully invested. Run all three. The case where the fall starts during the wait is the best case for staging; the case where it starts right after entry is the worst. The quiet-rally case, where nothing falls, is the one that shows the cost of the insurance.
