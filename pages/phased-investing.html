Treasury staging tool

The 24-36 month 3M-2Y Treasury parking plan: mechanics, the California state-tax advantage, honest pricing of its reinvestment risk, and a local-only synthetic-path simulator.

The requested path, formalized

Park sale proceeds in a 3-month to 2-year Treasury ladder for 24-36 months after the inheritance, then phase into equities. The stress table on the black-swan page prices this plan against real historical crash entries; this page explains the mechanics and the tradeoffs.

Decision rule of thumb

Choose staging length by regime awareness, not prediction: the real-data table shows the phased path ahead for 1929- and 2000-shaped entries and near parity for 2008, while the fast 2020 recovery punished parking by roughly a quarter of the decade's outcome. The interactive stress tool on the black-swan page recomputes both variants locally; nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

Ladder reinvestment risk — what maturing rungs cost you

A 24-36 month parking plan is a ladder: several short Treasury rungs mature at different times and each one is reinvested at whatever auction rate exists then. This panel isolates that effect. It runs the same crash and the same staging schedule twice — once with every renewal at the first-year yield, once with the later rungs repriced lower — and reports the reserve interest earned before any equity outcome is applied. Starting capital is normalized to 100 units; yields are entered net of estimated federal tax, state tax is not applicable to Treasury interest, and fees are not modeled.

Default yields are illustrative round settings, not a Treasury quote. Read the current auction results before relying on any yield.

What this tool is not

It is a stress illustration, not a historical backtest, not a forecast, and not investment advice. It exists so the family can feel how staging changes drawdowns under hypothetical regimes, then verify the conclusion against the real-data tables on the black-swan page.
