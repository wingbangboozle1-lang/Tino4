Rounding is a privacy control, not a precision claim

Amounts are rounded to the nearest ten dollars at the point of publication, after the arithmetic. That is enough to stop a published figure from being matched to a single online record by searching its exact value; it is not enough to make the set of figures unidentifiable to someone who already knows the household, which is why the site carries no locality, no postal code, no parcel identifier and no name, and why the repository's earlier history is treated as exposed.
