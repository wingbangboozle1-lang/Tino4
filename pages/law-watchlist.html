Law and market watchlist

A short list of rules and market inputs that can change the decision, without treating a proposed change or long-run projection as settled fact.

Watch enacted law, not headlines

The pages distinguish current statutes and agency tables from bills, stress assumptions and future uncertainty. No bill or proposal is represented as current law. The inherited transfer is far in the future; refresh federal, state and actual local rules near the event.

No future-year legal projection

This site intentionally does not extrapolate federal thresholds or California property-tax allowances to a future year. Pending bills are not incorporated as law. The cities are known at the summary level (Cupertino for Property A, San Francisco for Property B), but city and county code, local ordinances and their amendments must still be re-read from the official systems at the decision date.
