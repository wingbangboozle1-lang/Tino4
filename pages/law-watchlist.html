How to keep this page honest

Check the primary source, not the summary, and check it on a date. A rule that was current when this page was built can change. The two items most likely to move are the rent ceiling, whose current statute expires in 2030, and the family-home exclusion, which has already been rewritten once. Neither is a reason to delay the decision; both are reasons to avoid a plan that only works under today's statute.
