What is date-of-death fair market value and tax basis?No independent appraisal or estate basis schedule is available here.Obtain qualified written valuation as of date of death; reconcile improvements and depreciation with the CPA.MissingIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets
Does the heir qualify for Proposition 19?Relationship, transferor occupancy, transferee occupancy, value limit and claim status are unknown.Counsel/assessor confirms statutory eligibility and long-term occupancy intention in writing.MissingCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19 · California Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2 · California BOE — Letter to Assessors 2025/009
What are actual rental and occupancy rights?Leases, permits, unit status, deposits and local rules are unavailable.Review every lease, lawful-use record, inspection and local ordinance with counsel.MissingIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · California Civil Code §1947.12
What will costs be?Current bills, insurance terms, inspection and maintenance history are not in this public project.Collect multiple written insurance quotes, utility bills, contractor bids and a five-year capital plan.MissingCalifornia Department of Insurance — Residential insurance · California Contractors State License Board — Check a license
Could federal estate tax or liquidity be material?No inventory, prior taxable gifts, marital or portability details are available.CPA/estate counsel builds a confidential estate-level calculation and liquidity plan.MissingIRS — Estate tax · IRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs · IRS — About Form 706
What is meant by 'Prop H'?Ambiguous without jurisdiction, election year and official measure identifier; no terms are modeled.Identify the measure and obtain its official text, fiscal analysis and current status.Not verifiedCalifornia Secretary of State — Qualified ballot measures
What transfer tax and recording fees apply on sale?No amount is published, because the jurisdiction, price, exemptions and recorded documents are not public or verified. The published city-county schedule does establish that the tax is charged per 500 dollars of consideration and that the applicable tier can reach the entire consideration.Obtain the official transfer-tax and recorder fee schedules for each building's jurisdiction and a written closing estimate from an escrow officer, and ask the recorder whether a transfer at death pays the tax.MissingCalifornia statute — documentary transfer tax · California statute — recording fees · San Francisco County Recorder — fees to record a document
What has been depreciated, and what does that create at a sale?No depreciation schedule, land/building allocation or accumulated-depreciation figure is published. The recovery period, the mid-month convention, the exclusion of land, the allowed-or-allowable rule and the maximum federal rate on unrecaptured section 1250 gain are established rules.The CPA produces the depreciation history from returns, or reconstructs what was allowable, with the acquisition and improvement records, and calculates the repayment at an intended sale-price band and year.MissingIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · IRS Topic 409 — Capital gains and losses · FTB — Capital gains and losses
Will the heir occupy the inherited home long enough for the home-sale exclusion?The occupancy plan is not published, and the ownership-and-use tests are questions of fact. Whether an heir may count the decedent's ownership period is not resolved by the sources cited on this site.A dated occupancy plan, the intended sale year, any prior use of the exclusion, and a written CPA position on the period test before the exclusion is relied on in a model.MissingIRS Topic 701 — Sale of your home
Is an exchange intended, or is a taxable sale better?A like-kind exchange requires both sides to be held for business or investment, defers rather than eliminates tax, and generally carries the lower basis and the repayment forward into the replacement property.A written adviser note on held-for-use before the property is listed, plus the identification and receipt deadlines and a qualified intermediary's terms, if an exchange is pursued.MissingIRS Publication 544 — Sales and Other Dispositions of Assets · IRS — About Form 8824 (like-kind exchanges)
Can the estate meet what it may owe without selling?No estate inventory, cash position, debt schedule, insurance or retirement beneficiary designations or liquidity plan is published, and an estate-level filing test cannot be evaluated from a property value.A confidential inventory of accounts by type with balances, debts and liens, beneficiary designations, prior gift returns, and a written plan for the filing and payment dates — including whether a portability election requires a return below the threshold.MissingIRS — Estate tax · IRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs · IRS — About Form 706
What could the inherited home rent for, and at what cost?No rent comparable, management fee schedule, vacancy assumption or insurance quote is published for a specific property, and none is inferred from an area median or a program payment standard.Three written comparables from a licensed agent or manager, a written management fee schedule, a vacancy assumption from actual turnover, and two quotes each for owner-occupied and landlord coverage.MissingHUD — Fair Market Rents · IRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · California Department of Insurance — Residential insurance
Which property-tax relief survives a later rental or sale?The conditions on a family-home transfer are published; the consequences of the household's actual later use are property-specific determinations.The assessor's written determination and the claim file, kept with the trust records, plus a note on what a later conversion does to the assessment and to any gain.MissingCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19 · California Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2
How are California capital gains taxed, and when does the 1% surcharge apply?FTB states capital gains are taxed as ordinary income. Current statute and the 2025 Form 540-ES worksheet apply an additional 1% to taxable income above $1,000,000; that is not an inheritance tax. No household sale-year sensitivity is available.CPA calculates sale-year taxable income, federal and California tax, basis, exclusions and estimated-payment effects; verify the current Form 540 booklet, which was not retrieved for this build.Verified ruleCalifornia statute — additional tax on high taxable income · FTB — 2025 Form 540-ES instructions · FTB — Capital gains and losses · California Franchise Tax Board — Forms and publications
