Questions and missing evidence

Every question that needs an answer before a decision is reasonable, what is already known, and the concrete step that would produce the missing information.

How to use this register

Each question is stated with what is currently known and a concrete way to discover the missing information. Nothing is inferred to fill a gap. Where a value is unknown it stays unknown, and the model shows the break-even or the range instead of inventing a number.
