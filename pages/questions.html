Questions & missing evidence

A clear register of what is known at a high level, what is not established, and the document or official rule needed to resolve each question.

Answers are intentionally bounded

The only supplied case structure carried into the public site is that one property is expected through a trust and the other is already owned. No exact costs, valuations, locations, leases or estate amounts are published or inferred.

Discovery order

First collect trust and title records, then confirm estate valuation/basis and property-tax deadlines, then verify lawful use and leases, then request independent insurance/repair/utility data, and only then compare after-tax alternatives.

Planning framework only. Not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal or investment advice. A source supports only its stated scope, not a private fact. Blank means unknown, not zero. The public site intentionally contains no property-specific dollar values or locations.
