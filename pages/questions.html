Questions & missing evidence

Current law, owner figures, stated assumptions and open questions, kept apart on purpose.

Answer these in order, and do not decide before Q1 to Q6 are closed

The questions are ordered by what unlocks the most. The first six determine whether occupancy is even available, what the two bills really are, and what the property would rent for. Until those are answered on paper from an authorised source, any recommendation including the one on this site is conditional. The discovery suggestions exist so that an answer, when it arrives, is a document rather than an assumption.

Questions, who can answer them, and what each one unlocks

Status says what kind of evidence a row rests on: Verified means an official source states it, Owner means you supplied it and it is not independently confirmed, Projection means arithmetic on rounded inputs under a stated assumption, Assumption means an invented planning parameter, and Missing means the cell is blank because no evidence exists. A linked source supports the rule, not a private fact.
