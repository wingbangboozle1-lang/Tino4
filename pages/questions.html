What is meant by 'Prop H'?Identified 2026-11-17: the local annual parcel tax approved by voters on November 3, 2026 to fund transit operations in Property B's jurisdiction, July 1, 2027 through June 30, 2042, with inflation adjustments, a $129 single-family base rate for 2027, size-based multifamily rates ($249 and up), senior exemptions, and a $65-per-unit rent-controlled pass-through cap. What transfer tax and recording fees apply on sale?Property A's county: $0.55 per $500 or fraction (about $5,980 at base value) plus roughly $260 of recording fees; no city conveyance tax in that city. How are California capital gains taxed, and when does the 1% surcharge apply?FTB states capital gains are taxed as ordinary income. The 2025 Form 540 line 62 instructions apply an additional 1% to the portion of taxable income above $1,000,000; each $10,000 over the threshold adds $100 of this tax before other return effects. This is not an inheritance tax.CPA calculates the actual sale-year taxable income using the then-current return instructions.Verified ruleCalifornia statute — additional tax on high taxable income · FTB — 2025 Form 540 instructions · FTB — 2025 Form 540-ES instructions · FTB — Capital gains and losses · California Franchise Tax Board — Forms and publications
