3. Questions for the attorney

  1. Is the trust revocable now, and when does it become irrevocable? Trustee notice is due within 60 days of that event. Probate Code 16061.7
  2. Who files the death-transfer statement, the trustee or the transferee? Due within 150 days after death. RTC 480
  3. Can the heir meet the Proposition 19 tests: principal residence on both sides, an eligible relationship, and a claim within three years? BOE Proposition 19
  4. Will the estate need Form 706 (nine months after death, if required)? Form 706 instructions
  5. Confirm the state position: California imposes no estate or inheritance tax of its own. RTC 13301 RTC 13302

4. Questions for the CPA

  1. Basis: the inherited basis is generally fair market value on the date of death. IRS inherited basis
  2. Depreciation recapture on B and on any rented A: unrecaptured section 1250 gain is taxed at a maximum 25%. IRS capital gains IRS publication 527
  3. Net investment income tax on rents and gains, including the trust threshold. IRS NIIT
  4. Trust income tax filing (Form 541), if a filing test is met. FTB trusts
  5. Replace the model inputs: the 33.1% tax rate, the 6.0% equity return, the 4.0% home growth and the 2.8% gross rent yield.
  6. Solar export credits are usually lower than retail. CPUC solar tariffs

5. Deadlines (full timeline on its own page)

9 months after death: Form 706, if required. 150 days after death: death-transfer statement. 60 days after the trust event: trustee notice. Within 3 years: Proposition 19 claim. Each 1 November: county tax bill. Each 15 February: homeowners' exemption claim. Installment due dates and the Form 706 extension were not verified and are left blank. Appraisal of A at transfer; title reports; any debt or lien; the trust instrument; insurance quotes including earthquake; a rent roll and leases for B; the 2026 assessment notice for A; the legal status of the rental units in B. See Flagged for review.
