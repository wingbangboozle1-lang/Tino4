Methodology, equations and assumptions

How the tax formulas, cash-flow conservation and two wealth measures work, plus every parameter and limitation.

Privacy & verification controls. The two properties appear only as Property A (the trust-held residence to be inherited) and Property B (the already-owned 3-unit building). No name, street address, parcel or account number, city name, neighbourhood name or ZIP code is published. Every owner-supplied figure was rounded to the nearest $10 before modelling, so exact tax-roll values cannot be matched by search. Official cells that no government source supports are left blank and explained on the Flagged for review page.

Accounting rules the model follows

ComponentHow it is implementedOfficial basis
RoundingEvery owner-supplied figure is stored already rounded to the nearest $10, and every rendered dollar amount is re-rounded at output. Official statutory amounts are quoted exactly as published and linked.S21 S28
Property tax formulaTax = rate × max(0, assessed value − $7,000 exemption) + fixed parcel charges, with the fixed charges inflating at consumer prices. The formula reproduces the supplied 2025 bills and does not apply a fixed-charge ratio to a reassessed multi-million-dollar value.S21 S24 S28 S30
Assessment growthThe taxable base grows by the lower of 2% and modeled inflation, and is set to the lower of that base and market value so a decline is enrolled.S21 S25
Inheritance reassessmentOccupying under Prop. 19 re-bases the property to base + allowance + any excess over that sum; renting re-bases to full market value.S22 S23
Rental taxGross rent less 5% vacancy and 6% management, less the rental share of operating expenses, less depreciation on the building share, at the combined federal + NIIT + California rate, with losses suspended and released against later rental income.S11 S12
Cash-flow conservationEvery plan starts with the same assets, including an illustrative $1,000,000 cash reserve; each year's surplus or deficit compounds inside the household portfolio, so a plan that consumes cash is charged for it.S10 S18
Two wealth measuresPre-liquidation net worth counts assets at value. After-liquidation net worth deducts the costs and taxes that turning those assets into cash would actually incur, including embedded equity gains and property sale costs.S09 S10 S11 S17 S29

Reproducing the numbers

Everything on this site is generated from a single deterministic script. Running the engine regenerates data/summary.json; running the generator rebuilds every page from that data.
