Methods & limitations

A clear distinction between current law, missing evidence and review-only comparisons.

No unsupported numerical forecast

The public site publishes verified current-law money figures only. Household property values, costs, tax exposure, rent and future-law thresholds are blank. An official source link does not create a verified household amount, and a historical series does not forecast a specific property.
