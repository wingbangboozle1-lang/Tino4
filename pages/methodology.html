Methods & limitations

A clear distinction between current law, missing evidence and illustrative scenarios.

An honest boundary

No official agency publishes a verified 50-year valuation, exact household insurance premium or stock return for these properties. Household estimates are withheld, not silently promoted into facts. Synthetic scenarios illustrate mechanics and cannot satisfy a demand for officially verified future household numbers.
