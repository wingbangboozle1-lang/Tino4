Break-even questions

The handful of thresholds that actually decide between occupying, renting, selling and staging.

Ask for one number, not a fifty-year forecast

Most of this decision turns on a handful of quantities that can be obtained in writing. Until they arrive, a long projection is an elaborate restatement of the assumptions typed into it. This page separates the quantities that would settle the choice from the ones that only look precise.

How to use a break-even figure

Obtain the threshold first, then obtain the market evidence. If written comparables sit comfortably above the break-even rent, occupying survives the test; if they sit below it, the honest conclusion is that the area's rental value does not justify living in the home, and the comparison moves to selling versus leasing out. Either way the conclusion rests on an observed number rather than on a projection.

What a break-even figure cannot do

It cannot decide whether a family should live somewhere. It prices the housing, and the housing is only one part of the decision. Health, work, schools, distance from the household's support network and the wishes attached to the property are not modelled here and should not be dressed up as financial outputs.

How to use the hurdle

The hurdle is not a forecast of returns: it is the break-even earning power of the money, read off the model's own terminals so it cannot disagree with the ranking tables. A hurdle at or below zero means the hold path is behind even if the proceeds earn nothing, which is the strongest possible case for selling. If the household's own investment policy expects less than the hurdle after tax and costs, then the hold path wins on arithmetic and the sale argument has to rest on something else - liquidity, simplicity, tax exposure, or a view that the home will not be maintained. If the policy expects comfortably more, selling is the stronger case even before any appreciation assumption is discussed. Every row is computed from the same model terminals the path tables publish, so the two can be checked against each other.

Why the hurdle moves with the scenario, and what that means

Under the block-sequence scenarios the hurdle is usually modest, because the hold path's terminal is carried by house appreciation the same scenario grants to nothing else; under the deflation and bust-at-transfer scenarios it shifts again, because the property flow and the reserve leg change together. A hurdle that a plausible portfolio assumption cannot clear is the honest signal to stop arguing for a sale on return grounds - and it is a range across scenarios, not a single number to quote.
