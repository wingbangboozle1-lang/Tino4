Every requirement, one register

The owner's instruction list is long, and the honest answer to most of it is 'the method is published, the private record is not'. This register keeps both halves visible: what the site now does about each requirement, and the one next action that would close it. Rows marked Open are not oversights in the analysis — they are questions that cannot be answered from public sources for a private parcel.

Requirements register — treatment and next step

Statuses are evidence statuses, not completion grades: Handled means the method and its sources are published here, not that the household's question is answered. A row is closed only when the named private record exists.
