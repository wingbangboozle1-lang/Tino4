Property tax & history

A clear distinction between current law, missing evidence and illustrative scenarios.

Three values, three different purposes

Market value measures a sale valuation. Assessed value determines a property-tax base. Income-tax basis measures taxable sale gain. They are not interchangeable. The bill history is supplied by the household and has not been officially reconciled.
