Property tax on an inherited homeA qualifying claim may preserve the factored base value subject to the statutory value-limit calculation; failure to qualify can lead to reassessment.Obtain an assessor determination; do not treat a public model as eligibility advice.GuidanceCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19 · California Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2
No carry-forward of the value limitThe family-home value limit read in the statute on 2026-11-15 (as amended through 2025) applies per qualifying transfer: each transfer is limited to the then-current adjusted amount. The statute contains no carry-forward of an unused limit to a later transfer, and the biennial adjustment is published by the state board rather than projected here.Do not budget a transfer around an assumed stockpiled allowance; use the official amount in effect on the actual transfer date.Verified ruleCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2 · California BOE — Letter to Assessors 2025/009
Claim timing and corrective reliefRead in §63.2(f)-(g) on 2026-11-17: the claim is ordinarily due within three years of the transfer, or earlier if the property is transferred to a third party or the transferee stops occupying it; a claim can be treated as timely within six months of a supplemental or escape assessment notice, and a late claim on property still held is allowed with the exclusion applied from the lien date of the assessment year in which it is filed. The assessor may charge a late-claim processing fee capped at the lesser of actual reassessment cost or 175 dollars.Calendar every date immediately at transfer; counsel confirms the applicable period and any late-claim route in writing. A previous build cited a separate statute section that no longer renders code text and did not support these deadlines.Verified ruleCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2
Tax rate area (TRA)Property-tax rates are set by geographic tax rate areas published in the county's official rate compilation; the parcel's tax rate area line appears on the tax bill.Read the tax rate area from the actual bill and its published total rate from the county compilation; do not infer a rate from a generic statewide levy.GuidanceCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §51 · California BOE — County assessor contacts
