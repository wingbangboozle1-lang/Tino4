Proposition 19 family-home exclusionRequires the transferor's principal residence (or family farm), the child's principal residence within one year, a homeowners' or disabled-veterans' exemption filed within one year, and the statutory value limit. A rental or immediate-sale branch does not qualify.Claim only if eligible and the heir will occupy long term; verify consequences before a later rental or sale.Verified ruleCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19 · California Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2 · California BOE — Letter to Assessors 2025/009
Current indexed capThe official cap is $1,044,586 for qualifying transfers through February 15, 2027 (prior: $1,000,000 and $1,022,600); the next adjustment is due February 2027 and uses the FHFA California House Price Index.Use the BOE amount applicable on the transfer date, not a forward projection.Verified ruleCalifornia BOE — Letter to Assessors 2025/009
Property tax on an inherited homeIf the market value exceeds the parent's factored base year value plus the cap, the new taxable value is the factored base year value plus the excess over the limit (equivalently market value minus the cap). Failure to qualify leads to full reassessment to market value.Obtain an assessor determination; see the worked 2045 branches on the Projections page.Verified ruleCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19 · California Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2
No carry-forward of the value limitThe family-home value limit read in the statute on 2026-11-15 (as amended through 2025) applies per qualifying transfer: each transfer is limited to the then-current adjusted amount. The statute contains no carry-forward of an unused limit to a later transfer, and the biennial adjustment is published by the state board rather than projected here. The projections carry the indexed amount forward in time only as a labeled assumption.Do not budget a transfer around an assumed stockpiled allowance; use the official amount in effect on the actual transfer date.Verified ruleCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2 · California BOE — Letter to Assessors 2025/009
Claim timing and corrective reliefThe claim is filed within three years of the transfer, or before the property is transferred to a third party or the child stops occupying it, whichever comes first; the statute caps the late-claim processing fee and sets the applicable period.Calendar the claim immediately after transfer; counsel confirms the applicable period and any late-claim route in writing.Verified ruleCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2 · California BOE — Proposition 19 overview
Tax rate area (TRA)Property-tax rates are set by geographic tax rate areas published in the county's official rate compilation (FY2025-26: 816 TRAs; total rate = 1% maximum levy plus voter-approved debt levies). Lookup performed 2026-11-17 in the official compilation; the property's own TRA line is read from its tax bill and recorded in the private workpaper. Published general levies for comparable suburban combinations run about 1.13-1.24%; the household-reported ratios (A about 1.335%, B about 2.135%) exceed those and need bill-level reconciliation (flagged).Read the tax rate area from the actual bill and its published total rate from the county compilation; list direct charges separately.GuidanceCounty Controller - Property tax rate book (official compilation) · California Revenue and Taxation Code §51 · California BOE — County assessor contacts
