Why the effective rate looks higher than 1%

RuleVerified requirementEffect on these two propertiesOfficial basis
The 1% levy is not the whole billProp. 13 caps the general levy at 1% of assessed value plus voter-approved bonded debt; bills also carry per-parcel taxes and direct assessments.Property A's bill is about 1.34% of assessed value and Property B's about 2.14%: on a very small Prop. 13 base, the fixed parcel charges dominate the percentage levy.S21 S28
Fixed charges do not grow with valueDirect assessments and parcel taxes are levied per parcel or per benefit unit, not as a share of value.The model therefore separates rate × assessed value from a fixed-charge line that inflates with consumer prices.S21 S30
Annual growth is cappedFactored base-year value may rise by the lower of 2% and the California consumer price index each year.A 0% inflation year means a 0% assessment increase — the cap is a ceiling, not a schedule.S21
Declines are enrolled tooWhere January 1 market value falls below the factored base, the assessor temporarily enrolls market value and restores it as prices recover.This softens the tax on a holding plan during a crash, which the stress regimes model.S25

What a 2040–2050 inheritance does to Property A's tax base

RuleVerified requirementConsequence for each planOfficial basis
Transfer date is the death dateWhen a revocable trust becomes irrevocable at death, the change in ownership is dated to the death, not to the later deed — and a prorated supplemental bill follows.Starts the 150-day reporting clock and the 1-year occupancy clock on the same day.S26 S28
Parent-child relief is narrowThe exclusion applies only to the settlor's principal residence, only if the child occupies it as a principal residence within one year and claims the $7,000 exemption; value above the base plus $1,044,586 is added to the roll.Occupy it and the tax still rises to $53,580/yr in a 2045 transfer.S22 S23 S24
No occupancy, no reliefA child who keeps living elsewhere gets no parent-child exclusion — the property is reassessed to full market value.Renting it out costs $70,180/yr in the first year of a 2045 transfer.S21 S22
One exemption per householdThe $7,000 exemption may be claimed on only one principal residence per household.Claiming it on Property A means giving it up on Property B — a real but small cost of moving.S24
The allowance is re-set every two yearsThe parent-child allowance is adjusted every second February 15 by the prior year's California house-price change; the published figure covers 16 Feb 2025 – 15 Feb 2027.The 2040–2050 allowance is projected, not published, and is labeled as such everywhere it appears.S22 S23
