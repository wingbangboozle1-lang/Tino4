Property tax & history

Rounded case figures, labeled assumptions and verified rules — separated clearly so nothing reads as a promise.

Three values, three purposes

Market value is what a sale prices. Assessed value sets the property-tax base (Prop. 13: 1% base levy plus voter-approved debt service and direct charges). Income-tax basis (generally death-date FMV) sets taxable gain. They are not interchangeable. Owner-supplied tables below are rounded to $10 before use; official links verify the legal framework and the inflation-factor reconciliation, not the household figures.
