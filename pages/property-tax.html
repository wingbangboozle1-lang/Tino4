Worked example of the exclusion arithmetic, on rounded figures

A home whose capped taxable value is $550,540 and whose value at transfer is modelled at $5,435,870 cannot carry its whole base forward: the cap is the prior taxable value plus the indexed allowance, and everything above it is added back. The saving is real, and it is smaller than most summaries imply.
