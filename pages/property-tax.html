The rate is not verified

The model derives Property A's rate of 1.353% from the owner's own last bill, and Property B's rate of 2.136% the same way. Neither is an official county rate. Property B's implied rate is far above the 1 percent base plus voter-approved debt described by the city, which is flagged for review.
