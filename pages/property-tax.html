Property-tax rules

California property-tax mechanics that matter for a future trust transfer, with no address, assessor record or property-specific tax history disclosed.

Critical distinction

Income-tax basis and property-tax assessed value are different systems. A date-of-death basis analysis does not by itself determine the assessed value or a Proposition 19 exclusion.

California property-tax rules and missing parcel facts

The California BOE warns that statutes and rules change; verify the law and assessor's calculation at the actual transfer.

A question closed today: the transfer tax at death

Earlier sessions could not confirm whether the county charges documentary transfer tax when a house passes by death or out of a trust, because only commercial title and tax websites asserted the exemption. The code has now been read directly: §11930 states that the tax does not apply where realty is transferred outright to, or in trust for the benefit of, any person or entity by reason of an inter vivos gift or by reason of a death, and §11932 requires the tax to be shown on the face of any recorded document that is subject to it. The exemption covers the inheritance step only. A sale to a buyer afterwards is a separate, taxable instrument, which is why the sell-path costing on this site keeps the transfer tax on the sale line and removes it from the transfer line.

San Francisco Proposition H — an enacted parcel tax on Property B

Proposition H was treated in earlier sessions as a possible rent measure and left as pending research. It is neither: it passed on November 3, 2026 with preliminary returns of 244,760 yes and 84,209 no, and it is an owner-paid parcel tax running through fiscal 2041-42. Amounts follow the SFMTA's published rate schedule for fiscal 2027-28, indexed for inflation thereafter; the uncertified election result is flagged.

Private records to request

For each asset separately, obtain the assessor roll, full secured tax bill, supplemental assessment history, deed and trust transfer documents, homeowners' exemption status, parcel-specific direct levies, and any filed Proposition 19 claims.
