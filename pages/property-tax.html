Prop 19 is conditional

The exclusion only applies if the home was the transferor's principal residence, the transferee moves in within one year, and the homeowners' or disabled veterans' exemption is filed within one year. Renting the property does not qualify, and only one homeowners' exemption may be claimed by a couple. Because the allowance is roughly $1,044,590 while the property is worth several million, a large partial reassessment remains even when the exclusion applies.
