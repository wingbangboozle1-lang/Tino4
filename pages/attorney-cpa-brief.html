Leave with a written conditional recommendation that states each assumption, the source, the deadline, the tax and insurance reserve, and the specific fact that would reverse the answer. Bring the private documents; do not upload them to the public site.

Print settings for a true one-pager

This page is built for one sheet: the print stylesheet hides navigation, the sidebar, buttons and the footer beyond a single attribution line, and reduces table type to fit. Print or save as PDF in portrait with default margins. If the table spills to a second page, print the first page only for the meeting and keep the source register for the file.

Decision boundary

Property A is the anticipated trust transfer and Property B is already owned. Keep B's rent ledger, leases, assessment, basis and insurance separate from A, and test whether B can serve as a residence without assuming tenant obligations disappear. Compare live, rent, sell, mixed-occupancy and phased-investing alternatives only after counsel and the CPA confirm the legal and tax inputs.

Privacy

This printable sheet contains no address, locality, valuation, rent, utility amount, insurance premium, parcel identifier or household name. Completed adviser notes belong in an access-controlled file outside this repository.

Planning framework only. Not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal or investment advice. A source supports only its stated scope, not a private fact. Owner-supplied figures are privacy-rounded to the nearest $10 at the owner's direction and flagged; blanks mean unknown, not zero.
