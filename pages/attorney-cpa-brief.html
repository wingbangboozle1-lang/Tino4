How to use this brief

Print one copy for the attorney and one for the tax adviser. Ask each to confirm or correct the figures above against the sources on the date of the meeting, and to return written answers to the checklist. Record the answers, the date and the adviser's name in the private decision log. Do not attach household documents to this public page or upload them to the repository.

Research framework only; not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, investment, or property-management advice.

Recheck official authorities, source availability and law at the actual decision date.
