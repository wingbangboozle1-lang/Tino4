Attorney & CPA briefing sheet

Printable checklist for adviser meetings. No personal or property-specific data is included.

Trust and title

Obtain the executed trust and amendments, deed, trustee authority, beneficiary terms, debt and liens, and distribution or sale requirements.

Tax

Determine date-of-death valuation and basis records; federal estate-tax filing and payment deadlines; California property-tax change-in-ownership reporting; and eligibility and deadlines for any applicable parent-child property-tax exclusion.

Operations

Confirm legal unit status, leases, deposits, rent-control coverage, insurance availability, rebuilding limits, utilities, maintenance history, and solar/export-credit agreements.

Decision model

Request side-by-side after-tax cash flows for occupy, rent, sell, mixed use, and phased investing, including fees, vacancy, capital repairs, inflation, and adverse market entry scenarios.

Planning framework only; not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, or investment advice. No household-specific inputs are published.

Primary-source starting points: California Board of Equalization Proposition 19 · IRS estate tax · IRS Form 706 · Treasury bills
