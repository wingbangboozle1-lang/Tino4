Decision boundary

Property A is the anticipated trust transfer; Property B is already owned. Keep B's private rent ledger (if any), lease, assessment, basis and insurance separate from A, and test whether B can serve as a residence without assuming tenant obligations disappear. Compare live, rent, sell, mixed occupancy and phased-investing alternatives only after counsel and the CPA confirm the legal and tax inputs.
