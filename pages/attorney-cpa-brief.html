Decision boundary

Property A is the trust transfer (2040–2050 window); Property B is already owned and currently produces ≈$7,150/mo from two units. Leading paths: occupy A within the one-year window + rent all three B units (≈$47,430–$86,470/yr estimated net, 2026 money) versus sell A and stage ≈$2,945,000 into diversified assets while keeping B (≈$54,720–$67,540/yr from B plus portfolio). Renting A without occupancy is projected weakest.
