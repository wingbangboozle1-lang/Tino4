Meeting objective

Leave the meeting with a written conditional recommendation, the assumptions, the deadlines, the tax and insurance reserves, and the facts that would reverse the answer. Working position on file: keep Property B, sell Property A soon after the date of death and stage the proceeds ($2,883,000–$2,945,000 at a 5%–7% cost assumption) through 24–36 months of Treasuries into equities — unless the household chooses to occupy A and claim the family-home exclusion ($21,580–$27,540 versus $32,550–$41,540 per year in today's dollars).

Decision boundary

Property A is the anticipated trust transfer; Property B is already owned. Keep B's rent ledger, leases, assessment, basis and insurance separate from A, and test whether B can serve as a residence without assuming tenant obligations disappear. The ranking on file (sell-and-stage A, or occupy A and claim the exclusion) is conditional on the trust documents, the estate inventory, written quotes and reconciled tax bills — counsel and the CPA confirm those inputs before anything is signed.
