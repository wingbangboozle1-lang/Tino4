This page is the honest inventory of what is weak in this brief

Nothing below blocks the recommendation, because the recommendation does not depend on the items that are missing. It does depend on the trust allowing occupancy, on insurance being available at a sane price and on the family-home exclusion surviving. If any of those three fails, read the executive summary again: the fallback is a sale, not a rental.
