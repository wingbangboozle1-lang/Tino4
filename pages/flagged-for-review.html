History-remediation limitation

GitHub's guidance describes history rewriting as a coordinated, disruptive operation and warns that other refs, pull requests, forks, cached views and clones may retain copies. This platform pins the session to one feature branch and prohibits pushing another branch; therefore this work does not force-push or claim to scrub main. The repository owner/admin should review visibility and coordinate a supported, all-ref cleanup if desired.
