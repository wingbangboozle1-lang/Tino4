Formula correction: staged-investment reserve interestThe first version of the staged-versus-lump model deployed a fixed dollar slice each month, which left interest earned during the staging window permanently undeployed. The staged path therefore kept a cash allocation the household never chose, and the two paths were no longer comparable at the same asset mix. Deployment is now an equal fraction of the remaining reserve, which drains it exactly when the window closes.Fixed 2026-11-17 and pinned by a regression identity. The corrected results are less favourable to staging than the first version, which is why the bug is recorded rather than quietly fixed.Verified ruleTreasuryDirect — Treasury bills
