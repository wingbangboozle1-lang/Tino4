Model assumptions

CPI / cost inflation 3.0% (range 2.5%–4.0%) Drives charges, rent growth, housing rent and deflation to today's dollars.

These are assumptions, not sourced facts. They are listed here so every projected dollar can be traced and challenged.

Method

  • One model, no hand-typed results. tools/model.py holds every input and emits data/projections.json; tools/build.py renders the tables from that file. Change an assumption and every table changes consistently.
  • Rounding. All dollar outputs are rounded to the nearest $10, as requested. Percentages are shown to one or two decimals and are computed from unrounded values.
  • Nominal vs today's dollars. Every 2065 figure is shown in future dollars, with a deflated figure where the comparison would otherwise be misleading.
  • No inference. Where a value could not be sourced — insurance premiums, Cupertino water tariffs, the split of the bill between ad valorem tax and direct charges — the cell is left as reported or blank and flagged, never filled with a plausible number.
  • Privacy. No address, APN, owner, trustee or beneficiary identifier is stored. Parcel-level history is presented as an index (year, assessed value, tax) with no location beyond ZIP code.
Internal decision analysis. No names, addresses, parcel numbers or trust identifiers appear on this site.
