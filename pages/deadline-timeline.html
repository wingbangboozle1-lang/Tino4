Deadline timeline

Statutory clocks that start at a death, shown in order. Each step links to the official text. Only the rule is stated here; the dates depend on the actual date of death and on the trust terms.

Day 0 · Date of death

For property-tax purposes the change in ownership is dated to the date of death, even if the trust distributes the property years later. Reassessment to market value follows unless an exclusion applies.

BOE Information Sheet 800-9, Death of a Real Property Owner

Within 60 days · Trustee notice

When a revocable trust becomes irrevocable at a death, the trustee must serve a notification on beneficiaries and heirs, generally within 60 days (Probate Code section 16061.7(f)).

California Probate Code section 16061.7

Within 150 days · Change in ownership statement

A change in ownership statement (BOE-502-D) must be filed with the county assessor within 150 days after the date of death, by the trustee if the property was held in trust. If the estate is probated, it is due with the inventory and appraisal.

California Revenue and Taxation Code section 480

Within 9 months · Federal estate return and tax, if required

Form 706 is due nine months after the date of death, and estate tax is due within the same period. An automatic six-month extension to file is available on Form 4768, but it does not extend the time to pay. Whether a return is required depends on the estate; the IRS states the 2026 filing threshold as more than $15 million and the threshold for a later death year is not yet known.

IRS Instructions for Form 706 (Rev. July 2026)

Within 1 year · Principal-residence condition

For a parent-to-child exclusion of a principal residence, the child must make it their principal residence within one year of the change in ownership. The exclusion is claimed on form BOE-19-P with the assessor.

BOE Information Sheet 800-9, intergenerational exclusion

60 days after a notice · Appeal window

A notice of reassessment must be appealed within 60 days of the notice to dispute the reassessed value. Keep the assessor's mailing address current so the notice arrives.

BOE Information Sheet 800-9, mailing address and appeal

Each year · Property-tax installments

Property taxes are due in two installments. A first installment not paid by the December 10 delinquency date, or a second not paid by April 10, carries a 10 percent penalty (R&TC sections 2617 and 2618).

BOE Information Sheet 800-9, property tax payments
