How to read these two tables

The two assets are deliberately separated because they have different owners, different bases and different clocks. Property A's tax line is discontinuous: it is low and rising slowly until the transfer, then it jumps once. Property B's line is continuous. A combined table would hide exactly the discontinuity that drives the decision.

The assessment column for Property A is the factored base year value growing at the annual cap. It is not the market value, and the gap between the two columns is the reason the transfer matters so much.
