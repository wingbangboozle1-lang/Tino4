A projection is a sentence about its assumptions

Before reading any year-by-year figure, read the assumption list that produced it. If a projection shows a single number for 2076, the honest reading is that it shows one path through a set of guesses. Two runs of the same model with different, equally plausible growth assumptions can reverse the ranking, and the useful output is the range and the assumption that flips it, not the centre line.

Avoid false precision

Publish ranges or dated official figures only. For every modeled amount, preserve the input source, observation date, formula, tax treatment, scenario range, and whether it is measured, assumed, or missing. Rebuild after any input change and record whether the ranking moved.

Distribution of sale proceeds

Model the payout in the fixed order published on the Compare paths page: gross value, sale transaction costs, documentary transfer tax, recording fees, sale-year income tax, then net to invest. Distribution chart — how the range widens with horizon

A single line implies a precision nobody has. The chart below is illustrative geometry in index units: it starts at 100 and applies a 3.5 percent real drift with 15 percent annual volatility, both chosen as labelled assumptions, to show how a plausible band widens as the horizon lengthens. It is not a forecast, not a probability, and not a projection of any property or portfolio.

Reproduce this chart in the private workpaper with the household's own inputs, and print the input values beside it. Where an input is unknown, widen the band or leave the line out; do not narrow it to make a ranking look stable.

Rebuild after every input change

Any change to value, rent, cost, tax, occupancy or market assumptions requires a full rebuild of the model and the site, followed by a written check of whether the recommendation moved. Record the date of the change, the source that caused it, the previous ranking and the new ranking. If a ranking flips on an assumption that cannot be evidenced, the honest output is a deferred decision, not a new number.
