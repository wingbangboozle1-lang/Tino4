Year-by-year worksheet

Build a private table with one row per year and columns for market value, assessed value, property tax, rent or imputed rent, operating costs, capital work, debt, tax, liquidity and after-tax terminal wealth. Run every pure and mixed path at identical transfer dates and horizons. Publish no household values here.

Distribution chart

Use the local-only regime simulator on the Black-swan conditions page to compare the range of outcomes under documented assumptions. Do not treat a schematic or an illustrative distribution as a household forecast.

Rebuild after input changes

For the private workpaper, rerun the full model after any change to a valuation, lease, tax rule, cost, occupancy plan or assumption. Record the date, evidence and whether the path ordering changed. If an unsupported assumption flips the result, leave the decision open rather than choosing the favorable point estimate.

Research framework only; not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, investment, or property-management advice.

Recheck official authorities, source availability and law at the actual decision date.
