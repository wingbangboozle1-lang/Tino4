Horizon

The owner-planned transfer window is 2040–2050 — a planning assumption, not a confirmed date or a legal forecast. CBO's cited rate outlook runs only through 2036; it cannot responsibly be extended as a fifty-year house-price, inflation, insurance or stock-return forecast. The scenario rows above are arithmetic bands from stated assumptions; use them only alongside private source documents and adviser-approved inputs.
