No dollar forecast is published

The requested planning window is not a property valuation, entitlement or future-law forecast. No independently verified starting values, annual bills, insurance quotes, rent or approved economic assumptions are available. To honor the no-invented-values rule, monetary projection cells below remain blank; this is an empty annual template, not a model output.
