Avoid false precision

Publish ranges or dated official figures only. For every modeled amount, preserve the input source, observation date, formula, tax treatment, scenario range, and whether it is measured, assumed, or missing. Rebuild after any input change and record whether the ranking moved.

Distribution of sale proceeds

Model the payout in the fixed order published on the Compare paths page: gross value, sale transaction costs, documentary transfer tax, recording fees, sale-year income tax, then net to invest. A distribution chart belongs in the private model built on that order; this public page publishes the framework and the labels, not household amounts.

Research framework only; not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, investment, or property-management advice.
