Projection worksheet

A transparent projection framework with intentionally blank private fields. It is not a substitute for a private, source-backed spreadsheet prepared from the actual estate and property records.

Rounded figures, labeled assumptions

At the owner's direction the worksheet now publishes privacy-rounded figures (nearest ten dollars) and computed projections. Owner-supplied inputs are badged Owner-supplied (privacy-rounded); every forward number is badged Projection or Assumption and driven by the assumption table below. Unknown inputs — rent for Property A, insurance quotes, maintenance bids — stay blank; blank means unknown, not zero. No name, street address or parcel number is published.

Spread at a glance

Horizon and limits

The owner's stated transfer window is 2040-2050; the model uses the 2045 midpoint. CBO's cited outlooks run through 2036 (rates) and 2055 (inflation) and cannot be extended as house-price, insurance, rent or stock-return forecasts. The projection table is a decision aid: it shows which branch of the property-tax rule matters, not what any year will actually bring. Re-check every input with professionals at the actual transfer date.

Planning framework only. Not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal or investment advice. A source supports only its stated scope, not a private fact. Owner-supplied figures are privacy-rounded to the nearest $10 at the owner's direction and flagged; blanks mean unknown, not zero.
