Executive summary

The high-impact unknowns and a safe order of operations.

Executive takeaway

The answer is not currently knowable from public information. Separate the estate-tax, income-tax basis, and property-tax assessment questions; obtain independent evidence; and agree in writing on the decision criteria before calculating outcomes.

Recommendation standard

Do not call a projection the best plan until it includes the same tax year and horizon for all alternatives; sale taxes and fees; debt; occupancy; rental legality; vacancy; capital costs; utilities; insurance; liquidity; and reinvestment risk.

Research framework only; not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, investment, or property-management advice.

Recheck official authorities, source availability and law at the actual decision date.
