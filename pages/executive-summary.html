Executive summary

The high-impact numbers, the conditional leading plan with its reasons, and what would change it.

Best plan at current inputs, and why (conditional; dated 2026-11-17)

On the rounded household inputs and base assumption bands, the wealth-maximizing path is: model a sale of Property A in the inheritance tax year first, and if no signed long-term occupancy need appears, sell and stage the after-tax proceeds over 24–36 months in 3-month to 2-year Treasuries before phasing into stocks. Why: a transfer-year sale captures date-of-death basis with near-zero modeled gain before exceptions; renting Property A loses to selling because the property-tax base resets to market (about $72,570 per year at 2045 base values) against roughly $50,630 of net rent before income tax; and living in Property A costs about $-65,400 per year net of imputed rent even with the family-home exclusion claimed. Living at Property B remains the low-cost housing baseline. Staging buys crash-entry protection at the price of reinvestment risk and a rising-market lag (see Black-swan conditions). This is a model output on labeled assumptions, not advice.

What would change the recommendation

A signed decision to occupy Property A long-term (family-home claim); written market-rent comparables that clear the carrying cost; a repeal or restriction of the step-up at death; a change in the federal exclusion; estate-liquidity needs that force an earlier sale; or verified trust terms that change the timing or the parties. Rebuild after any input change and note whether the ranking moved.

Recommendation standard

Do not call a projection the best plan until it includes the same tax year and horizon for all alternatives; the full sale-cost waterfall (transaction costs, documentary transfer tax, recording fees, sale-year income tax including unrecaptured section 1250 gain and the California 1% add-on above $1,000,000 of taxable income); any like-kind-exchange decision; debt; occupancy; rental legality; vacancy; capital costs; utilities; insurance; estate liquidity; and reinvestment risk.
