Executive summary

A decision framework, not a claim that one strategy is guaranteed to maximize profit.

Privacy and verification first. This public site does not publish case-specific financial figures or dollar projections. Values that cannot be verified from official sources are blank; a source on a model row explains its context, not verification of the model inputs or prediction.

What matters most

The first property is expected to pass through a trust; the other is already owned and may provide a place to live without giving up existing rental income. That makes living in the inherited home an opportunity-cost decision, not a need to acquire housing. It does not establish that staying is cost-free or that the existing rent is guaranteed.

Provisional direction: compare a prompt sale and diversified investment against renting and occupying; do not treat any as the best plan until the trust, tax basis, assessments, operating costs and household objectives have been verified privately. A prior version asserted that selling was best on the basis of unverified assumptions and an incomplete tax model; that conclusion has been withdrawn.
