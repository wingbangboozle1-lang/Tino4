DECISION

Executive summary

Rounded case figures, labeled assumptions and verified rules — separated clearly so nothing reads as a promise.

Executive conclusion — sell Property A after the transfer and reinvest; keep living at Property B

On rounded owner figures, selling A at the stepped-up basis converts a high-cost, low-yield second home into a diversified portfolio with, on the baseline assumptions, roughly twice the 20-year wealth of renting A and far less work. Renting A carries a full reassessment (about $41,500/yr tax at today’s value), landlord duties and a thin cash margin. Living in A is a lifestyle choice that costs about $67,700/yr more than the current arrangement (2045 dollars) even with the Prop. 19 family-home limit. This ranking is conditional: trust terms, the whole-estate tax picture, debts and actual quotes must be checked first (Questions).
