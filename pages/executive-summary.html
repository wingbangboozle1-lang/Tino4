Executive summary

A clear distinction between current law, missing evidence and illustrative scenarios.

Executive conclusion — investigate selling first, do not pre-commit

If existing housing remains suitable, selling A can avoid the additional landlord exposure and reassessed carrying costs of a second property. But expected rent, net sale proceeds, estate liquidity and risk tolerance are unknown. There is not enough verified evidence to name a profit-maximizing household strategy.
