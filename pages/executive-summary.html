DECISION

Executive summary

Current law, owner figures, stated assumptions and open questions, kept apart on purpose.

Recommendation in one sentence

Plan to occupy Property A and keep Property B as it is, and hold a signed sell-and-reinvest plan as the fallback that you execute if occupancy turns out to be impossible, because on these rounded inputs renting A produces $10,977,570 over twenty years against $14,568,100 for selling and reinvesting, and only occupancy captures the tax base that produces the difference.

What this brief does not do

It does not appraise either home, quote an insurance policy, value the trust, read a deed, or compute a tax return. It does not tell you to sell, move, rent, raise rent or invest. Every dollar figure is rounded to ten dollars and every projection is arithmetic on stated assumptions. The numbers are clear so the reasoning can be checked, not because they are certain.
