How suggestions are reviewed

  1. A suggestion is added here with status Proposed.
  2. Someone with authority reviews the official basis and records Approved or Declined, with the date.
  3. Approved changes are implemented through the generator (tools/build.py) and re-verified (tools/verify.py) before publication.
  4. Declined suggestions stay on this page with the reason, so the same proposal is not re-litigated without new evidence.
  5. Nothing in this section is tax, legal, investment or appraisal advice.
