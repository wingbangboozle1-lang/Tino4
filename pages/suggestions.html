What adopting one of these is not

Adopting a presentation change is not a decision about the estate. A clearer site makes the missing facts obvious; it cannot supply them. No suggestion on this page should be read as an instruction to sell, rent, occupy or retain any property.

Two numbering schemes on one page, on purpose

The review board above numbers site and model improvements S1 to S10. The registry below numbers household actions S-01 upward. They are kept separate because they answer different questions: one is about improving this site, the other is about evidence the household must obtain. Cite the hyphenated form in the decision log so the two never collide.

What happens to a suggestion after review

Each item is adopted, deferred or rejected on the decision log with a date and a reason, and nothing on this page is implemented by the site itself. The queue is a review instrument, not a to-do list the build silently completes.
