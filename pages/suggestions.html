S01PrivacyKeep private records in an access-controlled vaultStore executed trust/deed, tax returns, leases, bills, quotes and inspections outside the public repository; publish only generic conclusions.History removal and access control must be coordinated with the repository owner/admin.GuidanceGitHub Docs — Removing sensitive data from a repository

S03TaxSeparate estate tax, income-tax basis and property-tax assessmentHave the CPA prepare one schedule for the gross estate and adjusted gifts, one for inherited basis, and one for county assessment/Prop 19.These are distinct legal calculations, not interchangeable values.GuidanceIRS — Estate tax · IRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ · California BOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax · California BOE — Proposition 19
S04PropertyVerify legal unit count and local rental rulesReconcile permits, leases, rent registration, tenant deposits, occupancy, and statewide/local rent limits before estimating net rent.Gross rent from an unverified unit is not a lawful or reliable return.GuidanceCalifornia Civil Code §1947.12 · HUD — Fair Market Rents · IRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
S05OperationsGet separate current insurance and repair evidenceRequest written owner-occupied and landlord/mixed-use quotes, replacement-cost estimates, exclusions, inspections and itemized bids.Insurance availability and deferred repairs can reverse an apparent cash-flow winner.GuidanceCalifornia Department of Insurance — Residential insurance · California Contractors State License Board — Check a license
S06UtilitiesReconcile bills and solar tariff before assuming a creditCollect actual water/electric bills and the signed solar interconnection and tariff documents; note NEM/NBT and true-up dates.Equipment alone does not establish future net export compensation.GuidanceCalifornia State Water Resources Control Board — Drinking Water Program · California Public Utilities Commission — Net metering and net billing
S07ModelCompare mixed paths alongside pure choicesInclude occupancy of lawful units, existing-property retention, rent on other lawful units, short hold-then-sale, sale of one asset, and staged reinvestment.Avoids forcing a false live-versus-rent-versus-sell choice.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · California Civil Code §1947.12 · California BOE — Proposition 19
S08MarketsPut reinvestment risk next to any staging headlineCompare 24–36 months of Treasury staging under crash-during and crash-after entry, lower renewal yields, inflation and a rising market before the decline.Staging changes risk timing; it does not make equities safe or guarantee a better ending value.GuidanceTreasuryDirect — Treasury bills · TreasuryDirect — Treasury notes · Federal Reserve History — Stock Market Crash of 1929 · Federal Reserve History — The Great Recession · U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — CPI, June 2022
S09GovernancePut reversal conditions on the first screenTrack the facts that change the path: trust restrictions, estate threshold, Prop 19 eligibility, unit legality, net rent, insurance, repair reserve and liquidity.A recommendation should name evidence that would reverse it.GuidanceIRS — Estate tax · California BOE — Proposition 19 · California Department of Insurance — Residential insurance · SEC Investor.gov — Stocks FAQs
S11Sell pathTake a priced transfer-year sale to the CPA before listingRun the same-tax-year sale case on the sale-timing page with the actual valuation date, then ask the two questions it depends on: is any gain left to defer under section 1031, and what would depreciation and recapture cost if the house is rented first.The stepped-up basis makes the transfer-year answer close to settled and every later year quantifiably more expensive; deciding after the property is listed turns a tax question into a closing-table negotiation.GuidanceIRS — Gifts and inheritances FAQ · IRS — Like-kind exchanges: real estate tax tips · IRS Tax Topic 409 — Capital gain and loss · California FTB — Capital gains and losses
S12Property taxReconcile Property A's bill against the published levy before trusting any tax lineRead the rate area and each bill line, compare them with the published levy structure, and settle the residual difference with the assessor's office rather than with an assumption.The modeled rate is derived from the owner's rounded figures and sits above the published levy example; the difference between those two numbers moves the ten-year property-tax result more than any investment assumption on this site.GuidanceSanta Clara County — Compilation of Tax Rates FY 2025-26 · Santa Clara County Assessor — 2026 Assessment Information booklet · Owner-supplied records — Property A (privacy-rounded to nearest $10)
S13OccupancyDecide the occupancy question in writing, before it is a deadlineState whether the household will actually live in the inherited house as a principal residence, for how long, and what the plan is if that changes; then have counsel confirm the claim, the exemption filing and the removal rule.Proposition 19's family-home exclusion requires occupancy within one year with no exceptions, and losing occupancy pulls the exclusion back retroactively to a factored full-cash-value base — an intention that is never written down is usually the intention that fails.GuidanceCalifornia BOE — Proposition 19 · California BOE — Letter to Assessors 2026/026 (Proposition 19) · California Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2
S14San FranciscoCalendar the Property B compliance items that now existAdd the Proposition H application and renewal dates, the parcel's area and classification check, the Rent Board registration for each unit, and the assessor's informal-review and appeal windows to the annual calendar.These are enacted, dated obligations with hard deadlines; a missed seniors application, a missed appeal window or an unregistered unit each costs money that no projection on this site can recover.GuidanceSFMTA — Muni Proposition H parcel tax · San Francisco Assessor-Recorder — Department page · San Francisco Rent Board — Department page
