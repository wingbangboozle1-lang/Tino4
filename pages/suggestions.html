S04PropertyVerify legal unit count and local rental rulesReconcile permits, leases, rent registration, tenant deposits, occupancy, and statewide/local rent limits before estimating net rent.Gross rent from an unverified unit is not a lawful or reliable return.GuidanceCalifornia Civil Code §1947.12 · HUD — Fair Market Rents · IRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property
S05OperationsGet separate current insurance and repair evidenceRequest written owner-occupied and landlord/mixed-use quotes, replacement-cost estimates, exclusions, inspections and itemized bids.Insurance availability and deferred repairs can reverse an apparent cash-flow winner.GuidanceCalifornia Department of Insurance — Residential insurance · California Contractors State License Board — Check a license
S06UtilitiesReconcile bills and solar tariff before assuming a creditCollect actual water/electric bills and the signed solar interconnection and tariff documents; note NEM/NBT and true-up dates.Equipment alone does not establish future net export compensation.GuidanceCalifornia State Water Resources Control Board — Drinking Water Program · California Public Utilities Commission — Net metering and net billing
S07ModelCompare mixed paths alongside pure choicesInclude occupancy of lawful units, existing-property retention, rent on other lawful units, short hold-then-sale, sale of one asset, and staged reinvestment.Avoids forcing a false live-versus-rent-versus-sell choice.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Residential Rental Property · California Civil Code §1947.12 · California BOE — Proposition 19
S08MarketsPut reinvestment risk next to any staging headlineCompare 24–36 months of Treasury staging under crash-during and crash-after entry, lower renewal yields, inflation and a rising market before the decline.Staging changes risk timing; it does not make equities safe or guarantee a better ending value.GuidanceTreasuryDirect — Treasury bills · TreasuryDirect — Treasury notes · Federal Reserve History — Stock Market Crash of 1929 · Federal Reserve History — The Great Recession · U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — CPI, June 2022
S09GovernancePut reversal conditions on the first screenTrack the facts that change the path: trust restrictions, estate threshold, Prop 19 eligibility, unit legality, net rent, insurance, repair reserve and liquidity.A recommendation should name evidence that would reverse it.GuidanceIRS — Estate tax · California BOE — Proposition 19 · California Department of Insurance — Residential insurance · SEC Investor.gov — Stocks FAQs
