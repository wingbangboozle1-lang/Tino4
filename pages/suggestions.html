Suggested approval sequence

Status says what kind of evidence a row rests on: Verified means an official source states it, Owner means you supplied it and it is not independently confirmed, Projection means arithmetic on rounded inputs under a stated assumption, Assumption means an invented planning parameter, and Missing means the cell is blank because no evidence exists. A linked source supports the rule, not a private fact.
