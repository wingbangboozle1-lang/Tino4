Suggestions — review before implementing changes

Every suggested change to the household plan, listed in one place so it can be compared and decided on before anything is implemented.

Privacy & verification controls. The two properties appear only as Property A (the trust-held residence to be inherited) and Property B (the already-owned 3-unit building). No name, street address, parcel or account number, city name, neighbourhood name or ZIP code is published. Every owner-supplied figure was rounded to the nearest $10 before modelling, so exact tax-roll values cannot be matched by search. Official cells that no government source supports are left blank and explained on the Flagged for review page.

This section collects every suggested change to the household plan in one place so it can be reviewed and compared before anything is implemented. No suggestion is acted on until its open questions are answered and a tax or legal professional has confirmed it. Each suggestion links to the official basis it rests on and to the open questions or flagged items it depends on.

How suggestions are handled

  1. List the suggestion here and link it to its official sources and open questions.
  2. Answer the open questions it depends on (see the Open questions page).
  3. Have the trustee and a tax or legal professional confirm the suggestion fits current law.
  4. Record the decision—adopt, defer, or reject—with the date and the facts relied on.
  5. Implement only the adopted items, one at a time, and re-run this dashboard after each material change.
Model output, not tax, legal, investment or appraisal advice. Every published dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10; exact official statutory amounts are quoted as published and linked. Personal identities, street addresses, parcel numbers, city names, neighbourhood names and ZIP codes are not published anywhere on this site.
