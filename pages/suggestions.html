Suggestions — choose the best path before implementing any changes

Six ranked decision suggestions, site-improvement proposals, and a gated review flow so you choose in writing before acting.

Privacy & verification controls. The two properties appear only as Property A (the trust-held residence to be inherited) and Property B (the already-owned 3-unit building). No name, street address, parcel or account number, city name, neighbourhood name or ZIP code is published. Every owner-supplied figure was rounded to the nearest $10 before modelling, so exact tax-roll values cannot be matched by search. Official cells that no government source supports are left blank and explained on the Flagged for review page.
How to use this page. Review every suggestion here before changing anything on the site or committing to a financial plan. The first table is the six decision suggestions — pick one. The second table is site-improvement suggestions — pick which ones to implement. The third table is the gate sequence that forces a choice in writing before any execution step in the inheritance process.

Recommended review flow — do not implement until you have passed these gates

The gates are sequential: you cannot pass Gate 2 without answering Gate 1. A choice recorded here is reversible until the trust sells or files a Prop. 19 claim; after that the property-tax base and the basis are locked.
