S12TaxModel depreciation and the recapture it creates, on both buildingsPublish the 27.5-year illustration, the land/building allocation question, and the maximum 25% federal clawback plus California ordinary-rate treatment next to every rental path, and add the depreciation schedule to the records list the CPA must produce.A rental path compared without recapture is overstated by the whole deduction stream; this was the largest single modelling gap on the site.GuidanceIRS Publication 527 — Depreciation of rental property (MACRS) · IRS Topic 409 — Capital gains and losses · FTB — Capital gains and losses
S13DecisionCredit the live-in path with the rent it avoids (imputed rent)Add an imputed-rent input to the what-if tool and a live-in-versus-sell row that shows the sell path paying market rent, with the number left blank until written comparables exist.Comparing an owned home at zero housing cost against an invested portfolio that must also buy housing is not a comparison; the control makes the omission visible.GuidanceHUD — Fair Market Rents · IRS Publication 527 — Depreciation of rental property (MACRS)

S15ModelShow a distribution chart instead of a single projected ending valueTen-year outcome spread across the assumption bands (worst, quartiles, best) as bars, next to the certainty term the band does not contain — the property-tax saving of the hold path.One point estimate implies precision the evidence does not support; the spread is what a household actually experiences.GuidanceSEC Investor.gov — Stocks FAQs · Congressional Budget Office — The Long-Term Budget Outlook: 2025 to 2055 · U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — CPI, June 2022

S17TaxSet the primary-residence switch deliberately, path by pathTurn the IRC §121 exclusion on only for a path where the heir actually occupies the home for two of the last five years, and record that the tacking question for an inherited home is unresolved here.Applying the exclusion to a never-occupied inherited home overstates the sell path; ignoring it for a lived-in home understates the hold path.GuidanceIRS Topic no. 701 — Sale of your home · IRS Topic no. 701 — Sale of your home · IRS Publication 551 — Basis of Assets (Inherited Property; Like-Kind Exchanges)
