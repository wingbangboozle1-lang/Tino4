Approval gates before anything is implemented

Do not implement a financial or site change until the owner has reviewed it, the underlying private records exist, a qualified professional has confirmed the applicable law, and the model's result is reproducible from the stated assumptions. A suggestion selected here is a candidate, not an instruction.
