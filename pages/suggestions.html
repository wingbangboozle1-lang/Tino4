Suggestions for review

Review these improvements before adopting any change; this site does not apply recommendations automatically.

Create a verified document vault

Store deeds, trust documents, leases, tax bills, insurance policies, utility statements, and repair records in an access-controlled private location; publish only anonymized conclusions.

Compare mixed choices

Model occupying one unit while renting others where lawful, retaining one property while selling another, and staged investment rather than limiting comparison to pure choices.

Put reversal conditions first

List the facts that could change the preferred path—trust restrictions, occupancy, tax treatment, insurance availability, net rent, and liquidity—and document how each would be verified.

Stress sequence risk

Compare immediate investment with 24–36 months in short-duration U.S. Treasury securities followed by phased equity purchases; include reinvestment and inflation risk, not just crash protection.

Update on decision dates

Re-check official rules, professional quotes, property condition, market values, and investment assumptions when the transfer becomes imminent.

Planning framework only; not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, or investment advice. No household-specific inputs are published.
