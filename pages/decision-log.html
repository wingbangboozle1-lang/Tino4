Decision log and review queue

Twelve suggestions for improving decision quality, each with its effort, privacy impact and a local-only review choice — including the recorded rounding-policy decision. Nothing is applied automatically.

A review queue, not an automatic change

Each suggestion below records the decision quality it improves, the effort, and its privacy impact. Choosing an option changes nothing on the site and sends nothing anywhere; it only records your own review state in this browser. Implement a change only after the relevant owner has reviewed it and the necessary private records exist.
