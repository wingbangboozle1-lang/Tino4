Questions & missing evidence

Collect documents rather than guessing. Blank facts are intentionally not estimated.

Trustee / attorney

What transfers, when, to whom, with what restrictions, debts, and distribution deadlines? Is continued occupancy allowed?

CPA / tax attorney

What are the estate's total taxable assets and deductions? What basis and valuation method apply? Which returns and payments are required, and when?

Assessor / tax adviser

What is the current assessed value, applicable tax rate and direct assessments, and which exclusion or reassessment rules apply to the actual transfer?

Insurers / contractors

Obtain written primary, landlord, fire, earthquake, and liability quotes; replacement-cost estimates; inspection findings; and multi-year repair plans.

Property manager / rent counsel

Verify lawful unit count, permitted use, lease terms, rent limitations, tenant protections, and net achievable rent using current documents.

Investment adviser

Compare immediate investment with 24–36 month Treasury staging and phased stock purchases; show liquidity, after-tax yields, inflation, and historical crash-entry stress tests.

Planning framework only; not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, or investment advice. No household-specific inputs are published.
