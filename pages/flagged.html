Three irregularities worth your attention first

1. San Francisco's effective tax rate is ~2.1% of assessed value, against a published secured rate of 1.18162182%. That is a ~96 basis point gap — about $1,089 a year. It is plausibly parcel taxes and direct assessments, but it has not been reconciled to a bill.

2. Cupertino's effective rate is ~1.33%, above the county's 1.2% planning guidance. The 12-year series is internally consistent, so the rate is probably real; the split between ad valorem tax and charges is not.

3. Both insurance figures ($1,500/yr) are owner estimates. No agency publishes a parcel-level premium, and a three-unit Sunset building is a landlord policy, not a homeowners policy.

What to send back

  • One annual property tax bill for each property (redact the address and APN) — resolves items 1–4.
  • Two insurance quotes per property — replaces the weakest input in the cash-flow model.
  • Which property is the parents' principal residence, and whether a homeowners' exemption is filed there — resolves item 6, the highest-value open question.
