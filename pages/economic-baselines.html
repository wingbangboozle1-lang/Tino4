Economic baselines

Long horizons require more than one growth rate; official historical sources provide context, not certainty.

Regime method

Model distinct growth, inflation, interest-rate, recession, recovery and deflation blocks. Stress entry immediately before a severe decline, compare immediate investing with a Treasury ladder, and show both the protection from an early fall and the opportunity cost if markets rise during staging. These are sensitivities, not forecasts.

Research framework only; not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, investment, or property-management advice.

Recheck official authorities, source availability and law at the actual decision date.
