TAX & PROCESS

Estate tax & headroom

Rounded case figures, labeled assumptions and verified rules — separated clearly so nothing reads as a promise.

Tax is a whole-estate question, not a penalty on the house

Federal estate tax is the decedent’s estate liability, filed on Form 706 within 9 months of death (6-month filing extension available; payment separately due). The 40% rate applies only to the excess over the exclusion. California adds no estate or inheritance tax. The inherited house passes with a stepped-up basis; the inherited money is not California taxable income to the beneficiary.

Estate-planning levers to raise with the CPA (guidance, not advice)

Lifetime gifting up to the annual exclusion; early large gifts using lifetime exclusion; portability election; life-insurance ownership structuring; GRAT/IDGT-type vehicles — each has rules and risks. With a 2040-2050 horizon, gifting years are now. Nothing here is a recommendation; verify current law before acting.
