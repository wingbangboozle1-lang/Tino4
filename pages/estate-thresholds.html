Estate and inheritance tax questions
TopicCurrent official informationNext stepEvidenceOfficial source / scope
Federal estate taxThe 2026 basic exclusion is $15,000,000; the official 2027 amount is $15,480,000. Filing tests are estate-level and include adjusted taxable gifts; threshold law can change.Compare the entire estate, not just the inherited home or one beneficiary's distribution.Verified ruleCongress.gov — Public Law 119-21 · IRS — 2027 tax inflation adjustments · IRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs
Annual gift exclusionRead 2026-11-17: IRS inflation guidance sets the annual gift exclusion at $19,000 for calendar year 2026 and $20,000 for 2027; it is a gift-tax annual exclusion, not an inheritance allowance.Do not treat the gift exclusion as an amount a person may receive tax-free; the estate-level test is separate.Verified ruleIRS — 2027 tax inflation adjustments · IRS — Estate tax
Top federal rate and creditRead 2026-11-17: the Instructions for Form 706 (Rev. 7-2026) unified rate schedule applies 40% to taxable amounts over $1,000,000, and the 2026 basic exclusion of $15,000,000 has a credit equivalent of $5,945,800.Use the rate schedule and exclusion from the same year; do not mix years.Verified ruleIRS — Instructions for Form 706
Is there a per-heir allowance?The federal basic exclusion is applied to the decedent's estate, not to each beneficiary; the filing test adds adjusted taxable gifts and specific gift tax exemption.Compute at estate level with counsel; a per-heir assumption can understate exposure.Verified ruleIRS — Estate tax · IRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs
Portability mechanicsRead 2026-11-17: IRS states the portability election requires a timely filed Form 706 even when the estate is below the filing threshold, that the return is due nine months after death, and that an automatic six-month extension to file is available by filing Form 4768 on or before the due date; Revenue Procedure 2022-32 allows a simplified election within five years where no filing requirement exists.Ask now about prior taxable gifts and deceased-spouse unused exclusion; a late return can forfeit the election.Verified ruleIRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs · IRS — About Form 706
Can thresholds for 2040-2050 be projected from official sources?No official source publishes a federal exclusion for those decades; IRS publishes year-by-year inflation adjustments and CBO publishes bounded current-law baselines.Use dated scenarios and refresh annually from enacted law and IRS annual guidance; never present a projected threshold as an official figure.AssumptionIRS — 2027 tax inflation adjustments · CBO — The Budget and Economic Outlook: 2026 to 2036 · Congress.gov — Public Law 119-21
California gain tax and additional taxFTB says capital gains are taxed as ordinary income. The 2025 Form 540 line 62 instructions compute an additional 1% on taxable income above $1,000,000 under current statute; this is a tax on taxable income, not on the inheritance itself.Model the sale tax year with the CPA. The 2025 Form 540 line 62 instructions were read; recheck the instructions and law for the actual future sale year and apply the formula to private taxable-income facts.Verified ruleCalifornia statute — additional tax on high taxable income · FTB — 2025 Form 540 instructions · FTB — Capital gains and losses · California Franchise Tax Board — Forms and publications
California estate taxCalifornia's published guidance says estate tax returns are not required for deaths on or after January 1, 2005; current law must be rechecked at death.Separate state estate tax from federal estate tax and income-tax consequences.GuidanceCalifornia State Controller — Estate tax · California statute — death-transfer tax
Portability and prior giftsA surviving spouse's portability may require an estate-tax return even where the estate is below the filing threshold.Ask now about prior taxable gifts, deceased-spouse unused exclusion and timely filing.GuidanceIRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs · IRS — Estate tax
Payment calendarA federal estate return is generally due nine months after death; an extension of time to file is not an extension of time to pay.Calendar both dates at the death and plan funding before the filing window opens.Verified ruleIRS — About Form 706 · IRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs
Estate liquidity planAn estate concentrated in real estate can owe taxes and expenses on a calendar that does not wait for a property sale; funding routes (insurance in trust, financing, staged or installment sale) must be priced before they are needed.Build a dated liquidity inventory and obtain quotes and lender indications early; keep proceeds outside the estate where counsel advises.GuidanceIRS — Estate-tax filing FAQs · IRS — Estate tax
Worked sale-year sensitivity (California 1% add-on)The add-on equals 1% of taxable income above the threshold: each $100,000 of taxable income above $1,000,000 adds $1,000, or $100 per $10,000. The 2025 Form 540 line 62 instruction says to use whole dollars. Capital gains count in taxable income because California taxes them as ordinary income.Compute the actual sale-year figure from private basis, deductions and other income; recheck current law and instructions for the actual year.Verified ruleCalifornia statute — additional tax on high taxable income · FTB — 2025 Form 540 instructions · FTB — Capital gains and losses
Future thresholdNo official source can establish a future estate exclusion decades ahead; Congress can amend law.Use dated scenarios only and refresh from enacted law and IRS annual guidance.AssumptionCongress.gov — Public Law 119-21 · IRS — 2027 tax inflation adjustments
