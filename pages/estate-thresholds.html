Three things to ask before the money arrives

Portability: a deceased spousal unused exclusion can double the amount available, but only if a Form 706 was filed for the first death, whether or not it was otherwise required, and the relief for missing that deadline is limited. Prior taxable gifts: gifts since 1976 are added back when the tax is computed, so a gift history changes the answer at death. Liquidity: tax is paid in cash, and an estate whose wealth is two houses and a brokerage account needs the plan written before it is needed. Each of these is a document request, not an opinion.
