What this means

In the model's 2045 central case, the projected exclusion is about $22,680,800 per decedent. After including Property A's modelled value of $5,435,870, roughly $17,244,930 of cash headroom remains before any federal estate tax. California adds no estate or inheritance tax, and the beneficiary owes no income tax on the inheritance itself. If the estate is near the threshold, a timely Form 706 portability election can double the available exclusion.
