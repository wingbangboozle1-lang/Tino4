The exclusion is not a per-heir allowance and it is not an inheritance tax

The federal test compares the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts against the exclusion for the year of death. California levies no estate tax on deaths on or after 2005-01-01 and no inheritance tax; what California taxes is income - a later sale, a rental profit, and one percent of taxable income above the statutory million-dollar threshold. Do not add a state inheritance percentage to a model of this transfer; it is not in the law read for this build.
