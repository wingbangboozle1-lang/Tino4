Estate tax thresholds

What the federal and California rules actually say, the published amounts, and what is deliberately left blank because no official source states it.

Federal filing threshold and exclusions by year of death

The threshold is not an amount per beneficiary. The filing test uses the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts and the specific gift tax exemption.

How the federal calculation actually works

None of this determines whether this particular estate owes tax. That requires the inventory, the prior gift history and the deductions.

The inheritance is described as more than the exempt amount

The owner has said the inheritance includes a considerable sum that may exceed the amount that can pass without tax. That makes the gross estate calculation, not the home, the governing question. Obtain the estate inventory and the prior gift history from the trustee before drawing any conclusion, and confirm the filing obligation with the CPA in the year of death. The published amounts above are the only amounts this site will state.
