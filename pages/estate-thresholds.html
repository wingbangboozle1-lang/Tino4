Estate tax & headroom

A clear distinction between current law, missing evidence and review-only comparisons.

Estate tax is not a tax on the beneficiary simply for receiving an inheritance

Federal estate tax is generally an estate-level obligation. Filing, taxable estate, credits, income tax and the cash transferred are different concepts. The statutory examples use only the law and official thresholds published for the stated year; no future-law amount is inferred.

Federal/state/local scope

A whole-estate analysis may require citizenship/domicile, assets in other jurisdictions, prior taxable gifts, portability, trust inclusion, deductions and liquidity. California’s current estate-return treatment does not settle federal or other-jurisdiction exposure. Recheck enacted law at the actual event.
