Estate tax, gift exclusion and headroom

Federal thresholds currently verified from IRS sources, California death-tax law, and the specific facts still required to determine estate exposure.

The threshold is not per heir

Federal estate tax is an estate-level transfer tax. The IRS filing test uses the decedent's gross estate, adjusted taxable gifts and specific gift-tax exemption; a portability election can require a return below the threshold. A recipient's share alone cannot establish whether a return is required or tax is due.

Future law

The Working Families Tax Cuts (Public Law 119-21, enacted July 4, 2025) set the basic exclusion at $15,000,000 for 2026, and the IRS announced $15,480,000 for 2027. Nothing in the record establishes the amount for a death around the owner-planned transfer window, and no official source here indexes the exclusion that far. Do not compound today's exclusion forward; re-check the Internal Revenue Code, the annual IRS revenue procedure, California statutes and any enacted amendments in the actual year of death.
