Estate & inheritance law

Federal estate tax, California income tax and property tax are separate questions.

TopicVerified rule / limitationStatusOfficial source
Federal estate exclusionThe 2026 basic exclusion is $15,000,000; it is not a separate tax-free amount for every heir. Actual tax depends on the estate and gift history.Source-linked; check applicabilityIRS: estate and gift tax · IRS: estate tax
California income taxThe FTB says gifts and inheritances are generally not included in recipient income; later interest, dividends, rent and gains may be taxable.Source-linked; check applicabilityCalifornia FTB: gifts and inheritance
Inherited basisInherited property generally takes fair market value at death, or the alternate valuation date where properly elected; exceptions and trust inclusion matter.Source-linked; check applicabilityIRS: inherited property basis
California state/local death and inheritance taxCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §13301 bars the state and its political subdivisions from imposing taxes on an inheritance or death transfer. Property tax reassessment and later taxable income are distinct.Source-linked; check applicabilityCalifornia Revenue and Taxation Code §13301 · California BOE: Proposition 19

Estate tax is generally computed at the estate level, not as a tax penalty for receiving an inheritance. A trust’s terms do not by themselves establish tax treatment or an automatic basis adjustment.
