Trust vs. probate

A revocable living trust avoids probate for trust-titled property, but it does not avoid the property-tax change of ownership at death, nor federal estate-tax filing if the estate is large enough. The trust must be actually funded with the real property for this to apply; confirm title with the successor trustee and county recorder.
