Estate & inheritance law

Federal estate tax, California income tax and property tax are separate questions.

TopicVerified rule / limitationOfficial source
Federal estate exclusionThe 2026 basic exclusion is $15,000,000; it is not a separate tax-free amount for every heir. Actual tax depends on the estate and gift history.IRS: estate and gift tax · IRS: estate tax
California income taxThe FTB says gifts and inheritances are generally not included in recipient income; later interest, dividends, rent and gains may be taxable.California FTB: gifts and inheritance
Inherited basisInherited property generally takes fair market value at death, or the alternate valuation date where properly elected; exceptions and trust inclusion matter.IRS: inherited property basis
California estate taxThe available state analysis describes the state pick-up tax as zero after repeal of the federal credit. Reconfirm current law and special circumstances.California FTB: estate pick-up tax analysis (2017)
Local estate or inheritance taxIRS: estate tax

Estate tax is generally computed at the estate level, not as a tax penalty for receiving an inheritance. A trust’s terms do not by themselves establish tax treatment or an automatic basis adjustment.
