TopicRule / limitationStatusOfficial source / scope
Trust ownershipTrust administration follows the instrument. A revocable trust becoming irrevocable at death is typically the property-tax ownership-change moment; the label "trust" is not an exclusion.Verifiedprop19
Inherited basisGenerally fair market value at death (or valid elected alternate valuation); California conforms. Basis can step down as well as up.Verifiedbasis
Property-tax exclusionProp. 19 eligibility and continuing residence are separate from federal basis. Formula and claim deadlines are on the Property tax page.Verifiedprop19 fact19
Inherited principal vs incomeGifts and inheritances are excluded from California income; later income from them is taxable. Trust/estate income is separately reported (Form 541).Verifiedftbinherit 541
Federal gains on a later saleLong-term rates 0/15/20% by income; a prompt sale after inheritance usually shows little gain; a rental converts future growth into taxable gain.Verifiedgains basis
Depreciation recaptureUnrecaptured section 1250 gain on a rental is taxed up to 25% federal; California taxes gains as ordinary income; NIIT may add 3.8%.Verifiedgains niit cagains
Principal-residence sale exclusion$250,000/$500,000 potential exclusion with 2-of-5-year ownership/use tests — relevant if the household later sells A after living there.Verifiedhomegain
Residential rental rulesReport rent, allocate expenses on a documented basis, depreciate over 27.5 years (GDS). Personal-use days limit deductions.Verifiedrental
Rental caps for ASingle-family homes are generally outside AB 1482 caps only with the statutory notice; local rent-control coverage (Costa-Hawkins) must be checked for the actual tenancy.Verifiedab1482
San Francisco rules for BAllowable increase 1.6% for covered units (Mar 2026-Feb 2027); the Empty Homes Tax collection stopped after the Court of Appeal ruling; transfer tax applies on sale (inheritance transfers exempt).Verifiedrentlocal eht asrsf
Transfer tax on an eventual saleSan Francisco tier table (official factsheet): $750,000 per $500 in the 1-5 million tier (0.75%); county documentary tax is $0.55 per $500 (0.11%) under RTC 11911. Confirm current tiers before a large sale.Verifiedasrsf dtt
Federal investment contextTreasury interest is federally taxable but state-tax-exempt; FDIC covers $250,000 per depositor, bank and ownership category; stocks can lose value.Verifiedbills fdic stocks
