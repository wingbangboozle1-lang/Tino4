Taxes and simplifications

Example taxes are invented combined effective rates: 30% ordinary/interest, 25% capital gains/dividends and 30% depreciation-related gain. These are NOT statutory rates. No personal loss refund, NIIT stacking, SALT, passive loss carryovers, debt, fees, estate tax, half-month depreciation or local transfer charges are modeled.
