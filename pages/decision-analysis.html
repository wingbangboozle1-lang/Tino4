Why the mixed paths are modelled separately

Forcing a choice between live, rent and sell hides the paths that actually preserve optionality. Occupying for the qualifying year and then letting the property is a different economic path from occupying permanently, and selling one asset while keeping the other is different from selling both. Each has its own tax trigger, its own liquidity profile and its own reversal condition.

How to read this page

The paths are not ranked because the ranking depends on facts that are not yet in evidence. Choose the reversal column first: pick the two or three paths whose reversal conditions you could actually live with, then go and get the evidence that separates them. That inverts the usual order and prevents analysing a path the trust, the county or the insurance market will not permit.
