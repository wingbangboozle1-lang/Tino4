Terminals are after modeled taxes in nominal dollars, for Property A only (B is common to all paths). The transfer year is a picker on the Projections page; three examples are shown here. Model rates are invented combined effective rates (30% ordinary, 25% gains, 30% recapture) — NOT statutory. Achievable rent for A is unverified: the grid shows four assumption points.

ReasonMechanicsStatusOfficial source / scope
