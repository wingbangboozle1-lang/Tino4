Live, rent or sell

Current law, owner figures, stated assumptions and open questions, kept apart on purpose.

This page compares paths, it does not choose for you

The three modelled columns use the same transfer year, the same selling costs and the same tax assumptions. Property B's own value and rent are deliberately excluded from all of them, so the columns measure only the incremental decision about A. Anything you do to B is a separate comparison on the same arithmetic.

A warning about the assumptions that do the most work

Three invented numbers move this analysis more than anything else: the growth rate on the house, the return on the invested proceeds, and the rent that A could actually achieve. They are set at 4.0%, 6.0% and a 2.8% gross yield. The black-swan page shows what happens when each of them behaves badly, which is the only honest way to use them.
