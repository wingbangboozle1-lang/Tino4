Decision analysis: live in, rent out, or sell and reinvest

Six plans compared for every inheritance year 2040–2050, with sensitivity cases and the year-by-year cash-flow trail.

Privacy & verification controls. The two properties appear only as Property A (the trust-held residence to be inherited) and Property B (the already-owned 3-unit building). No name, street address, parcel or account number, city name, neighbourhood name or ZIP code is published. Every owner-supplied figure was rounded to the nearest $10 before modelling, so exact tax-roll values cannot be matched by search. Official cells that no government source supports are left blank and explained on the Flagged for review page.

This page compares the three decisions the brief asks about — live in the inherited house, rent it out, or sell and reinvest — for every possible inheritance year between 2040 and 2050. Each plan keeps Property B, pays its own property taxes, insurance, utilities, maintenance and income tax, and is then charged the tax that would be owed to convert the final assets into cash.
