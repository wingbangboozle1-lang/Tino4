Taxes and simplifications

Example taxes are invented combined effective rates: 30% ordinary/interest, 25% capital gains/dividends and 30% depreciation-related gain. These are NOT statutory rates. No personal loss refund, NIIT stacking, SALT, passive loss carryovers, debt, fees, estate tax, half-month depreciation or local transfer charges are modeled. A warning about the assumptions that do the most work

Three invented numbers move this analysis more than anything else: the growth rate on the house, the return on the invested proceeds, and the rent that A could actually achieve. They are set at 4.0%, 6.0% and a 2.8% gross yield. The black-swan page shows what happens when each of them behaves badly, which is the only honest way to use them.
