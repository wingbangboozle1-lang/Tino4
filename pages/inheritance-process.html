Step-by-step inheritance process

The trustee and qualified professionals should verify the actual trust and deadlines.

StepActionStatusOfficial source
1 · Before transferReview trust documents and title privately; determine who controls and inherits the property.Source-linked; check applicabilityCalifornia Probate Code: trustee notice
2 · At deathTrustee gives any required statutory notices and secures property, records and coverage.Source-linked; check applicabilityCalifornia Probate Code: trustee notice
3 · Establish valuesObtain date-of-death valuations and determine federal and California basis and estate inclusion.Source-linked; check applicabilityIRS: inherited property basis · IRS: estate tax
4 · Notify assessorCheck change-of-ownership forms, applicable due dates and supplemental assessments with the county.Source-linked; check applicabilityCalifornia BOE: property tax overview
5 · Review exclusionIf the relationship and residence qualify, occupy within one year and file the homeowners’ or disabled veterans’ exemption as required. Check the separate claim deadline and exceptions.Source-linked; check applicabilityCalifornia BOE: Proposition 19
6 · Administer taxesReview final individual, fiduciary and estate tax filing obligations, elections and payments.Source-linked; check applicabilityIRS: estate tax · California FTB: gifts and inheritance
7 · Decide and documentCompare occupy, rent or sell after verified taxes, insurance, maintenance, liquidity and existing housing are considered.Source-linked; check applicabilityIRS: residential rental property · IRS: inherited property basis
