This is not a calendar

The actual deadline depends on death date, transfer route, title, claim and statutory facts. Have counsel calculate every due date from official documents and put reminders in the private estate plan.

Two lessons the sequence teaches

First, the assessor filings and the exclusion claim run their own clocks while the household is still deciding what to do with the house, so the decision is time-limited even though the market is not. Second, most of these steps produce documents worth more to the plan than any forecast: the appraisal, the lease file, the insurance binder, the tax bill lines and the loan statement. Collect them first and the model becomes arithmetic instead of argument.
