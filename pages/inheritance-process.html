LAW AND PROCESS

Deadline timeline

What the inheritance process looks like step by step, with every deadline traced to the statute or the official calendar that sets it.

Every clock runs from the date of death

The timeline below is built from statutory deadlines and the official property tax calendar. It is a planning aid; the trustee and counsel control the actual dates and must confirm them.

Deadline timeline

  1. 1

    Day 0

    Date of death

    Every clock below is measured from this date. Nothing in this plan can be executed before the trustee confirms the trust terms and their own authority.

    California Probate Code §16061.7 IRS — Filing estate and gift tax returns

  2. 2

    60 days

    Trustee notice to beneficiaries and heirs

    A trustee must serve the statutory notification within 60 days after a revocable trust becomes irrevocable by reason of death, or within 60 days after becoming aware of a person entitled to notice. This is the step that starts the beneficiary's own review window.

    California Probate Code §16061.7

  3. 3

    150 days

    Change-in-ownership statement to the county

    For an interest in real property transferred by reason of death outside probate, including a transfer through the medium of a trust, the change-in-ownership statement must be filed within 150 days after the date of death. For other transfers the period is 90 days.

    California Revenue and Taxation Code §480

  4. 4

    9 months

    Federal estate tax return, if required

    Form 706 is generally due nine months after the date of death. A six-month extension is available only if it is requested before the due date and the estimated correct tax is paid before the due date. Filing may still be required below the threshold to elect portability.

    IRS — Filing estate and gift tax returns IRS — Frequently asked questions on estate taxes IRS — Estate tax

  5. 5

    Within 1 year

    Occupy the home and claim the homeowners' exemption

    Under the current intergenerational exclusion the transferred principal residence must become the transferee's principal residence within one year, and the transferee must file for the homeowners' or disabled veterans' exemption within one year. Missing this window removes the exclusion.

    California Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2 California BOE — Proposition 19 California Revenue and Taxation Code §218

  6. 6

    Within 3 years

    File the exclusion claim

    The claim must be filed within three years after the transfer, or before the property is transferred to a third party, or before the transferee stops occupying it, whichever is earliest. In defined circumstances a claim filed after the period may be considered but applies only prospectively.

    California Revenue and Taxation Code §63.2 California BOE — Proposition 19

  7. 7

    Every January 1

    Lien date

    January 1 is the lien date for all property. The assessment and the exemptions that apply on that date drive the bill for the following fiscal year.

    BOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax: An Overview (March 2025)

  8. 8

    Every February 15

    Exemption claim deadline

    February 15 is the legal deadline for filing most exemption claims, including the homeowners' exemption. A later filing can still receive a reduced share of the exemption by December 10.

    BOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax: An Overview (March 2025)

  9. 9

    Every July 2 to September 15

    Assessment appeal window

    Applications for reduction in assessed value are filed with the clerk of the county board of supervisors in the stated window, which can extend to November 30. This is the route for disputing a reassessment the owner believes is wrong.

    BOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax: An Overview (March 2025)

  10. 10

    December 10 and April 10

    Installment delinquency dates

    The first secured installment is delinquent after December 10 and the second after April 10. Missing either date adds penalties that compound the carrying cost of a high-value property.

    BOE Publication 29 — California Property Tax: An Overview (March 2025)
