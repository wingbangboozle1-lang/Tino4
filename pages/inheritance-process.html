LAW & PROCESS

Inheritance process and deadline timeline

A relative step-by-step sequence for a trust-based transfer, with official clocks distinguished from operational checkpoints.

Start clocks only from their legal trigger

These deadlines are not interchangeable. A trust-notice clock, county property-tax filing, federal estate return and Proposition 19 claims can have different triggers and responsible filers. Counsel should confirm the actual dates from the death and transfer records.

Deadline timeline

Relative deadlines only. No personal date is published. The clock on each row starts from a different legal trigger, so they are not interchangeable.

  1. Day of deathThe trust's irrevocable trigger and the date every later clock measures from.Who: Trustee / counselKeep: Death certificate, trust instrument, prior amendments
  2. By day 60Trustee notification to beneficiaries and heirs, if the statutory trigger applies.Who: Trustee / attorneyKeep: Copy of the notification and proof of service
  3. By day 150Change-in-ownership statement for property transferred by reason of death, including through a trust.Who: Trustee / transfereeKeep: Filed statement and county acknowledgement
  4. By month 9Form 706 due if the estate meets the filing test; an extension of up to six months can be requested by the due date.Who: Executor / CPAKeep: Return, extension request, valuation reports
  5. By month 12Qualifying transferee establishes principal residence and files the homeowners' or disabled veterans' exemption for a family-home exclusion.Who: Transferee / assessorKeep: Exemption filing, occupancy evidence
  6. By month 36Intergenerational exclusion claim window closes, or earlier if the property is transferred to a third party.Who: Transferee / assessorKeep: Claim form and assessor determination
  7. Every year by February 15Timely exemption-claim deadline published by the BOE for most exemptions, with a reduced benefit for later homeowners' filings.Who: Owner / assessorKeep: Filed claim receipt
  8. Every year on the lien dateJanuary 1 valuation date for the property-tax roll; the annual assessment and any decline-in-value review attach here.Who: Assessor / property-tax adviserKeep: Roll value and notice
  9. Each April 10 and December 10Last day to pay the second and first secured-tax instalments without a penalty.Who: Owner / tax collectorKeep: Payment receipts
  10. Annually, from now until transferRe-check enacted federal and California law, the assessor record, insurance availability, leases and market inputs.Who: Attorney / CPA / adviserKeep: Dated review note in the private file

No dates have been assigned

The public page does not publish a personal transfer date. Once counsel confirms the date of death, date title changes, trust status and occupancy plan, replace these relative deadlines with calendar dates in a private checklist.
