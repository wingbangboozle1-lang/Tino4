TAX & PROCESS

Inheritance step by step

Rounded case figures, labeled assumptions and verified rules — separated clearly so nothing reads as a promise.

Timeline starts at the legally relevant event

Do not wait for a 2040-2050 distribution date to find deadlines. Death, trust irrevocability, beneficial vesting, recording and distribution can be different events. The clock below assumes death is the trigger — the trustee’s attorney must confirm.
