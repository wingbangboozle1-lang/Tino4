Inheritance step by step

Current law, owner figures, stated assumptions and open questions, kept apart on purpose.

This is the process, with the clocks that carry penalties

Four statutory clocks matter and each one costs money if it is missed: 60 days for the trustee notification, 150 days for the death-transfer statement, 9 months for a federal estate return, 1 year for the homeowners exemption and 3 years for the exclusion claim. Only the last two carry a benefit, and only if they are filed. Nothing in this table is legal advice for a particular trust; the trustee and a lawyer have to apply it.
