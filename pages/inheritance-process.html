Inheritance step by step

A clear distinction between current law, missing evidence and review-only comparisons.

Timeline starts at the legally relevant event

Do not wait for a 2040–2050 planned distribution date to identify deadlines. Death, trust irrevocability, beneficial vesting, recording and distribution may be different events. Exact dates and exceptions require the trustee’s attorney.
