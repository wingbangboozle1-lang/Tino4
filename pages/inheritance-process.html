The inheritance, step by step

What to do before and after the transfer, in order, with the form and the deadline for each step.

The steps in order. Deadlines run from the date of death. Forms named BOE are filed with the county assessor where the property is.

StepWhatWhy and howWhenOfficial sourceStatus
