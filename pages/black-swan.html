Black swan conditions

Four officially documented economic regimes, what each does to the decision, and where a staged entry fits.

Regimes, not averages

Growth and inflation are never a constant line for fifty years. This page describes four officially documented regimes and what each one does to the decision. Every figure quoted is a historical observation read from an official history page; none is a forecast.
