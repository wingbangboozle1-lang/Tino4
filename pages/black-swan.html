Read this before the other pages

Every other page grows a number by a constant percentage for fifty years. Nothing in the real world does that. This page replaces the constants with the actual published record where one exists, and with labelled inventions where it does not. No probability, success rate or forecast appears anywhere on it, because none has been verified.

What is missing from this page on purpose

There is no probability of a crash, no expected return, no success rate and no backtest of an investment strategy. The United States Treasury and the securities regulator both publish risk warnings rather than forecasts, and the one official long-run interest-rate projection that exists, from the Congressional Budget Office, covers ten years and not fifty. Anyone quoting a fifty-year number with a decimal point is inventing it.
