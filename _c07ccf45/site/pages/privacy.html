Privacy and anonymization controls

What is omitted, why rounding to $10 matters, and the residual exposure that no page-level control can remove.

What is deliberately absent

ControlHow it is appliedWhy it mattersOfficial basis
Placeholders instead of identitiesThe site names the two properties only as Property A and Property B. No individual, trustee, beneficiary, lender or adviser is named.The repository is public; a name in a page is a name on the internet.S28
No addresses, parcels or jurisdictionsNo street address, assessor parcel number, account number, city name, neighbourhood name or ZIP code appears anywhere on the site.A parcel number or a ZIP plus a value is enough to identify the property on a county roll.S21 S28
Rounding to the nearest $10Every supplied assessment, tax bill, market value, rent and utility figure was rounded before modelling, and every published amount is a multiple of $10.An exact tax bill is a search key. Rounding to $10 removes the exact match while preserving every decision.S28 S30
Blank rather than guessedWhere no official source exists, the cell is empty and logged on the Flagged for review page.An invented official value would be both wrong and unfalsifiable.S36 S38
Not indexed by search enginesEvery page carries a no-index directive.Keeps the model out of search results even though the repository is public.S28
Working files excluded from publicationModel code, data files, the coordination board and this session's directory are excluded from the site build and are not served.Publication is limited to the static pages that were reviewed.S21

Residual exposure you should know about

Residual riskStatusWhat remains to be doneOfficial basis
The Git repository is publicRounding and placeholders reduce but do not eliminate re-identification when combined with knowledge of two properties in one market.If the material must be non-public, the repository should be made private or the analysis kept out of version control.S21
Earlier commits in the repository's historyPrior working commits may contain pre-rounding figures; the live site does not serve them, but history is not rewritten by a normal commit.History rewriting requires owner action with a force-push; it has not been performed.S21
Third-party cachesSearch engines and archives can retain copies of previously served pages.Removal requests and a no-index directive are the available controls.S28
Facts the model reveals indirectlyA recommendation this specific implies a household with two properties in one metropolitan area.The site states the trade-offs in general terms and avoids facts that are not needed for the decision.S21

This page describes controls, not a guarantee. Rounding and anonymisation reduce the risk of casual re-identification; they do not make a public repository private.

Model output, not tax, legal, investment or appraisal advice. Every published dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10; exact official statutory amounts are quoted as published and linked. Personal identities, street addresses, parcel numbers, city names, neighbourhood names and ZIP codes are not published anywhere on this site.
