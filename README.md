# Privacy-first inheritance decision workspace

This repository builds a static, source-linked decision framework for two properties: an anticipated future trust transfer (Property A, Cupertino area) and a separate already-owned rental/residence (Property B, San Francisco Outer Sunset area). The planning window for the trust transfer is the owner's stated 2040-2050 range.

## Site contents (24 pages, all generated by `tools/sitebuild.py`)

- `index.html` — decision overview: the conditional best plan, "what would change the answer" triage, reinvestment risk beside the headline.
- `pages/executive-summary.html` — the short version with the key computed numbers.
- `pages/decision-analysis.html` — pure and mixed paths, a computed 2045 path comparison, and local-only what-if controls.
- `pages/property-profiles.html` — both properties side by side with owner-supplied, privacy-rounded tax/assessment histories, utilities, insurance and rent context.
- `pages/projections.html` — baseline assumptions and the year-by-year 2026-2050 projection table (market value, assessed value, property tax in both Prop 19 branches, Property B, indexed federal estate threshold).
- `pages/property-tax.html`, `pages/operating-costs.html` — rules, range methods, and owner-supplied cost rows with verification paths; the rate-area method and the enacted San Francisco Proposition H schedule.
- `pages/sale-timing.html` — why a sale in the same tax year as the transfer is modeled first, the four exit-year cases with depreciation and recapture, the written valuation the estate needs, the section 1031 question, the transfer-tax stack, and the Proposition 19 occupancy rule with its break-even and carry-forward allowance.
- `pages/capital-plan.html` — five-year maintenance and capital plans for both buildings, carrying-cost and reserve bands, the depreciation/recapture rules, and the annual re-run protocol.
- `pages/investment-policy.html` — a ten-section policy to sign before proceeds arrive, the yield and risk facts it must state honestly, rebalance bands, review triggers, and the estate-liquidity clauses.
- `pages/black-swan.html`, `pages/phased-investing.html` — stress regimes, November 2026 Treasury context, and the 3M–2Y / 24–36-month staging comparison (interactive tool plus a static dollar-mode table).
- `pages/estate-thresholds.html` — official 2026/2027 amounts and the assumption path to 2030-2050; gift exclusions; California's no-estate-tax rule.
- `pages/inheritance-process.html` — step-by-step process and the deadline timeline (60-day trust notice, 150-day change-in-ownership statement, 9-month Form 706, 1-year exemption/occupancy, 3-year claim, payment dates).
- `pages/law-watchlist.html`, `pages/questions.html`, `pages/suggestions.html`, `pages/decision-log.html` — law and market watchlist; the question-by-question register of missing information with discovery steps; reviewable suggestion queues.
- `pages/requirements-tracker.html` — every requirement from the owner's list mapped to how this build treats it now and the next step that would close it (the register that keeps the two privacy postures comparable).
- `pages/attorney-cpa-brief.html` — one-page printable adviser handoff (Print button; no identifying details).
- `pages/flagged-for-review.html`, `pages/glossary.html`, `pages/sources.html`, `pages/privacy.html` — flags F-01..F-28, glossary, the official source register, and privacy boundaries.

## Build and verify

```sh
python3 tools/build.py
python3 tools/verify.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools -p 'test_*.py' -v
```

CI (`.github/workflows/verify.yml`) runs the tests, rebuilds, verifies, and confirms the committed site matches a fresh build on every push and PR.

The site uses no remote scripts, fonts, images, APIs, analytics or form submission. Calculator inputs stay in browser memory; suggestion review choices stay in this browser's local storage. Official links open only when a reader follows them.

## Evidence and limitations

- Current rules and public observations are linked to official IRS, Congress, U.S. Code, Treasury, California BOE/legislation, Federal Reserve History, BLS, CBO, FHFA, HUD, CDI, CPUC and provider sources in the source register.
- Derived numbers (2045 tax branches, rent hurdle, sale proceeds, staging table) are reproducible from the labeled assumptions in `tools/sitebuild.py` and rounded to the nearest ten dollars.
- Owner-supplied figures are not independently verified; professional confirmation (attorney, CPA, assessor, insurer) is required before any real decision. Re-check law, thresholds and costs at the actual transfer date.
