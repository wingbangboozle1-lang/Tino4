# Privacy-first inheritance decision workspace

This repository builds a static, source-linked decision framework for two properties: an anticipated future trust transfer (Property A, a Peninsula property in Santa Clara County) and a separate already-owned two-of-three-unit rental/residence (Property B, City and County of San Francisco). Place names below are anonymised; the county and city-county appear only because their ordinances are the governing rules.. The planning window for the trust transfer is the owner's stated 2040-2050 range.

## Privacy and exposure — read first

- **Rounded-figure policy (owner-directed, 2026-11-15).** The owner instructed that decision-driving costs and numbers be published but rounded to the nearest ten dollars so they cannot be searched back to a source record. Owner-supplied rows carry an `Owner-supplied (privacy-rounded)` badge and a link to the official record that can confirm them. Every published currency amount must end in 0 (or be a listed official figure such as the IRS exclusions) — `tools/verify.py` fails the build otherwise.
- **Never published:** names, street addresses, parcel/APN numbers, account numbers, unrounded amounts. **Place identifiers were removed from the published site on 2026-11-15:** ZIP codes, the city name and the neighbourhood name no longer appear on any page; only the governing jurisdictions (Santa Clara County, City and County of San Francisco) are named, because their ordinances are the rules being applied. A second dial, `PUBLISH_ASSESSMENT_LEVELS`, replaces the absolute assessed-value series with an index (2014 = 100) for owners who want the residual re-identification risk removed as well.
- **Verification discipline.** Every table row carries an evidence badge and at least one official source link. Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are left blank or badged `Owner-supplied (privacy-rounded)` / `Cross-checked` / `Not verified` and are listed on the [Flagged for Review page](https://wingbangboozle1-lang.github.io/Tino4/pages/flagged-for-review.html). Nothing is inferred to fill a gap.
- **Repository exposure (urgent, unresolved).** The repository is public. Earlier Git history contains earlier, unrounded case material (pattern-audit counts are recorded on the flagged page). This platform pins each session to one branch and forbids force-pushing other branches, so **the history has not been scrubbed**. Repository-owner/admin coordination is required for visibility review, a supported history rewrite of all refs, and GitHub Support follow-up (PR diffs, forks, caches and clones can retain data). Do not store deeds, trust documents, statements, tax bills, leases, quotes or other identifying records in this repository.

## Site contents (21 pages, all generated by `tools/sitebuild.py`)

- `index.html` — decision overview: the conditional best plan, "what would change the answer" triage, reinvestment risk beside the headline.
- `pages/executive-summary.html` — the short version with the key computed numbers.
- `pages/decision-analysis.html` — pure and mixed paths, a computed 2045 path comparison, and local-only what-if controls.
- `pages/property-profiles.html` — both properties side by side with owner-supplied, privacy-rounded tax/assessment histories, utilities, insurance and rent context.
- `pages/projections.html` — baseline assumptions and the year-by-year 2026-2050 projection table (market value, assessed value, property tax in both Prop 19 branches, Property B, indexed federal estate threshold).
- `pages/property-tax.html`, `pages/operating-costs.html` — rules, range methods, and owner-supplied cost rows with verification paths.
- `pages/black-swan.html`, `pages/phased-investing.html` — stress regimes, November 2026 Treasury context, and the 3M–2Y / 24–36-month staging comparison (interactive tool plus a static dollar-mode table).
- `pages/capital-and-tax-model.html` — the costs a gross-vs-net comparison forgets: depreciation and its recapture, transfer tax and recording charges, the primary-residence switch, imputed rent on the live-in path, a five-year capital plan, and a rent-comparables ledger that stays blank until written comps exist.
- `pages/estate-thresholds.html` — official 2026/2027 amounts and the assumption path to 2030-2050; gift exclusions; California's no-estate-tax rule.
- `pages/inheritance-process.html` — step-by-step process and the deadline timeline (60-day trust notice, 150-day change-in-ownership statement, 9-month Form 706, 1-year exemption/occupancy, 3-year claim, payment dates).
- `pages/law-watchlist.html`, `pages/questions.html`, `pages/suggestions.html`, `pages/decision-log.html` — law and market watchlist; the question-by-question register of missing information with discovery steps; reviewable suggestion queues.
- `pages/attorney-cpa-brief.html` — one-page printable adviser handoff (Print button; no identifying details).
- `pages/flagged-for-review.html`, `pages/glossary.html`, `pages/sources.html`, `pages/privacy.html` — flags F-01..F-27, glossary, the official source register, and privacy boundaries.

## Build and verify

```sh
python3 tools/build.py
python3 tools/verify.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools -p 'test_*.py' -v
python3 tools/recheck.py                 # did the recommendation move? (MOVED / UNCHANGED)
node tools/dom-test.cjs                  # optional: needs `npm install --no-save jsdom`
```

`tools/recheck.py` rebuilds, verifies, and compares a snapshot of every value the published ranking rests on
(`tools/recommendation-snapshot.json`) against the current build. After changing any assumption in
`tools/sitebuild.py`, run it before repeating the conclusion: `MOVED` means the reasoning has to be re-read,
`UNCHANGED` means the published recommendation still follows from the inputs. Accept a reviewed change of
state with `python3 tools/recheck.py --update`. The release checks are mechanical, not stylistic: any published
currency amount must be $10-rounded or a registered official figure, no file in the tree may contain a
comma-grouped unrounded amount or a rounded/unrounded mapping, no page may carry a five-digit identifier
that is not an allowlisted public figure, and no page may name a street address, email, phone or parcel/APN.

CI (`.github/workflows/verify.yml`) runs the tests, rebuilds, verifies, and confirms the committed site matches a fresh build on every push and PR.

The site uses no remote scripts, fonts, images, APIs, analytics or form submission. Calculator inputs stay in browser memory; suggestion review choices stay in this browser's local storage. Official links open only when a reader follows them.

## Evidence and limitations

- Current rules and public observations are linked to official IRS, Congress, U.S. Code, Treasury, California BOE/legislation, Federal Reserve History, BLS, CBO, FHFA, HUD, CDI, CPUC and provider sources in the source register.
- Re-check cadence is published on the law watchlist: annually (fixed date, re-run + recheck), biennially in February for the Proposition 19 adjustment, every legislative session for proposals, and immediately on any triggering event because the statutory clocks do not wait for analysis.
- Derived numbers (2045 tax branches, rent hurdle, sale proceeds, staging table, distribution percentiles) are reproducible from the labeled assumptions in `tools/sitebuild.py` and rounded to the nearest ten dollars.
- Owner-supplied figures are not independently verified; professional confirmation (attorney, CPA, assessor, insurer) is required before any real decision. Re-check law, thresholds and costs at the actual transfer date.
