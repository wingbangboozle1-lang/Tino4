Overview / Decisions

Decision framework

Compare net outcomes, not headline values.

Use the same forecast period, taxes, maintenance assumptions, and liquidity reserve for all three Home A options. Home B remains a separate baseline so using Home A is not treated as if housing at Home B were cost-free.

No financial winner until verified inputs exist

Executive recommendation

Do not commit today to keeping, renting, or selling an inheritance expected in 2040–2050. Preserve flexibility; validate the trust and records; then compare after-tax cash flows at the time of transfer. If the property will be retained and Prop 19 eligibility is sought, occupancy and timely exemption filing are time-sensitive conditions.

Minimum model equations

  • Rental net cash flow = collected rent − vacancy/credit loss − owner-paid operating costs − taxes/insurance − capital reserve − income tax.
  • Sale investable cash = sale price − selling costs − secured debt − taxes − required reserve.
  • Hold vs. sell = future after-tax property equity + net rental cash flows vs. future value of after-tax investable sale cash.
  • Live-in value = avoided comparable rent − ownership carrying cost − foregone net rent/portfolio return.

A formula is not a projection: no unknown input is assumed or silently populated.

Why current winner is unknown

  • Current estimated market values and historic bills provided by the user are not independently verified and are not republished.
  • Independent insurance quotes and reliable repair/utility costs are missing.
  • Trust terms, basis, liens, co-beneficiaries, tax domicile, and federal adjusted taxable gifts are unknown.
  • Long-run investment returns, sale costs, inflation, laws, and property markets are uncertain.
