How a trust inheritance can unfold.

These are general procedural checkpoints. The trust, title, family relationships, property location, tax residence, and asset values control the actual result.

Official-source summary
Key correction: the federal estate-tax exclusion is not a cap on how much a beneficiary may inherit. It is applied to the decedent's taxable estate and adjusted taxable gifts. A beneficiary's income-tax basis and a property's county-assessed value are separate concepts.

Transfer timeline (trust-owned California real property)

1
Locate the governing documents and confirm title.

Get the complete trust and amendments, death certificate, recorded deed, asset schedule, and any loan/insurance records. Confirm that each property was actually transferred into the trust; a trust only governs assets titled to it. Consult trust counsel if ownership or terms are unclear. Santa Clara Superior Court: living trusts

2
Successor trustee accepts authority and secures property.

California court guidance says a successor trustee can use an acceptance of trusteeship and/or certification of trust to establish authority with institutions. Arrange safe access, preserve records, keep coverage active, and identify occupants and ongoing rental obligations. Follow trust terms and fiduciary duties. Santa Clara Superior Court: probate trusts

3
Notify the county assessor about trust real estate.

For California real estate subject to property tax, the trustee must give written notice to the county assessor within 150 days of the settlor's death. Confirm required forms and change-in-ownership filings directly with each county. Santa Clara Superior Court: trustee duties

4
Determine federal estate / gift tax filing and portability.

For a U.S. citizen or resident dying in 2026, Form 706 filing is generally required when gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts and specific exemption is over $15,000,000. An executor can file to elect portability even below that amount. The ordinary due date and tax payment date are nine months after death; certain extension rules apply. Verify the year-of-death form and threshold at the time. IRS Instructions for Form 706

5
Obtain defensible date-of-death valuation and tax basis records.

IRS states inherited-property basis is generally fair market value on the date of death (or a properly elected alternate valuation date). Keep appraisal, improvements, depreciation and estate-value documentation. Basis is not the same as the county's Proposition 13 taxable value. IRS: gifts and inheritances · California BOE: property assessment

6
File the county Prop 19 claim if potentially eligible.

Determine family relationship and family-home qualification. The transferee must occupy the home as a principal residence and apply for the homeowners' or disabled veterans' exemption within one year. Ask the assessor to confirm all filing and valuation steps, including treatment of a multi-unit property. California BOE: Prop 19 exclusion adjustment

7
Settle valid obligations, then distribute or continue in trust.

Follow trust terms; administer debts, taxes, income, tenants and beneficiary distributions. A trust can continue after death. Whether a deed needs recording, title insurance, lender notice, or local rental action depends on the documents and facts. Santa Clara Superior Court: trustee duties

What can trigger a federal estate tax return?

For 2026 only, IRS instructions say a U.S. citizen/resident decedent's executor must file Form 706 if the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts and specific exemption exceeds $15 million; or if electing DSUE portability regardless of estate size. A return can therefore be useful or required even if no estate tax is payable. Noncitizen/nonresident estates have distinct rules.

Why future thresholds are blank

The stated 2040–2050 transfer date is many years after the currently published 2026 amounts. Tax laws can change and future inflation-adjusted values are not yet known. The page does not grow today's $15 million amount by a speculative inflation rate. Recheck IRS instructions and applicable federal law in the transfer year.
