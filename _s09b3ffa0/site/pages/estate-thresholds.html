Verified Three-Level Death & Cash-Inheritance Tax Summary:
  • Local (County & City): $0 Inheritance / Estate Tax — California Revenue & Taxation Code §13301 (enacted by Proposition 6) constitutionally bars all California counties and cities from levying any inheritance, estate, or death transfer tax; bona fide inheritances without consideration are also exempt from documentary/real-property transfer tax.
  • State of California: $0 Inheritance / Estate / Income Tax on Principal — California's dormant pickup estate tax (CA RTC §13302) dropped to $0 on January 1, 2005 (State Controller), and inherited cash/property is excluded from California taxable income (FTB).
  • Federal Estate & Gift Tax (IRC §2010(c)(3) as amended by P.L. 119-21): Basic exclusion is $15,000,000 in 2026 and $15,480,000 in 2027 (Rev. Proc. 2026-35 §3.44), permanently indexed for inflation ($30,000,000 in 2026 / $30,960,000 in 2027 for married couples electing DSUE portability on timely Form 706, or within 5 years under Rev. Proc. 2022-32). Annual gift-tax exclusion is $19,000 in 2026 and $20,000 in 2027 per recipient.
