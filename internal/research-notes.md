## A. Federal estate / gift / income tax

- **IRS, Instructions for Form 706 (Rev. July 2026)** — https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i706.pdf
  - "For decedents dying in 2026 … The basic exclusion amount is $15,000,000." "The basic
    credit amount is $5,945,800." "Form 706 must be filed by the executor of the estate of
    every U.S. citizen or resident: Whose gross estate, plus adjusted taxable gifts and
    specific exemption, is more than $15,000,000".
  - "You must file Form 706 … within 9 months after the date of the decedent's death …
    Form 4768 … automatic 6-month extension". "The estate and GST taxes are due within 9
    months". Portability (DSUE) requires a timely Form 706.
- **IRS, IRM 3.12.263** — https://www.irs.gov/irm/part3/irm_03-012-263r — 2026
  $15,000,000; 2025 $13,990,000; 2024 $13,610,000.
- **IRS, Rev. Proc. 2025-32** — https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf
  - §4.03 2026 maximum capital-gains rate thresholds: MFJ 0% to $98,900, 15% to $613,700;
    all other individuals 0% to $49,450, 15% to $545,500; estates/trusts $3,300 / $16,250.
  - §4.42 "For calendar year 2026, the first $19,000 of gifts to any person … are not
    included in the total amount of taxable gifts". Also states the $15,000,000
    §2631(c) GST amount "will be adjusted for inflation for calendar year 2027 and future
    years".
- **IRS, 2026 Instructions for Form 709 (early-release draft)** —
  https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-dft/i709--dft.pdf — "The annual gift exclusion for 2026 is
  $19,000"; "The top rate for gifts and generation-skipping transfers remains at 40%";
  "basic exclusion amount ($15,000,000 in 2026)" and "lifetime GST exemption … for 2026 is
  $15,000,000". NOTE: the document is labelled DRAFT — NOT FOR FILING on irs.gov; flagged.
- **Congress.gov, Public Law 119-21 (H.R.1), §70106** —
  https://www.congress.gov/119/plaws/publ21/PLAW-119publ21.pdf and
  https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/1/all-info — "(Sec. 70106) This
  section increases the base estate tax, gift tax, and generation-skipping transfer tax
  exemption amount after 2025 to $15 million (from $5 million), adjusted for inflation."
  Enacted 07/04/2025; no sunset.
- **IRS Publication 551 (12/2025)** — https://www.irs.gov/publications/p551 — "Inherited
  Property: 1. The FMV of the property at the date of the individual's death … If a
  federal estate tax return does not have to be filed, your basis in the inherited property
  is its appraised value at the date of death …" (step-up in basis).
- **IRS Topic 701** — https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc701 — "exclude up to $250,000 of that
  gain … or up to $500,000 … if you file a joint return"; ownership and use tests = 24
  months out of the last 5 years.
- **IRS Topic 409** — https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409 — capital-gain rates 0% / 15% /

- **IRS Topic 559** — https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc559 — "A 3.8 percent net investment

- **IRS Publication 527 (2025)** — https://www.irs.gov/publications/p527 — residential

- **Congress.gov CRS IF12846 "The Estate and Gift Tax"** —

  https://www.ftb.ca.gov/tax-pros/law/legislation/2017-2018/sb726-032317.pdf —

  the estate 'pick-up' tax … The federal estate tax credit was repealed for decedents dying

  rate is 40 percent." NOTE: a 2017 bill analysis describing law in force; the bill itself
  did not enact a CA estate tax. Flagged for confirmation that no later CA estate tax was
  enacted (see 2026 Proposition 40, below, which is a wealth tax on >$1B, not an estate tax).

## C. California property tax (state and county)

- **BOE Publication 29 (March 2025)** — https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/pub29.pdf —
  "limited the property tax rate to 1 percent plus the rate necessary to fund local
  voter-approved bonded indebtedness"; "properties are reassessed to current market value
  only upon a change in ownership or completion of new construction"; "generally limits
  annual increases in the base year value … to no more than 2 percent".
- **BOE LTA 2025/023** — https://boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/lta25023.pdf and **Pub 800-10** —
  https://boe.ca.gov/pdf/pub800-10.pdf — FBYV increases capped at 2%/yr; assessor enrolls
  lower of FBYV and market value.
- **BOE LTA 2025/015 (Publication 800-1/800-2, May 22, 2025)** —
  https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/lta25015.pdf — Prop 19 conditions: family home must
  have been transferor's principal residence; transferee must live in the home as primary
  residence **within one year** and file homeowners'/disabled veterans' exemption within one
  year; "no exceptions to this requirement are available"; if transferee moves out, taxable
  value becomes market value as of the date the transferee obtained ownership adjusted for
  inflation.
- **BOE News Release 25-02 (March 7, 2025)** —
  https://www.boe.ca.gov/news/2025/nr-25-02.htm — "The excludable amount is capped at an
  amount equal to the principal residence or family farm's factored base year value
  (taxable value) at the time of the transfer plus $1,044,586" for transfers 2/16/2025 –
  2/15/2027; must apply for Homeowners' or Disabled Veterans' Exemption within one year.
  Adjustment is biennial, based on FHFA House Price Index for California.
- **BOE "Introduction to Proposition 19" (April 2023)** —
  https://www.boe.ca.gov/pdf/Introduction-to-Prop-19-042023.pdf — value-limit worked
  example: FBYV $300,000 + $1,000,000 = excluded amount $1,300,000; FMV $1,500,000 →
  new taxable value $500,000 (i.e. FMV less the $1,000,000 allowance).
  "Submit form BOE-19-P … within 3 years of transfer date"; "file … homeowners' or
  disabled veterans' exemption within one year … by filing form BOE-266 or BOE-261-G".
- **BOE sample BOE-19-P (REV. 04 (07-25))** —
  https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/sample-boe19p.pdf — "For real property that is
  inherited via trust, will, or intestate succession, date of death is the date of change in
  ownership"; "If the transfer was through the medium of a will and/or trust, you must
  attach a full and complete copy of the will and/or trust"; claim timely if filed within
  three years; prospective relief if filed late. NOTE: SAMPLE ONLY — flagged.
- **BOE Publication 800-6** — https://www.boe.ca.gov/pdf/pub800-6.pdf — Homeowners'
  Exemption = $7,000 of assessed value (~$70 at 1%, $82 at 1.17%); "The exemption does not
  apply to property that is rented, vacant, or under construction"; "In the case of
  inheritance, the date of death is considered the date of transfer for property tax
  purposes, even if the property was held in a trust and remains in the trust after the
  death"; only one exemption per couple.
- **BOE sample BOE-502-AH** — https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/sample-boe502ah.pdf —
  change of ownership statement generally within 90 days; "except that where the change in
  ownership has occurred by reason of death the statement shall be filed within 150 days
  after the date of death"; penalty $100 or 10% of taxes on new base year value (capped).
- **BOE Board Meeting May 2026, Property Tax Forms** —
  https://www.boe.ca.gov/meetings/pdf/2026/202605-PT-Forms.pdf — R&T §480(b): for transfers
  by reason of death including through a trust, "the change in ownership statement or
  statements shall be filed by the trustee (if the property was held in trust) or the
  transferee … within 150 days after the date of death"; BOE-502-AH / BOE-502-D.
- **BOE Property Tax Annotation 220.0760** —
  https://www.boe.ca.gov/lawguides/property/current/ptlg/annt/220-0760.html — R&T §62(d)
  excludes from change in ownership transfers into a revocable trust (or irrevocable trust
  where transferor is present beneficiary); §61(g): interests vesting in persons other than
  the trustor when a revocable trust becomes irrevocable **are** a change in ownership.
- **Santa Clara County Assessor, Assessment Information 2026 booklet** —
  https://files.santaclaracounty.gov/exjcpb1521/2026-04/assessment-information-2026-booklet-english-3-23-26-web.pdf
  - "The reassessment of property acquired by inheritance from an estate or living trust
    occurs as of the date of the death of the former owner, not on the date of distribution
    to the beneficiary."
  - "State law requires the transferee to file a Preliminary Change in Ownership Report
    (PCOR) with the County Recorder when recording certain documents."
  - "Whenever there is a reassessment … a Supplemental Assessment is issued … prorated for
    the number of months remaining in the fiscal year ending June 30 … separate, and in
    addition to, the regular tax bill"; appeal within 60 days of notice.
  - "Santa Clara County is divided into more than 800 tax rate areas … A typical Tax Rate
    Area … total = $1.1488" per $100 of assessed value (1.1488%). Worked example:
    $1,000,000 assessed → $11,488.00, less $7,000 Homeowners' Exemption ($80.42) →
    $11,407.58.
  - "A tax bill may include other special assessments that are not property taxes …
    Mello-Roos bonds, service fees, liens, and direct assessments."
- **SF Board of Supervisors Resolution File 250876 (passed 09/30/2025)** —
  https://sfbos.archive.sf.gov/sites/default/files/r0447-25.pdf — "the combined property
  tax rate … shall therefore be $1.18268325 on each $100 valuation" (1.18268325%) for FY
