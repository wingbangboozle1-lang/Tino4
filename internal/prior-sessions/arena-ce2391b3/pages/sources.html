Sources that were checked and deliberately rejected

Recorded so it is clear they were considered, not missed:

  • Commercial insurance comparison sites offering "average premium" figures by county. Their numbers for Santa Clara and San Francisco counties differed from each other by roughly a factor of three, none identified a methodology that could be audited, and none is an official publisher. Rejected; the insurance inputs remain labelled as the user's estimates.
  • Blog and law-firm summaries of Prop 19, AB 1482 and the estate-tax exclusion. Useful for orientation only. Every figure that ended up in the model was then read from the BOE, the IRS, the FTB, the State Controller, or the statute itself.
  • A Wikipedia article on Proposition 19 that cited the correct BOE letters. The letters were fetched and read directly instead; the article is not cited.
