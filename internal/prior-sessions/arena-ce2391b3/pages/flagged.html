The honest summary

Everything that matters legally is verified: the Prop 19 rules and their $1,044,586 allowance, the Prop 13 2% cap and the 1.02 inflation factor for the 2026 roll, the homeowners' exemption, the date-of-death basis rule, the §121 exclusion, the $15,000,000 federal exclusion, the absence of any California estate or inheritance tax, the rent caps, the transfer tax rates and the utility tariffs. Each is quoted from the agency that administers it on the Sources page.

Everything that matters financially is an estimate: what the properties are worth, what insurance costs, what Property A would rent for, how fast values and rents grow, and what a reinvested portfolio returns. Those are labelled as assumptions with ranges, and the sensitivity analysis shows what happens at the ends of each range instead of hiding them.

The one supplied figure that looks wrong rather than merely uncertain is Property B's tax (F1). It is carried as supplied rather than corrected, and it is flagged here so it is not mistaken for verified data.
