Source integrity notes

  • The linked BOE Prop 19 dollar adjustment expires for transfers after February 15, 2027; it is not a 2040–2050 forecast.
  • IRS tax amounts are year-specific. The 2026 amounts must not be represented as guaranteed future thresholds.
  • Insurance, utility and maintenance amounts cannot be inferred from official general guidance; blank cells remain blank.
  • Santa Clara and San Francisco assessor data are never combined as if the counties share tax rates, bill formats or parcel facts.
  • California State Controller guidance supports the general no-return rule for deaths after 2004; unusual nonresident, federal-credit, trust, and multi-jurisdiction cases still require individual advice.
  • Local transfer-tax rates are current-law inputs only. San Francisco election results remain labeled preliminary and are not treated as certified; recheck the adopted rate schedule before any future conveyance.
