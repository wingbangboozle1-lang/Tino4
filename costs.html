Property tax——County tax bill and assessor's enrolled value; reassessment and Prop 19 decision in writing. The general 1% rate is not the full bill: voter-approved bonds/direct assessments apply.SF Assessor
Owner-occupied / landlord insurance——Request current, written, coverage-matched quotes by occupancy, number of units, rebuild limit, deductible and rental exposure. Statewide comparison tools are not individualized bindable quotes.CA Department of Insurance guide
Water / sewer——Confirm exact retail water provider, meter/unit configuration, current rate schedule, sewer charges, usage and billing periods. Cupertino residents can be served by different providers; citywide averages would be unsafe.Cupertino water service providers · SFPUC rate information
Electricity / solar export credits——Use utility bills, tariff / net-energy-metering agreement, true-up statement, and inverter production records. A credit is not guaranteed income and solar arrangements/rates can change.California Public Utilities Commission: NEM
Routine repair and maintenance——Obtain property inspection and 5–10 years of invoices; separate repairs from capital improvements. IRS treatment distinguishes repairs/maintenance and capital improvements; it does not publish local cost estimates.IRS Pub. 527
Major capital replacement reserve——Inspect roof, structure, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, drainage and solar equipment; get contractor estimates and lifecycle information. No universal official local annual percentage is assumed.IRS Pub. 527
Rental operating income——Reconcile leases, deposits, receipts, vacancies and unit expenses. Model personal-use and rental portions separately; avoid gross rent as a proxy for return.IRS Pub. 527
