Privacy, Anonymization & $10-Rounding Controls

How personal data is excluded and parcel-identifying numbers are rounded to the nearest $10.

Privacy & verification controls active. Personal identities, street addresses, parcel numbers, and exact tax-roll dollars are never published. All owner-supplied figures and model outputs are rounded to the nearest $10. Official cells lacking a verified government source remain blank and are logged on Flagged for Review.
