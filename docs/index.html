What the analysis concludes

The decisive cost is property tax reassessment

Under California law, trust-held real property is reassessed to market value as of the date of death. Property A's assessed value would move from a Prop 13–capped base of about $802,030 in 2045 to roughly $5,435,870 — an annual tax increase of about $53,230 at the County's typical rate. Even with the Prop 19 parent-child exclusion, the published allowance only shelters about $1,044,590 of market value.

Why selling wins the model

Inherited property receives a basis equal to fair market value at death, so a prompt sale normally produces little or no capital gain. Selling avoids the reassessment and converts the asset into a diversified portfolio. Over a 10-year horizon from 2045, the model puts sell-and-reinvest at $8,875,420 versus $6,936,100 for renting and $5,317,810 for occupying the home.

How to read this site

VERIFIED rows are facts read from official government documents, each with its own source link. MODEL rows are computed projections that cite the official rule used. UNVERIFIED rows have a blank official value and are listed on the Flagged for Review page. Dollar amounts are rounded to the nearest $10.
