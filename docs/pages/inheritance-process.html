13Receive and review the supplemental assessment / reassessment noticeSeparate bill for the difference between old and new value, prorated to June 30; appeal within 60 days.VERIFIEDS30
14Update title, insurance, utilities, and solar/net-billing arrangementsNotify the insurer; a landlord policy is needed if renting; NBT export credits are usually below retail rates.VERIFIEDS30 S46 S41
15Execute the chosen optionSell using stepped-up basis (gain ≈ 0; §121 if 2 of 5 years occupied), or rent (27.5-yr depreciation), or occupy.VERIFIEDS05 S06 S09
16Distribute assets, provide a final accounting, terminate the trustFinal distribution is reported on the fiduciary return; California taxes capital gains as ordinary income.VERIFIEDS19 S16

Each step cites the official document that establishes it. Deadlines are statutory; confirm the exact dates with the successor trustee and county assessor at the time of death.
