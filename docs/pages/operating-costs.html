Insurance research result

The only official, parcel-independent benchmark found is the California Department of Insurance statewide average of $1,570 per year. ZIP-level data exist [S47] but no official ZIP-specific premium for 95014 or 94122, and no landlord-policy quote for the 3-unit property, was confirmed. The user's ~$1,500/yr guess is close to the statewide average but is not treated as verified. Obtain actual quotes before finalizing any decision.
