Which changes matter most here

Any federal move to tax unrealized gains at death or repeal stepped-up basis would directly reduce the sell option's main tax advantage. Any change to Prop 19 (SB 293, or future ballot measures) would change the size of the reassessment. Local rent caps affect Property B's income, not Property A's decision. Re-verify this page at the time of inheritance (2040–2050), since projections 15–25 years out cannot price future legislation.
