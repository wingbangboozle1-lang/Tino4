CALIFORNIA PROPERTY RESEARCH BRIEF · 2026
07 / PROCESS

From trust to title.

A practical sequence to review with a California estate attorney and tax professional. Timing depends on the trust and the facts.

StepActionOfficial source
01 · Establish authorityTrustee reviews trust terms, beneficiaries, ownership, powers and transfer date; secure property, insurance and records.IRS estate overview ↗
02 · Document valuesInventory all estate assets and debts, obtain supported date-of-death appraisals, and determine what is included in the estate and each beneficiary’s basis.IRS basis ↗
03 · Check eligibilityFor a potential family-home exclusion, verify relationship, transferor’s prior principal residence, recipient’s use, trust structure, partial interests and value cap.BOE guidance ↗
04 · Protect deadlinesMove into qualifying home within one year and file the homeowner or disabled-veteran exemption. Generally file BOE-19-P within three years and before third-party transfer for relief from transfer; exceptions and late prospective relief apply.BOE filing instructions ↗
05 · County transferCoordinate deeds, change-of-ownership reporting and exclusion claims with the county assessor and trustee; resolve supplemental and annual assessments.BOE Prop 19 ↗
06 · Tax returnsAssess Form 706, portability election and fiduciary/income returns; filing and payment deadlines vary, so confirm promptly.IRS Form 706 instructions ↗
07 · Choose and maintainReconcile rent law, insurance, utilities, capital costs and taxes; compare after-tax outcomes. Continuing occupancy may be required to retain exclusion.BOE FAQ ↗
