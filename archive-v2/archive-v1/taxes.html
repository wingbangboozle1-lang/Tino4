Verified current-law anchors, not a forecast of what legislation will look like decades from now.

TopicVerified rule / calculationOfficial source
Federal 2026Basic estate exclusion: $15,000,000 per decedent. Filing threshold considers gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts and specific gift tax exemption. It is not a tax-free inheritance allowance per heir.IRS estate tax ↗
PortabilityUnused exclusion may be transferred to a surviving spouse by timely election on Form 706; a $30 million exemption is not automatic.IRS ↗
Federal rateForm 706 instructions give a top marginal estate tax rate of 40%; do not multiply all inherited assets by 40%. Credits, gifts and deductions change the result.IRS Form 706 instructions ↗
Headroom / estate tax— — estate composition, prior taxable gifts, deductions, spousal status and future law are unknown.IRS ↗
2027–2050 exclusions— — future annual official amounts not verified; future statutes and inflation adjustments are uncertain.IRS 2026 adjustment ↗
California inheritance incomeA gift or inheritance is not itself included in California income; subsequent interest, rent or gain may be taxable. This does not by itself decide all estate-tax questions.California FTB ↗
California estate taxCalifornia statute links the tax to the federal state-death-tax credit; applicability and future law require specialist review. Do not assume a permanent zero for future years.California Revenue & Taxation Code §13302 ↗
Property basisFederal inherited basis is generally fair market value on date of death, with exceptions including alternative valuation and special-use property. California treatment and this trust’s assets require confirmation.IRS Publication 551 ↗
Parent–child home exclusionEligible transferor and transferee principal residence required; value cap is old factored base plus a biennially adjusted allowance. Beyond that cap, excess is added to assessed value.BOE publication ↗
Allowance at current periodExact statutory adjustment: $1,044,586 for qualifying transfers February 16, 2025 through February 15, 2027; not a 2040–2050 prediction. Exact amount retained to avoid misstatement.BOE publication ↗
Rental regulationApplicable statewide rent cap generally is the lower of 5% plus CPI or 10%, with exemptions; cited statute’s repeal date is January 1, 2030. Future legislation may change it.California Legislature ↗
SF rent controlFor covered rent-controlled units, the 2026–27 annual allowable increase is 1.6%. Applicability and banking exceptions need review.SF Rent Board ↗
Solar policyCPUC says existing legacy NEM periods can continue for the remainder of 20 years and survive a change of hands; verify actual tariff and future rules.CPUC 2025 report ↗
