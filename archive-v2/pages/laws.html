How much can be inherited before a death tax applies?

Federal: up to $15,000,000 for a 2026 death, per person, indexed afterwards. Above that, 40% of the excess is payable by the estate. California: no limit, because there is no California estate or inheritance tax. A couple with portability starts at $30,000,000. Sources: IRS, Santa Clara Superior Court.

Inheritance thresholds in practice

The federal estate tax is imposed on the estate, not on each beneficiary, so "how much can be inherited tax-free" really means "how large can the estate be". The 2026 figure is $15,000,000 per person and it is indexed after that. On the model's 2.5% indexing assumption:

What would change the decision

  • A cut to the federal exclusion would raise estate tax but not change which house strategy is better — the houses would be a smaller share of a taxable estate, and liquidity would become more valuable. That argues for selling, not holding.
  • Any reinstated California estate tax would do the same, with a larger effect, because California has the largest concentration of high-value homes.
  • Tighter rent control in San Francisco (or repeal of Costa-Hawkins vacancy decontrol) would reduce the Sunset building's value and push toward Scenario D in the Decision Analysis.
  • Any weakening of Prop 19 would make the Cupertino base worthless on transfer and remove the main reason to occupy the house. Nothing verified suggests this is pending.
