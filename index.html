Decision briefing

What the evidence says today

  • There is no universal “inheritance limit” for a beneficiary. U.S. federal estate tax is generally measured at the decedent/estate level. The 2026 basic exclusion is $15 million; it can be affected by lifetime taxable gifts, deductions, and a deceased spouse's unused exclusion (DSUE). A Form 706 may be needed to elect portability even if no estate tax is otherwise due. IRS Form 706 instructions
  • California property tax is the main immediate uncertainty. Under Proposition 19, the parent-child exclusion for a family home is conditional: an eligible transferee must use it as a principal residence and apply for the homeowners' or disabled veterans' exemption within one year. The reassessment cap is based on the transferor's factored base-year value plus an adjusted amount; a high-value inherited home may be partially reassessed. The applicable threshold for a 2040–2050 transfer is not yet knowable. CA BOE, current 2025–27 adjustment
  • “Live, rent, or sell” requires after-tax cash-flow comparison. For renting, count rent less vacancy, operating costs, capital repairs, taxes, and income tax. For sale, count net proceeds after basis, selling costs, federal and California tax, and reinvestment risk. Living in Home A is a housing-cost alternative, not rental yield; compare housing costs avoided and foregone income.
  • Home B matters. Its actual net rental cash flow and the marginal value of occupying its available space should be compared with the cost and tax consequences of taking Home A as a principal residence. Published user-supplied rents and utility estimates are intentionally suppressed and remain unverified.

Recommended sequence

Decide at the decision date

  1. Confirm title, trust instrument, beneficiaries, debts, and who has authority.
  2. Obtain date-of-death appraisal and tax-basis records.
  3. Get county written determinations on Proposition 19 / reassessment and actual tax bill.
  4. Request current landlord, owner-occupied, and multi-unit insurance quotes.
  5. Compare 10-year net cash flows for all options, then confirm tax treatment with qualified advisers.
