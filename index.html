What changes the answer

A source-linked decision workspace for a home expected to transfer through a trust, and a separate three-unit property the decision maker already owns. Working figures are rounded to the nearest ten dollars and no personal identifier appears anywhere on this site.

Read this first: the answer is conditional

No path can be responsibly ranked until the trust terms, the transferor's occupancy at transfer, the estate inventory and the actual insurance and repair quotes exist. The figures below are the owner's own working numbers, rounded to the nearest ten dollars, combined with rules read from official pages. They are a starting model, not a conclusion.

Keep the two assets separate in every calculation

Property A is an anticipated transfer that the decision maker does not own today. Property B is already owned, is the current residence, and carries its own basis, assessment and tenancies. A choice about one must never silently assume the other's basis, assessment, rent or operating cost.
