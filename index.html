Decision workspace

A private planning framework for an anticipated inheritance and a separately owned rental property. No household-specific values, locations, histories, or projections are published here.

Start with the decision gates

Confirm the trust terms, transfer date, beneficiaries, tax basis, occupancy intent, debts, leases, and insurance before comparing live, rent, or sell paths.

Keep the properties separate

Distinguish the anticipated inheritance from the separately owned property. Verify title, tax assessment, rental status, and applicable rules for each property independently.

Test mixed paths

Compare occupancy, partial rental where lawful, sale, and a staged investment plan. Model cash reserves and reinvestment risk alongside returns.

Do not treat a forecast as a fact

A transfer expected decades from now depends on future law, markets, costs, trust language, and the property's condition. Refresh all inputs with qualified advisers near the event.

Planning framework only; not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, or investment advice. No household-specific inputs are published.
