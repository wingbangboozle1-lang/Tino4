Decision overview

Official rules linked by source, household figures published only as rounded scenario inputs, and identifiers withheld.

Figures are published rounded; identifiers are not

The analysis below ranks the modeled paths on the household's own rounded report, because a ranking that shows no numbers cannot be checked. Amounts are rounded to the nearest ten dollars and labelled as assumptions where an official source does not establish them, while locality, parcel, account and party identifiers stay out of this repository entirely. Exact records belong in a private workpaper, and nothing here is legal, tax or investment advice.

Urgent privacy notice: historical exposure is unresolved

The current site is generalized, but prior commits, pull-request views, forks, caches, downloads, or clones may retain older public material. This cleanup does not prove that historical copies are erased. Repository administrators must assess and coordinate any history remediation; do not upload private case records here. Safe decision order

Set the family's written decision rules before seeing a forecast. Confirm trust and title, get independent values, establish tax basis and assessed-value treatment, validate use and leases, price recurring costs, then compare every path at the same dates and horizon. Keep all private inputs in a locally controlled, non-public workpaper.

Conditional fork

If a qualifying heir intends to occupy the transferred home as a principal residence long-term, test the Proposition 19 family-home exclusion and its deadlines. If not, model the reassessed tax branch and compare rental, sale, or mixed paths. No branch can be chosen from public facts alone.

Research framework only; not legal, tax, insurance, appraisal, investment, or property-management advice.

Recheck official authorities, source availability and law at the actual decision date.
