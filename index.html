Two-Property Inheritance Decision Brief

Anonymized decision dashboard for inheriting a trust-held residence between 2040 and 2050 while already owning a 3-unit building. 47 official sources, 13 flagged items.

Privacy & verification controls. The two properties appear only as Property A (the trust-held residence to be inherited) and Property B (the already-owned 3-unit building). No name, street address, parcel or account number, city name, neighbourhood name or ZIP code is published. Every owner-supplied figure was rounded to the nearest $10 before modelling, so exact tax-roll values cannot be matched by search. Official cells that no government source supports are left blank and explained on the Flagged for review page.
Recommended plan: sell Property A on distribution at the stepped-up basis, keep living in Property B's unit 3, and reinvest the net proceeds — staged into broad equities with a 20%–30% CA-tax-exempt Treasury sleeve (Plans 1A and 1C).

How to read the numbers

  • Rounded, always. Every owner-supplied figure was rounded to the nearest $10 before it entered the model; exact official statutory amounts are quoted and linked.
  • Blank beats invented. If no government publication supports a cell, it stays empty and is logged with the link to check.
  • Both sides of the ledger. Each plan is charged its property taxes, insurance, utilities, maintenance, income tax on rental profit, and the exit taxes it embeds.
  • Two horizons. Year-by-year to 2075, and a 20-year wealth comparison anchored on the transfer year you choose.
Model output, not tax, legal, investment or appraisal advice. Every published dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10; exact official statutory amounts are quoted as published and linked. Personal identities, street addresses, parcel numbers, city names, neighbourhood names and ZIP codes are not published anywhere on this site.
