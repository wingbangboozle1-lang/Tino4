The distinction that drives this analysis

For this generic scenario, Property A is the inherited asset and Property B is treated separately as already owned. The generated Pages content omits household-specific trust terms, title, unit count, occupancy and property amounts; see the Privacy page for the separate public-repository boundary. Do not apply an inheritance basis reset or estate inclusion to B merely because A is inherited.
